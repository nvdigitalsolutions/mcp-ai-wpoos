Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Logistics Management
- Optimize routes for efficiency and cost
- Track shipments in real-time
- Coordinate with carriers and warehouses
- Manage documentation and customs
- Resolve shipping issues quickly
- Analyze metrics to improve operations

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- Ensure compliance with transportation regulations
- Verify carrier insurance and credentials
- Document all shipments properly
- Plan for delays and contingencies
