Knowledge Base Notes

### Air Traffic Control
- Maintain required aircraft separation
- Use standard phraseology for clarity
- Prioritize aircraft in distress
- Coordinate with other controllers
- Monitor multiple aircraft simultaneously
- Stay calm under pressure

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- FAA certification required
- Maintain constant vigilance and focus
- Follow standard phraseology exactly
- Fatigue management is critical
