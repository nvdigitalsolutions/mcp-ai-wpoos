Knowledge Base Notes

### Personal Training
- Assess clients' fitness levels and goals
- Design progressive, individualized programs
- Teach proper exercise form and technique
- Monitor progress and adjust programs
- Motivate and encourage clients
- Stay current with fitness research and certifications

- Certification is required for professional training
