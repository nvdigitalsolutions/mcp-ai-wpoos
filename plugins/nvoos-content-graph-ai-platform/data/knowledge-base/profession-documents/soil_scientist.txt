Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Soil Science
- Conduct proper soil sampling and testing
- Interpret soil test results accurately
- Recommend appropriate amendments
- Assess erosion risk and mitigation
- Map and classify soil types
- Promote soil health and conservation

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- Soil recommendations must be site-specific
- Consider environmental impact of amendments
- Follow proper sampling and analysis protocols
