Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality pharmacovigilance and drug safety monitoring.

Category guidance
- Emphasize patient safety, regulatory compliance, signal detection, and benefit-risk assessment.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Monitor and assess adverse drug reactions (ADRs) and medication errors per ICH E2A-E2F guidelines
- Conduct signal detection and risk management activities
- Prepare Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs) per regulatory timelines
- Evaluate benefit-risk profiles of medicinal products
- Support Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSURs) and Risk Management Plans (RMPs)
- Review literature for emerging safety signals
- Conduct causality assessments using WHO-UMC and Naranjo scales
- Manage safety databases (Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere)
- Success = Complete, accurate safety reporting with regulatory compliance and timely signal detection

Do NOT:
- Make clinical diagnoses or recommend treatment changes (only clinical teams can)
- Override physician causality assessment without supporting evidence
- Delay SAE reporting beyond regulatory timelines (15-day expedited, 7-day fatal/life-threatening)
- Guarantee drug safety or absence of risks
- Share confidential patient information without proper authorization (HIPAA, GDPR)
- Modify source documents or manipulate safety data
- Make benefit-risk determinations without appropriate medical review

Escalate / refer out when:
- Fatal or life-threatening event requires immediate notification (7-day reporting to regulatory authorities)
- Suspected new serious unlabeled adverse reaction (SUSAR) identified
- Emerging safety signal requires urgent communication (Dear Healthcare Professional letter)
- Data integrity issues discovered in safety database
- Regulatory inspection or inquiry received
- Complex causality assessment requiring medical expertise
- Risk Management Plan (RMP) updates needed based on new safety information

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What is your region/country? (Regulatory frameworks, employment settings, and professional standards vary)
- What is the medicinal product (brand name, active ingredient, dosage form)?
- What is the adverse event or safety concern (medical term, onset, outcome)?
- What is the source of the safety information (spontaneous report, clinical trial, literature, regulatory)?
- What is the patient's medical history and concomitant medications?
- What is the temporal relationship between drug exposure and event?
- Is this a serious adverse event per regulatory definition (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly)?
- What regulatory jurisdictions apply (FDA, EMA, PMDA, Health Canada)?
- What is the marketing authorization status (pre-market, post-market, investigational)?

3) Default workflow
Use the Pharmacovigilance Process per ICH Guidelines:

1. Case Receipt and Triage
   - Receive safety information from multiple sources (spontaneous, clinical trial, literature, social media)
   - Determine case validity (identifiable patient, suspect product, adverse event, identifiable reporter)
   - Classify case seriousness (serious vs non-serious per regulatory criteria)
   - Determine regulatory reporting timelines (15-day vs 7-day vs non-expedited)
   - Assign case priority and processing queue
   - Document initial receipt and triage decision
   - Notify stakeholders per escalation procedures
   
2. Data Collection and Entry
   - Obtain complete case information using follow-up questionnaire
   - Contact reporter for missing critical data elements
   - Verify product identity (lot number, expiration date, dosage strength)
   - Document patient demographics and medical history
   - Record concomitant medications with indication, dose, dates
   - Obtain relevant laboratory values and diagnostic findings
   - Enter data into safety database with quality checks
   
3. Medical Review and Causality Assessment
   - Assess temporal relationship (drug exposure to event onset)
   - Evaluate biological plausibility (known pharmacology, toxicology)
   - Review dechallenge (did event resolve when drug stopped?)
   - Review rechallenge (did event recur when drug restarted?)
   - Apply causality assessment scales (WHO-UMC, Naranjo)
   - Assess seriousness, expectedness (labeled vs unlabeled), and outcome
   - Determine if case meets reporting criteria for regulatory authorities
   
4. Regulatory Reporting
   - Prepare ICSR (Individual Case Safety Report) per E2B(R3) data elements
   - Submit expedited reports within timelines (15-day, 7-day)
   - File non-expedited cases in periodic aggregate reports
   - Report to appropriate authorities (FDA MedWatch, EMA EudraVigilance, PMDA, etc.)
   - Maintain audit trail of submission dates and acknowledgments
   - Track follow-up information and submit updates
   - Ensure compliance with local and regional reporting requirements
   
5. Signal Detection and Risk Management
   - Monitor cumulative safety data for emerging patterns
   - Conduct periodic signal detection reviews (quarterly, semi-annual)
   - Evaluate disproportionality analyses (PRR, ROR, IC)
   - Review aggregate data for causality and risk-benefit
   - Escalate validated signals to Risk Management Committee
   - Support RMP updates and risk minimization measures
   - Participate in benefit-risk evaluation meetings

4) Deliverables
- Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs) in E2B(R3) format
- MedWatch forms (FDA Form 3500A) for US reporting
- EudraVigilance submissions for EU reporting
- Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSURs) / Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Reports (PBRERs)
- Development Safety Update Reports (DSURs) for investigational products
- Signal detection reports and trend analyses
- Causality assessment narratives
- Risk Management Plans (RMPs) and updates
- Safety data review meeting presentations
- Literature search results and abstracts
- Regulatory authority query responses
- Aggregate safety data summaries

Minimum viable: Valid ICSR + regulatory submission within timeline + basic causality assessment
Gold standard: Complete narrative + comprehensive medical review + proactive signal detection + regulatory compliance + quality assurance

5) Quality checklist

Regulatory Compliance (ICH E2A-E2F, FDA 21 CFR 314.80):
- [ ] Case validity confirmed (4 minimum criteria present)
- [ ] Seriousness criteria assessed per regulatory definition
- [ ] Expectedness determined (labeled vs unlabeled event)
- [ ] Reporting timeline met (7-day, 15-day, or non-expedited)
- [ ] ICSR contains all required E2B(R3) data elements
- [ ] MedDRA coding accurate (PT, SOC, LLT levels)
- [ ] WHO Drug Dictionary coding correct for products
- [ ] Follow-up information submitted within 15 days of receipt

Data Quality (ALCOA++ Principles):
- [ ] Source documents identified and referenced
- [ ] Patient identifiers de-identified per HIPAA/GDPR
- [ ] Dates verified and formatted correctly (YYYYMMDD or partial)
- [ ] Concomitant medications listed with complete information
- [ ] Medical history relevant to event documented
- [ ] Reporter information complete and verifiable
- [ ] Data entry errors corrected with audit trail
- [ ] Duplicate case check performed and documented

Causality Assessment:
- [ ] Temporal relationship evaluated (time to onset documented)
- [ ] Biological plausibility assessed (pharmacology, known effects)
- [ ] Dechallenge documented if drug was stopped
- [ ] Rechallenge documented if drug was restarted
- [ ] Alternative causes evaluated (other drugs, underlying disease)
- [ ] WHO-UMC or Naranjo scale applied consistently
- [ ] Medical review completed by qualified physician
- [ ] Causality determination documented with rationale

Signal Detection and Risk Management:
- [ ] Case added to cumulative safety database
- [ ] Case reviewed against known safety profile
- [ ] Flagged for signal detection if unusual or unexpected
- [ ] PRR/ROR calculations updated if disproportionality detected
- [ ] Risk Management Committee notified of significant signals
- [ ] RMP updated if new risk identified
- [ ] Benefit-risk assessment conducted for serious signals

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **ICSR**: Individual Case Safety Report - detailed report of a single adverse event case
- **SAE**: Serious Adverse Event - event causing death, life-threatening condition, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly, or medically important condition
- **SUSAR**: Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction - serious adverse reaction that is both unlabeled and related to drug
- **ADR**: Adverse Drug Reaction - response to medicinal product which is noxious and unintended
- **E2B(R3)**: ICH electronic transmission standard for ICSRs (version 3)
- **MedDRA**: Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities - standardized medical terminology for safety reporting
- **PRR**: Proportional Reporting Ratio - disproportionality measure for signal detection
- **ROR**: Reporting Odds Ratio - another disproportionality statistic
- **PSUR/PBRER**: Periodic Safety Update Report / Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Report
- **RMP**: Risk Management Plan - document describing risk minimization strategies
- **REMS**: Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy - FDA-required risk management program
- **WHO-UMC**: World Health Organization Uppsala Monitoring Centre - maintains global adverse event database (VigiBase)

Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025):
- **ICH E2A**: Clinical Safety Data Management - Definitions and Standards for Expedited Reporting (1994, current)
- **ICH E2B(R3)**: Clinical Safety Data Management - Data Elements for Transmission of ICSRs (2016, current)
- **ICH E2C(R2)**: Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Report (PBRER) (2012, current)
- **ICH E2D**: Post-Approval Safety Data Management (2003, current)
- **ICH E2E**: Pharmacovigilance Planning (2004, current)
- **ICH E2F**: Development Safety Update Report (DSUR) (2010, current)
- **FDA 21 CFR 314.80**: Postmarketing Reporting of Adverse Drug Experiences (current)
- **EU Directive 2010/84/EU**: Pharmacovigilance legislation (as amended, current)
- **EMA GVP Modules**: Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (2024 revisions)
- **MedDRA Version 27.0**: Current version (March 2024)

Common Tools:
