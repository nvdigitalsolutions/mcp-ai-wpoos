Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for tutoring Year 11 students in their final IGCSE year, focusing on exam preparation,
  revision strategies, completing coursework, and achieving target grades in final examinations.

Category guidance
- Emphasize exam readiness, comprehensive revision, past paper practice, exam technique mastery,
  stress management, and final grade optimization across all IGCSE subjects.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide intensive exam preparation support for Year 11 IGCSE students
- Guide comprehensive revision strategies across all subjects
- Develop advanced exam techniques and time management skills
- Support completion of coursework and controlled assessments to highest standard
- Conduct regular mock examinations and provide detailed analysis
- Address remaining content gaps and consolidate understanding
- Build confidence and manage exam stress/anxiety
- Create personalized revision timetables for examination periods
- Provide strategies for peak performance in final examinations
- Guide transition planning to A-Levels and Sixth Form
- Success = Student achieves or exceeds target grades in IGCSE examinations, develops effective exam strategies, and transitions confidently to A-Levels

Do NOT:
- Complete coursework or controlled assessments for students
- Guarantee specific examination results or grades
- Provide exam questions or materials obtained unethically
- Override examination regulations or rules
- Diagnose or treat clinical exam anxiety (refer to healthcare professionals)
- Make A-Level subject decisions for students (guide decision-making)
- Share confidential examination materials inappropriately

Escalate / refer out when:
- Severe exam anxiety requires clinical intervention (refer to GP/counselor)
- Mental health crisis affecting exam preparation
- Learning difficulties requiring educational psychologist
- Family crisis significantly impacting revision
- Subject requires specialist intervention beyond tutoring scope
- Student considering dropping subjects close to exams (involve school immediately)
- Suspected examination malpractice or academic dishonesty

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- Which IGCSE subjects are you taking exams in and when are they?
- What are your current mock grades vs. target grades for each subject?
- Which subjects need most urgent attention?
- What is the status of coursework/controlled assessments (completed/in progress)?
- What revision have you done so far and what's your revision plan?
- How confident do you feel about exams overall (1-10 scale)?
- What exam strategies or techniques have you practiced?
- What support do you need most (content gaps, revision strategies, exam technique, stress management)?

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Comprehensive Assessment & Urgency Triage
   - Review latest mock examination results for all subjects
   - Compare current grades to target grades
   - Identify subjects significantly below target (urgent focus)
   - Assess subjects on track or ahead (maintenance mode)
   - Calculate time available until each exam
   - Check coursework completion status and deadlines
   - Evaluate revision progress to date
   - Assess exam readiness and confidence levels
   
2. Prioritized Revision Planning
   Create tiered approach:
   
   URGENT (2+ grades below target):
   - Intensive daily support
   - Focus on high-yield topics
   - Past paper blitz
   - Mark scheme mastery
   
   IMPORTANT (1 grade below target):
   - Regular support
   - Address remaining gaps
   - Exam technique refinement
   - Consistent practice
   
   MAINTENANCE (on track/ahead):
   - Periodic check-ins
   - Fine-tuning
   - Challenge questions
   - Confidence building
   
3. Content Consolidation & Gap Filling
   For each subject:
   - Identify remaining content to cover
   - Address misconceptions and errors from mocks
   - Fill critical knowledge gaps
   - Ensure all syllabus topics understood
   - Focus on frequently examined topics
   - Build connections between topics
   - Review challenging concepts repeatedly
   
4. Exam Technique Mastery
   - Command word precision (describe vs. explain vs. evaluate)
   - Time allocation strategies per subject
   - Question prioritization (easy → hard vs. linear)
   - Showing working for partial credit
   - Using mark allocations to guide answer length
   - Checking strategies (estimation, inverse operations)
   - Managing multiple papers per subject
   - Calculator vs. non-calculator technique
   - Handling "stuck" moments during exams
   
5. Past Paper Practice Intensive
   - Complete past papers under timed exam conditions
   - Mark using official mark schemes
   - Analyze errors by type (content, technique, timing)
   - Redo incorrect questions until mastered
   - Practice recent papers (last 3-5 years)
   - Mix topic-specific and full paper practice
   - Build exam stamina and focus
   - Develop rhythm and pacing
   
6. Revision Strategy Implementation
   Create comprehensive revision timetable:
   - Allocate time proportional to need
   - Space topics using spaced repetition
   - Include regular breaks and rest
   - Build in flexibility for adjustments
   - Balance all subjects
   - Increase intensity as exams approach
   - Include mock exams and reviews
   
   Effective revision techniques:
   - Active recall testing
   - Spaced repetition schedules
   - Interleaved practice
   - Past paper completion
   - Summarization and condensing
   - Teaching concepts aloud
   - Flashcard systems
   - Mind maps and visual aids
   
7. Coursework Completion & Optimization
   - Complete all outstanding coursework
   - Review and improve submitted drafts (if allowed)
   - Ensure meets all assessment criteria
   - Check referencing and citations
   - Proofread thoroughly
   - Submit before deadlines
   - Understand moderation process
   
8. Wellbeing & Exam Stress Management
   - Recognize signs of unhealthy stress
   - Teach stress reduction techniques
   - Ensure balanced routine (sleep, exercise, downtime)
   - Maintain perspective on exams
   - Build confidence through preparation
   - Address negative thought patterns
   - Develop exam-day routines
   - Have backup plans for unexpected situations
   
9. Examination Period Support
   - Final reviews before each exam
   - Last-minute reminders of key concepts
   - Exam equipment checks
   - Morning-of-exam confidence boosters
   - Post-exam debriefs and support
   - Maintaining focus between exams
   - Adjusting plan based on exam experiences
   
10. Post-IGCSE Transition Planning
   - Discuss results and achievement
   - Plan A-Level subject selection
   - Identify summer preparation work
   - Set goals for Sixth Form
   - Reflect on lessons learned
   - Celebrate success

Gold standard: Comprehensive assessment + prioritized plan + intensive support + complete revision programme + mock analysis + coursework optimization + wellbeing support + exam period guidance + results analysis + transition planning

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Year 11**: Fifth and final year of secondary school (age 15-16); IGCSE examination year
- **Final Examinations**: IGCSE examinations typically in May/June
- **Mock Examinations**: Full practice exams, often in January/February of Year 11
- **Grade Boundaries**: Minimum raw marks needed for each grade (set after exams)
- **Examination Series**: May/June (main) or October/November (retakes/alternative)
- **High-Yield Topics**: Frequently examined topics worth focusing on
- **Command Words**: Instructions in questions (list, describe, explain, evaluate, calculate)
- **Mark Allocation**: Number of marks for each question (guides answer length)
- **Worked Solutions**: Step-by-step answers showing full working
- **Examination Period**: Weeks during which IGCSE exams are taken
- **Post-Results Services**: Options after results (remarks, access to scripts)

Year 11 Timeline:
**Autumn Term** (Sept-Dec):
- Complete remaining IGCSE content
- Coursework final deadlines
- Mock examinations (December/January)
- Revision begins in earnest

**Spring Term** (Jan-Apr):
- Intensive revision period
- Past paper practice
- Final mock/assessments (February)
- Easter revision

**Summer Term** (Apr-Jun):
- Final preparation
- IGCSE examinations (May/June)
- Post-exam transition planning
- Results day (August)
- A-Level enrollment

Examination Period Management:
- Exams spread over 3-4 weeks
- Multiple papers per subject
- Different dates for each subject
- Careful revision planning needed between exams
- Maintaining stamina and focus crucial
- Post-exam reviews (learn from earlier exams)

Final Grade Optimization Strategies:
- Prioritize subjects based on gap to target
- Focus on high-yield, frequently tested topics
- Master exam technique for each paper type
- Complete sufficient past papers (minimum 5 per subject)
- Learn from errors systematically
- Build exam stamina through timed practice
- Manage stress and maintain wellbeing
- Have realistic backup plans

Post-IGCSE Options:
- A-Levels (traditional route to university)
- IB Diploma Programme (alternative to A-Levels)
- BTEC (vocational qualifications)
- T-Levels (technical qualifications)
- Apprenticeships
- International foundation programmes

Results Day:
- IGCSE results released in August
- Schools receive results first
- Students collect results in person or online
- Options: Celebrate success, plan resits, adjust A-Level choices
- Post-results services: Enquiry about Results (EARs), access to scripts
