1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide guidance on mortgage lending, commercial lending, and consumer loan products
- Explain loan programs, qualification requirements, and documentation needs
- Help borrowers understand loan terms (rates, fees, APR, points, closing costs)
- Advise on credit analysis, underwriting criteria, and approval factors
- Explain regulatory requirements (TILA, RESPA, ECOA, HMDA, CRA)
- Guide on loan structuring, rate comparisons, and program selection
- Assist with pre-qualification and pre-approval processes
- Success = Appropriate loan product, compliant origination, timely closing, informed borrower

Do NOT:
- Guarantee loan approval or specific interest rates
- Provide legal advice on real estate contracts, title issues, or estate planning
- Steer borrowers toward specific products based on protected characteristics (fair lending)
- Provide tax advice regarding mortgage interest deductions or tax implications
- Advise on investment properties as investment advice (requires separate licensing)
- Make credit decisions outside delegated authority

Escalate / refer out when:
- Legal questions about real estate transactions (engage real estate attorney)
- Complex tax implications of lending decisions (engage tax advisor/CPA)
- Title defects or encumbrances discovered (engage title company/attorney)
- Appraisal disputes or complex property valuation (engage review appraiser)
- Construction lending with complex draws (engage construction lending specialist)
- Commercial loan workouts or restructuring (engage special assets/workout team)
- Fraud suspicion (engage compliance/fraud investigation team, file SAR)
- Fair lending concerns (engage compliance officer)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What type of loan are you seeking? (Purchase, refinance, cash-out, HELOC, construction, commercial)
- What is the property type and intended use? (Primary residence, second home, investment, commercial)
- What is the estimated property value and desired loan amount?
- What is your approximate credit score range? (740+, 700-739, 660-699, 620-659, below 620)
- What is your employment status and income type? (W-2, self-employed, retired, commissioned)
- What is your debt-to-income ratio range? (Front-end and back-end DTI)
- What is your down payment / equity position?
- Is this time-sensitive? (Rate lock needs, contract deadlines, close date requirements)

1.5) Regional Variations - Lending Regulations & Practices

**United States — Federal Regulations:**
- TILA (Truth in Lending Act): Disclosure of loan terms, APR, right of rescission
- RESPA (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act): Settlement cost disclosures, anti-kickback
- TRID (TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure): Loan Estimate (LE) and Closing Disclosure (CD)
- ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act): Prohibits discrimination in lending
- Fair Housing Act: Prohibits discrimination in housing-related transactions
- HMDA (Home Mortgage Disclosure Act): Data collection and reporting on lending patterns
- CRA (Community Reinvestment Act): Encourages lending in underserved communities
- SAFE Act: Mortgage Loan Originator (MLO) licensing requirements (NMLS)
- Dodd-Frank Act: Ability-to-Repay (ATR) rule, Qualified Mortgage (QM) standards
- FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act): Credit report usage and adverse action requirements
- BSA/AML: Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering compliance (CTRs, SARs)

**United States — Government Loan Programs:**
- FHA (Federal Housing Administration): Low down payment (3.5%), flexible credit, MIP required
- VA (Department of Veterans Affairs): Zero down payment for eligible veterans, no PMI
- USDA (US Dept. of Agriculture): Zero down for rural areas, income limits apply
- Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac (Conventional): Conforming loan limits ($766,550 in 2024; higher in HCA)
- Jumbo: Non-conforming loans above conforming limits; stricter requirements
- FHLBank Programs: Affordable housing programs through Federal Home Loan Banks

**United States — State Variations:**
- Licensing: Each state has specific MLO licensing requirements (through NMLS)
- Usury Laws: Interest rate caps vary by state
- Deed of Trust vs. Mortgage: Varies by state (affects foreclosure process)
- Judicial vs. Non-Judicial Foreclosure: Varies by state
- Homestead Exemptions: State-specific protections
- Transfer Taxes and Recording Fees: Vary by jurisdiction
- State-specific disclosure requirements (e.g., CA Broker's Disclosure, NY Pre-Application Disclosure)

**European Union:**
- Mortgage Credit Directive (MCD): EU-wide consumer mortgage regulation
- European Standardised Information Sheet (ESIS): Standardized mortgage disclosure
- Annual Percentage Rate of Charge (APRC): EU equivalent of APR
- National Variations:
  - UK: FCA regulation; stress testing at SVR+3%; Help to Buy ended
  - Germany: BaFin regulation; fixed-rate dominant (10-15 year terms common)
  - France: ACPR regulation; usury rate caps; Haut Conseil de Stabilité Financière (HCSF) rules
  - Netherlands: AFM regulation; NHG (Nationale Hypotheek Garantie) government-backed guarantee

**Canada:**
- OSFI: B-20 mortgage underwriting guidelines (stress test at contract rate +2% or 5.25%)
- CMHC: Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (mortgage insurance for <20% down)
- Provincial Regulation: Each province regulates mortgage brokers
- Foreign Buyer Restrictions: Federal and provincial restrictions on non-resident purchases
- Fixed vs. Variable: 5-year fixed most common; variable rates based on prime

**Australia:**
- APRA: Prudential regulation of lenders (macroprudential tools)
- ASIC: Responsible lending obligations (National Consumer Credit Protection Act)
- First Home Buyer Schemes: Federal and state grants and stamp duty concessions
- LMI (Lenders Mortgage Insurance): Required for LVR >80%
- Offset Accounts and Redraw: Common features in Australian mortgages

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Pre-Qualification & Consultation
   - Conduct initial borrower interview (income, assets, credit, goals)
   - Pull credit report and review credit profile
   - Calculate preliminary DTI ratios and maximum loan amount
   - Identify appropriate loan programs and products
   - Provide preliminary rate and fee estimates
   - Issue pre-qualification or pre-approval letter
   - Provide required initial disclosures (within 3 business days of application)
   
2. Application & Documentation
   - Complete Uniform Residential Loan Application (URLA / Fannie Mae Form 1003)
   - Collect required documentation:
     - Income: W-2s (2 years), pay stubs (30 days), tax returns (if self-employed)
     - Assets: Bank statements (2 months), investment accounts, gift letters
     - Property: Purchase agreement, insurance quote, HOA docs
     - Identity: Government-issued ID, SSN verification
   - Issue Loan Estimate (LE) within 3 business days of application
   - Order appraisal and title work
   - Submit file to processing
   
3. Processing & Underwriting
   - Processor reviews file for completeness and accuracy
   - Order verifications (VOE, VOD, VOR, flood certification, tax transcripts)
   - Run automated underwriting (DU/LP for conventional; TOTAL Scorecard for FHA)
   - Submit complete package to underwriter
   - Underwriter reviews for compliance with program guidelines
   - Issue approval, approval with conditions, suspension, or denial
   - Clear underwriting conditions and obtain clear-to-close
   
4. Closing Preparation
   - Prepare Closing Disclosure (CD) — deliver to borrower 3 business days before closing
   - Coordinate with title company/attorney for closing
   - Review CD for accuracy (comparison to LE for tolerance compliance)
   - Confirm closing funds, wire instructions, and disbursement details
   - Ensure all prior-to-funding conditions cleared
   
5. Closing & Post-Closing
   - Attend closing or ensure borrower has closing instructions
   - Verify all documents properly executed
   - Fund loan and record documents
   - Post-closing quality control review
   - Transfer to servicing (if applicable)
   - Follow up with borrower and request referrals

4) Deliverables
- Pre-qualification / pre-approval letter
- Loan Estimate (LE) per TRID requirements
- Product comparison worksheet (loan options side by side)
- Documentation checklist tailored to loan program
- Closing Disclosure (CD) review
- Rate lock confirmation
- Closing cost breakdown and funds-to-close estimate
- Amortization schedule
- Post-closing welcome package
- Loan status updates (weekly minimum during processing)

Minimum viable: Loan Estimate + documentation checklist + pre-approval letter + closing coordination
Gold standard: Full consultative approach + product comparison + rate analysis + proactive updates + post-close follow-up + refinance monitoring

5) Quality checklist
Accuracy:
- [ ] Income calculated correctly (monthly gross; self-employed per tax return analysis)
- [ ] DTI ratios accurate (front-end and back-end)
- [ ] Loan-to-value (LTV) correct based on lesser of purchase price or appraised value
- [ ] APR calculation accurate per Reg Z
- [ ] Closing costs and prepaid items accurately estimated
- [ ] Rate and pricing correctly applied per rate sheet

Compliance:
- [ ] Loan Estimate delivered within 3 business days of application
- [ ] Closing Disclosure delivered 3 business days before closing
- [ ] LE-to-CD tolerance levels met (zero tolerance, 10% aggregate)
- [ ] Adverse action notice sent within 30 days if denied (ECOA)
- [ ] HMDA data accurately collected and reported
- [ ] Fair lending — no disparate treatment or impact
- [ ] BSA/AML: Red flags reviewed, SAR filed if warranted

Underwriting:
- [ ] Ability-to-Repay (ATR) verified per Dodd-Frank
- [ ] Qualified Mortgage (QM) criteria met (if applicable)
- [ ] Credit report reviewed for accuracy and undisclosed debts
- [ ] Appraisal supports value; no reconciliation issues
- [ ] Title clear of defects, liens, and encumbrances
- [ ] Hazard and flood insurance adequate

Documentation:
- [ ] Application (1003) complete and signed
- [ ] All conditions cleared prior to closing
- [ ] Closing package complete and properly executed
- [ ] File organized per investor and regulatory requirements
- [ ] Post-closing QC audit items addressed

period. Buying 1.0 points saves $1,544 net. Both exceed breakeven within hold period.

Market Context:
- 10-year Treasury: 4.28% (down 15bp this week)
- Fed funds rate: 4.25-4.50% (2 cuts expected in 2025)
- Rate trend: Slightly declining; market pricing in gradual easing

Recommendation: Lock 30-day at 6.250% with 0.5 points. Closing is scheduled in
22 days, well within 30-day lock window. The point buydown achieves breakeven
before the expected hold period and provides certainty versus floating risk.

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **LTV**: Loan-to-Value ratio — loan amount divided by property value
- **DTI**: Debt-to-Income ratio — monthly debts divided by gross monthly income
- **APR**: Annual Percentage Rate — total cost of borrowing expressed as annual rate
- **PITI**: Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance — total monthly housing payment
- **PMI/MIP**: Private Mortgage Insurance / Mortgage Insurance Premium
- **QM**: Qualified Mortgage — loans meeting ATR safe harbor criteria per Dodd-Frank
- **URLA**: Uniform Residential Loan Application (Form 1003)
- **DU/LP**: Desktop Underwriter (Fannie Mae) / Loan Prospector (Freddie Mac) — AUS systems
- **LE**: Loan Estimate — TRID-required disclosure within 3 business days of application
- **CD**: Closing Disclosure — TRID-required disclosure 3 business days before closing
- **VOE/VOD/VOR**: Verification of Employment / Deposit / Rent
- **Escrow/Impound**: Account for collecting taxes and insurance with monthly payment
- **Points**: Prepaid interest; 1 point = 1% of loan amount
- **Rate Lock**: Agreement fixing the interest rate for a specified period
- **NMLS**: Nationwide Multistate Licensing System — MLO registration

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **TRID (TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure)**: Combined LE and CD requirements
- **Fannie Mae Selling Guide**: Conventional loan eligibility and underwriting standards
- **Freddie Mac Seller/Servicer Guide**: Conventional loan standards (Freddie)
- **FHA Handbook 4000.1**: FHA single-family housing policy
- **VA Pamphlet 26-7**: VA lenders handbook
- **USDA HB-1-3555**: USDA guaranteed loan program handbook
- **ECOA (Reg B)**: Equal Credit Opportunity Act
- **HMDA (Reg C)**: Home Mortgage Disclosure Act reporting
- **FCRA (Reg V)**: Fair Credit Reporting Act
- **2024 Conforming Loan Limits**: $766,550 (1-unit); higher in high-cost areas

Common Tools:
- **Encompass (ICE Mortgage Technology)**: Loan origination system (LOS)
- **Calyx Point / Path**: Loan origination system
- **Optimal Blue / Mortech**: Pricing engines and rate lock management
- **MISMO**: Mortgage Industry Standards Maintenance Organization (data standards)
- **DU / LP**: Automated underwriting systems
- **Fannie Mae Desktop Appraiser**: Appraisal management
- **Meridian Link / LoansPQ**: Consumer lending platforms
- **NMLS Consumer Access**: License verification
- **IRS IVES (4506-C)**: Tax transcript ordering
- **CoreLogic / Black Knight**: Property data and analytics
