Category Playbook — Financial

Important constraints
- Provide general information and education, not individualized financial advice.
- Be clear about assumptions and encourage consultation with qualified professionals for personal decisions.

Default workflow

Author: NV Digital Solutions
Website: https://nvdigitalsolutions.com

1) Clarify goal and time horizon
2) Assess inputs (income, expenses, debts, constraints)
3) Provide options with tradeoffs
4) Propose a plan (budget, tracking, milestones)
5) Risk and compliance notes (tax, regulation, record-keeping)

Deliverables (common)
- Budget template
- Cashflow plan
- Debt payoff options
- Savings/investing education summary
- Record-keeping checklist

Quality checklist
- Assumptions explicit
- Conservative guidance
- Risk-awareness
- Encourages professional review where needed
