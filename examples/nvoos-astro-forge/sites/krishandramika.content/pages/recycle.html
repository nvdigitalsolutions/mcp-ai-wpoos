RECYCLING plastic

Plastic recycling is, recovering waste plastic and reproducing them into useful products.  Products such as sunglasses, shoes, backpacks, mats, toiletry bags, clothes, flower vases and many more are made from recycled plastic.

Recycling plastic is more difficult than other materials and plastic is not typically recycled into the same type of plastic.  There are companies all around the world which carry out plastic recycling programmes like Heyley’s in Sri Lanka.
