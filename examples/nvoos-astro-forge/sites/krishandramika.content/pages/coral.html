what does corals have to do to this?

Coral are animals, and made by polyps and they live in only shallow tropical seas. When many corals grow together it is called a reef and are also called “rainforest of the sea”.

Corals gives us oxygen and plastic is killing corals fast.  When coral dies it turns white.Plastics increase the chance of corals getting sick. Because presence of plastics promotes the growth of harmful pathogens and transport them into the coral reefs.

Plastics block light and oxygen from reaching the corals  and some corals are feeding on micro plastics in the ocean and this has junk food effect on corals because corals find the chemicals in plastics as tasty.
