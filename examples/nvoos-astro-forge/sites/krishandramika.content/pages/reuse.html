Ways we can reuse and why?

One of the main things we should try to do is replace single use items with reusable items like metal/bamboo instead of plastic straws.

Plastic can kill coral and coral gives us O2 (oxygen).   In order to help stop this we should reuse things that we don’t have to throw away.  We can reuse plastic bags, glass or plastic bottles, tooth brushes, cups, boxes and many more.
