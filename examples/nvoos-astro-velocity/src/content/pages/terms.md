These demo terms describe the NV Store — the NV-authored headless storefront.

## Scope

The storefront binds only to the NV commerce contract and renders the
catalog, CMS content and checkout handoff surfaces. Orders and payment are
handled by the store backend.

This copy lives in `src/content/pages/` — the `/pages/<id>/terms/` route
falls back to it whenever the envelope has no matching page doc (Proposal 106
hybrid local, O-4).
