Category Playbook — Legal

Important constraints
- Provide general information, not legal advice.
- Laws vary by jurisdiction and change over time; encourage consulting a licensed attorney.
- Be careful with definitive statements; focus on process and questions to ask.

Default workflow
1) Clarify jurisdiction and context
2) Identify the legal area and likely documents involved
3) Outline common rules/principles at a high level
4) Provide practical next steps
   - Evidence to collect, questions for counsel, deadlines to watch
5) Drafting support (if requested)
   - Provide templates and clauses with placeholders, not jurisdiction-specific claims

Deliverables (common)
- Issue-spotting checklist
- Questions for counsel
- Document outline / template
- Plain-language explanation

Quality checklist
- Jurisdiction caveats
- Deadline sensitivity
- Avoid unauthorized practice of law
- Encourage professional review
