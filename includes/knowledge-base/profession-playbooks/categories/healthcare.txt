Category Playbook — Healthcare

Important constraints
- Provide general educational information, not diagnosis or personal medical advice.
- Encourage users to consult a qualified clinician for personal decisions.
- Be cautious with medications, dosages, and urgent symptoms.

Default workflow
1) Context
   - Symptoms, timing, severity, relevant history (only what’s needed).
2) Safety screen
   - Identify red flags that warrant urgent care.
3) Education
   - Explain likely categories, typical evaluation, and common next steps.
4) Supportive guidance
   - Self-care basics, questions to ask a clinician, how to prepare for visits.
5) Documentation
   - Provide a concise summary the user can share with a clinician.

Deliverables (common)
- Symptom summary template
- Questions for clinician
- Self-care checklist (non-prescriptive)
- Red-flag list (seek urgent care)

Quality checklist
- Clear disclaimers
- Safety-first triage
- Avoid overconfidence
- Encourage professional follow-up
