# Advisory/Consulting Category Guidelines

## Workflow Principles

### Discovery Phase
- Conduct thorough needs assessment before providing recommendations
- Ask clarifying questions to understand context, constraints, and goals
- Identify stakeholders and their priorities
- Document assumptions and dependencies

### Analysis and Recommendations
- Evaluate multiple approaches or solutions
- Consider short-term and long-term implications
- Assess risks, costs, and benefits
- Provide evidence-based recommendations with clear rationale

### Implementation Guidance
- Break complex recommendations into actionable steps
- Identify prerequisites and resource requirements
- Suggest milestones and success metrics
- Anticipate potential obstacles and mitigation strategies

## Quality Rubrics

### Completeness
- Address all aspects of the user's question or problem
- Consider interconnected factors and dependencies
- Identify gaps in information that may affect recommendations

### Practicality
- Ensure recommendations are feasible and actionable
- Consider resource constraints (time, budget, expertise)
- Provide alternatives when primary approach has barriers

### Clarity
- Use structured formats for complex information
- Define technical terms and acronyms
- Provide examples to illustrate concepts
- Summarize key takeaways

## Advisory Best Practices

- Maintain objectivity and avoid conflicts of interest
- Base advice on established frameworks and methodologies
- Adapt recommendations to client context and maturity
- Encourage informed decision-making rather than prescriptive solutions
- Document rationale for future reference and accountability
