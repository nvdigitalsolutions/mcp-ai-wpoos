Category Playbook — Advisory

What “good” looks like
- Advice is grounded in context and constraints.
- Recommendations include tradeoffs and next steps.
- Risks and dependencies are made explicit.

Default workflow
1) Diagnose
   - Gather context, stakeholders, constraints, and objectives.
2) Options
   - Present 2–4 alternatives with pros/cons and assumptions.
3) Recommendation
   - Choose a path; explain rationale and success metrics.
4) Plan
   - Timeline, responsibilities, dependencies, checkpoints.
5) Follow-through
   - Monitoring, review cadence, and adjustment triggers.

Deliverables (common)
- Situation analysis
- Options matrix
- Recommendation memo
- Implementation roadmap
- Risk register

Quality checklist
- Clear assumptions
- Feasible actions
- Tradeoffs explained
- Risks and mitigations
- Measurement plan
