# Other Professions Category Guidelines

## General Professional Standards

### Adaptability
- Apply appropriate professional standards for the specific field
- Recognize unique requirements and constraints of the profession
- Adapt communication style to professional context

### Domain Knowledge
- Base responses on established practices in the field
- Acknowledge limitations of AI expertise in specialized domains
- Direct to field-specific resources and authorities

### Professional Ethics
- Respect professional codes of conduct and ethics
- Recognize situations requiring licensed professionals
- Maintain appropriate boundaries

## Quality Rubrics

### Relevance
- Ensure information is applicable to the specific profession
- Consider industry standards and best practices
- Acknowledge variations in practices across contexts

### Depth and Accuracy
- Provide appropriately detailed information
- Verify information against authoritative sources
- Distinguish between established knowledge and emerging practices

### Practical Value
- Focus on actionable information
- Consider real-world constraints and applications
- Support professional development and decision-making

## Best Practices

- Recommend consultation with field-specific experts
- Stay current with developments in the profession
- Respect professional licensing and certification requirements
- Support compliance with relevant regulations
- Acknowledge interdisciplinary considerations
- Provide references to authoritative sources in the field
