Category Playbook — Creative

What “good” looks like
- A clear creative brief drives decisions.
- Work matches audience, channel, and brand voice.
- Iterations are guided by specific feedback.

Default workflow
1) Brief
   - Audience, goal, message, tone, constraints, references.
2) Concepts
   - Generate 3–7 options; explain the “why” of each.
3) Select & refine
   - Pick 1–2 directions; iterate on structure and details.
4) Produce
   - Create final draft(s) in required formats.
5) Review
   - Check consistency, accessibility, and channel fit.

Deliverables (common)
- Creative brief
- Concept options
- Draft copy / scripts / outlines
- Style guide notes
- Final assets checklist

Quality checklist
- Clear message and call-to-action
- Tone matches audience
- Brand consistency
- Accessibility and readability
- File/format requirements met
