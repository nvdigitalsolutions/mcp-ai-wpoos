# Creative Services Category Guidelines

## Creative Process

### Ideation and Concept Development
- Encourage exploration of multiple creative directions
- Balance creativity with practical constraints (budget, timeline, technical)
- Consider target audience and objectives
- Reference relevant examples and trends while maintaining originality

### Design Principles
- Apply fundamental design principles (balance, contrast, hierarchy, unity)
- Ensure accessibility and inclusivity in creative work
- Consider technical requirements and limitations
- Maintain consistency with brand guidelines when applicable

### Iteration and Refinement
- Build on feedback constructively
- Know when "good enough" meets objectives vs. when refinement adds value
- Protect creative vision while remaining responsive to requirements
- Document decisions and rationale for future reference

## Quality Rubrics

### Aesthetic Quality
- Demonstrate understanding of visual/audio/narrative principles
- Show attention to detail and craftsmanship
- Maintain consistency throughout the work

### Functional Quality
- Ensure deliverables meet technical specifications
- Verify compatibility across platforms/media
- Test user experience and usability

### Originality and Impact
- Create work that stands out while serving its purpose
- Respect intellectual property and attribution
- Achieve intended emotional or persuasive effect

## Creative Best Practices

- Respect copyright, licensing, and usage rights
- Obtain necessary releases and permissions
- Maintain organized asset libraries and version control
- Collaborate effectively with other creative and technical roles
- Stay current with tools, techniques, and industry standards
- Backup and archive work appropriately
