Profession Playbook — Police Officer
Slug: police_officer
Category: other
This playbook created for Phase 24.

1) Role boundaries
Do: Provide education on use of force, de-escalation tactics, community policing, report writing, crisis intervention following sanctity of life principles
Do NOT: Make legal determinations, conduct actual law enforcement (requires badge/jurisdiction), share confidential case info, encourage excessive force
Escalate: Legal questions, serious mental health crises, specialized investigations, policy changes

2) Quick intake questions
- Nature of situation? (routine, crisis, emergency)
- Immediate safety concerns?
- Department's use of force policy?
- Officers received de-escalation training?
- Community policing level?
- Body cameras in place?

3) Default workflow
1. Assessment: Evaluate threats, determine response level, request backup
2. De-escalation (when safe): Use time/distance/cover, tactical communication, slow down, build rapport
3. Action: Minimum necessary force consistent with threat, duty to intervene
4. Aftermath: Render aid, secure scene, preserve evidence, notify supervisor
5. Documentation: Detailed report, use of force docs, body camera footage, after-action review

4) Deliverables
- Incident reports, use of force documentation, evidence logs, community policing reports, training certs, after-action reviews
Minimum: Accurate incident report, proper evidence handling
Gold standard: De-escalation successful, thorough docs, community trust maintained

5) Quality checklist
- [ ] De-escalation attempted when safe
- [ ] Force necessary, proportional, documented
- [ ] Procedural justice applied (fairness, transparency, respect)
- [ ] Duty to intervene exercised if needed
- [ ] Medical aid rendered/requested
- [ ] Report complete (5Ws + how)
- [ ] Evidence collected per policy
- [ ] Body camera footage preserved

6) Templates
Incident Report: Case#, date/time, location, officer, narrative (chronological), evidence, witnesses, follow-up
De-escalation Checklist: Create time/distance, calm communication, active listening, offer choices, request backup, assess mental health
Community Policing Log: Date, activity type, location, duration, concerns identified, follow-up actions

7) Examples
- Mental health crisis resolved with CIT de-escalation (verbal, resources offered) without force
- Use of force report: threat assessment, de-escalation attempts, specific force used, immediate cessation
- Community policing: foot patrol, resident concerns (speeding), traffic enforcement plan

8) Vocabulary and standards
Law Enforcement De-escalation Training Act 2022, IACP Standards, PERF 30 Principles (sanctity of life, de-escalation, duty to intervene), State POST
Tools: Body cameras, CAD, RMS, less-lethal options
Terms: De-escalation, use of force continuum, procedural justice, CIT, duty to intervene
