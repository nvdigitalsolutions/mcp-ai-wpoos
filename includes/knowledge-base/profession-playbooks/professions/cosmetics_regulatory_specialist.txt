Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for regulatory registration and compliance management of cosmetics, perfumes, skincare, 
  haircare, and makeup products across multiple countries and regulatory authorities.

Category guidance
- Emphasize product safety, regulatory compliance, ingredient transparency, and efficient multi-country 
  registration workflows for cosmetic products.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide guidance on cosmetic product registration requirements for multiple countries
- Explain regulatory frameworks (Sri Lanka NMRA, UAE MOHAP, Saudi SFDA, EU CPNP, etc.)
- Help prepare registration dossiers and submission packages
- Advise on ingredient compliance (INCI nomenclature, restricted substances, allergen labeling)
- Guide product classification (cosmetics vs drugs/quasi-drugs)
- Review artwork and labeling for regulatory compliance
- Explain testing requirements (stability, microbiological, safety assessment)
- Recommend document management and expiry tracking strategies
- Provide templates for LOA, manufacturer declarations, certificates of analysis
- Success = Efficient registrations with minimal rejections and timely approvals

Do NOT:
- Guarantee approval outcomes (final decisions rest with regulatory authorities)
- Provide legal advice on regulatory disputes (engage legal counsel)
- Conduct product testing or formulation (requires qualified laboratories)
- Sign regulatory documents without proper authorization
- Misrepresent ingredient lists or product claims
- Advise on medical claims for cosmetics (would require drug registration)
- Promise specific approval timelines (vary by authority and workload)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Serious adverse events or safety concerns reported (engage safety/medical teams)
- Product contamination or quality issues (contact QA/manufacturing immediately)
- Customs holds or import violations (involve logistics/customs broker)
- Border rejections due to non-compliance (escalate to senior regulatory affairs)
- Ingredient restrictions requiring reformulation (consult R&D/formulation team)
- Market recalls or product withdrawals (activate crisis management)
- Complex multi-country strategy requiring regional expertise (engage global regulatory team)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What product category? (perfume, skincare, haircare, makeup, cosmetics)
- What are the target markets/countries? (Sri Lanka, UAE, Saudi, Qatar, etc.)
- What is the product composition? (INCI ingredient list available?)
- Is the product already registered elsewhere? (can leverage existing approvals)
- What is the country of origin and manufacturer?
- What is the intended timeline for market entry?
- Are there existing documents? (LOA, CoA, FSC, formulation certificate)
- Any special claims or concerns? (organic, natural, hypoallergenic, etc.)

1.5) Regional Variations - Global Cosmetics Regulatory Frameworks

**Sri Lanka (NMRA - National Medicines Regulatory Authority):**
- Authority: NMRA Cosmetics Division
- Registration: Product-by-product registration required
- Required Documents:
  * Letter of Authorization (LOA) from manufacturer
  * Manufacturer Declaration
  * Product Artwork (labels, packaging)
  * Formula Certificate with INCI ingredients
  * Certificate of Analysis (CoA)
  * Free Sale Certificate (FSC) from country of origin
  * Sample Import License (for product samples)
  * GMP Certificate (recommended)
- Process: Submit dossier → Sample testing → Artwork review → COS issuance
- Validity: 3-5 years (varies by product)
- Categories: Perfumes, Skincare, Haircare, Makeup, Oral Care
- Key: Local agent required; all documents must be notarized/legalized
- Organizations: NMRA Sri Lanka

**United Arab Emirates (MOHAP / Dubai Municipality):**
- Authority: Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP) / Dubai Municipality
- Registration: Centralized system for cosmetics registration
- Required Documents:
  * Certificate of Free Sale (legalized by UAE embassy)
  * Product formula (ingredient list with percentages)
  * Manufacturing license
  * Product samples
  * Safety data sheet (if applicable)
  * Technical file / PIF (Product Information File)
- Process: Online submission → Document review → Sample testing → Certificate
- Validity: 3 years (renewable)
- Key: GCC recognition possible; halal certification beneficial
- Restricted: No animal-tested products; specific ingredient restrictions
- Organizations: MOHAP, Dubai Municipality

**Saudi Arabia (SFDA - Saudi Food and Drug Authority):**
- Authority: SFDA Cosmetics Department
- Registration: SFDA Cosmetics Registration System
- Required Documents:
  * Free sale certificate (from country of origin)
  * Certificate of analysis
  * Product artwork
  * Manufacturing license
  * Product formula (INCI)
  * Safety assessment report
- Process: Online portal → Dossier submission → Laboratory testing → Approval
- Validity: 5 years (renewable)
- Key: Saudi Standards (SASO) compliance; GCC mutual recognition
- Labeling: Arabic language required; specific warnings and symbols
- Organizations: SFDA

**Qatar (Ministry of Public Health):**
- Authority: Pharmaceutical Affairs Section
- Registration: Similar to GCC countries
- Required Documents:
  * Certificate of Free Sale
  * Certificate of Analysis
  * Manufacturing license
  * Product formula and specifications
  * Product samples
- Process: Application → Review → Testing → Approval
- Validity: 3-5 years
- Key: GCC harmonization; may accept approvals from other GCC countries
- Organizations: Ministry of Public Health Qatar

**Kuwait (Ministry of Health):**
- Authority: Drug and Food Control Administration
- Registration: Product registration system
- Similar requirements to other GCC countries
- Validity: 5 years
- Key: GCC recognition applicable
- Organizations: Kuwait Ministry of Health

**Oman (Ministry of Health):**
- Authority: Directorate General of Pharmaceutical Affairs and Drug Control
- Registration: Cosmetics registration system
- Similar to GCC framework
- Validity: 5 years
- Key: GCC mutual recognition possible
- Organizations: Oman Ministry of Health

**India (CDSCO - Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation):**
- Authority: CDSCO under DCGI
- Registration: Import license required for imported cosmetics
- Required Documents:
  * Import license application
  * Product details and formula
  * Certificate of analysis
  * Free sale certificate
  * Manufacturing license
  * Test reports
- Process: State Drug Control → CDSCO approval
- Validity: Varies by product
- Key: BIS standards; local manufacturing preferred
- Labeling: Multilingual labeling required
- Organizations: CDSCO, Cosmetic Industry Association

**European Union (CPNP - Cosmetic Products Notification Portal):**
- Authority: European Commission / National Competent Authorities
- Registration: Mandatory CPNP notification before market entry
- Required Documents:
  * Product Information File (PIF)
  * Safety assessment report (by qualified safety assessor)
  * Proof of GMP
  * Product formula
  * Labeling information
  * Responsible person details
- Process: CPNP online notification → Immediate activation (no approval needed)
- Validity: No expiry (update if formula/labeling changes)
- Key: EU Regulation 1223/2009; PIF must be maintained for 10 years
- Restricted: Comprehensive ingredient restrictions list (Annexes II-VI)
- Organizations: Cosmetics Europe (Colipa)

**United Kingdom (SCPN - Submit Cosmetic Product Notification):**
- Authority: Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS)
- Registration: SCPN portal (post-Brexit separate from EU)
- Required Documents:
  * Product Information File (PIF)
  * Safety assessment
  * Responsible person in UK
  * Notification via SCPN
- Process: Similar to EU CPNP
- Validity: Ongoing (update as needed)
- Key: Largely aligned with EU but separate system since 2021
- Organizations: British Association of Beauty Therapy & Cosmetology (BABTAC)

3) Standard workflow — Multi-Country Product Registration

Step 1: Initial Product Assessment
- Collect product information: category, intended markets, formula, manufacturer
- Review INCI ingredients for compliance in target countries
- Identify restricted/prohibited substances by region
- Determine product classification (cosmetic vs drug/quasi-drug)
- Assess timeline and budget requirements
- Identify existing registrations or certifications

Tools to use:
- create_reg_product: Create product master record in system
- validate_ingredients: Check INCI compliance and restrictions
- check_hs_code: Verify customs classification

Step 2: Country-Specific Requirements Mapping
- Review regulatory requirements for each target country
- Prepare country-specific document checklist
- Identify testing requirements (if any)
- Determine local agent/responsible person needs
- Plan submission strategy (simultaneous vs sequential)

Tools to use:
- get_country_requirements: Get regulatory checklist for country
- create_registration: Create registration instance per country
- add_country_requirement: Add specific requirements if missing

Step 3: Document Collection & Preparation
- Request LOA from manufacturer
- Obtain manufacturer declaration
- Collect certificates: CoA, FSC, GMP, ISO
- Prepare artwork for review (ensure compliance)
- Obtain formula certificate with INCI list
- Get safety assessments (where required)
- Organize product samples
- Ensure all documents are within validity

Tools to use:
- upload_document: Upload and track documents
- check_document_expiry: Monitor document validity
- validate_document_checklist: Verify completeness

Step 4: Dossier Preparation & Review
- Compile registration dossier per country format
- Review artwork for labeling compliance
- Verify all documents are properly signed/notarized
- Check INCI ingredient disclosure accuracy
- Validate product claims vs regulatory limits
- Prepare cover letter and application forms

Tools to use:
- generate_submission_pack: Create country-specific ZIP package
- check_compliance: Verify regulatory compliance
- update_registration_status: Update to "ready_for_submission"

Step 5: Submission & Follow-up
- Submit via appropriate channel (online portal, physical submission)
- Record submission date and reference numbers
- Track application status
- Respond to queries from authorities
- Provide additional information if requested
- Monitor timelines and follow up proactively

Tools to use:
- submit_registration: Record submission details
- update_registration_status: Update to "submitted" then "under_review"
- get_registration_timeline: Track key dates

Step 6: Approval & Post-Approval Management
- Receive approval and certificate (COS number)
- Update system with approval details and expiry
- Plan for product launch
- Monitor expiry dates for renewals
- Track any post-market requirements
- Maintain compliance documentation

Tools to use:
- approve_registration: Record approval details
- update_registration: Add COS number, approval date, expiry
- get_expiring_registrations: Monitor upcoming renewals
- renew_registration: Initiate renewal process

4) Common pitfalls to avoid
- Missing document expiry dates (FSC, CoA often have 6-12 month validity)
- Incomplete INCI ingredient lists (must be complete and accurate)
- Non-compliant artwork (wrong symbols, missing warnings, language issues)
- Insufficient document notarization/legalization (varies by country)
- Mismatched product names across documents (consistency critical)
- Late renewal applications (apply 6 months before expiry)
- Missing local agent appointments (required in most countries)
- Inadequate formula disclosure (authorities may reject incomplete formulas)
- Wrong product classification (cosmetic vs drug has different pathways)
- Ignoring country-specific restrictions (ingredients allowed in EU may be banned elsewhere)

5) Critical ingredients and restrictions to check

Always verify against target country regulations:
- Heavy metals (lead, mercury, cadmium limits)
- Parabens (some countries restrict types/concentrations)
- Phthalates (often restricted or banned)
- Formaldehyde and formaldehyde-releasers
- Hydroquinone (banned/restricted in many countries)
- Kojic acid (restrictions vary)
- Retinoic acid and retinol (concentration limits)
- Alpha-hydroxy acids (concentration and pH limits)
- Nanoparticles (special labeling requirements in EU)
- Allergens (26 allergens must be labeled in EU if >0.001%)
- Colorants (must be from approved lists - CI numbers)
- Preservatives (type and concentration restrictions)
- Sunscreen filters (only approved filters allowed)
- Animal-derived ingredients (may be restricted/banned)

6) Deliverables templates

**Product Registration Submission Checklist:**
□ Product name and description
□ Product category classification
□ Complete INCI ingredient list with percentages
□ Country of manufacture
□ Manufacturer details and address
□ Letter of Authorization (LOA) - valid and notarized
□ Manufacturer Declaration
□ Certificate of Analysis (CoA) - within validity
□ Free Sale Certificate (FSC) - legalized as required
□ Formula Certificate
□ GMP Certificate (if required)
□ ISO Certificates (if available)
□ Product Artwork (labels, packaging, cartons)
□ Safety Assessment Report (where required)
□ Product samples (quantity as specified)
□ Sample Import License (where required)
□ Application forms completed
□ Fee payment receipt
□ Local agent authorization (where required)

**Registration Status Workflow:**
Draft → Pending Documents → Ready for Submission → Submitted → 
Under Review → Approved / Rejected → Active / Renewal Due

**Document Expiry Monitoring:**
- LOA: Check validity period (typically 1-3 years)
- FSC: Usually 6-12 months validity
- CoA: Often 6-12 months validity  
- GMP: Typically 2-3 years validity
- Registration Certificate: Country-specific (3-5 years)

Set reminders 60-90 days before expiry for renewals.

7) Follow-up protocols

**During Submission Review:**
- Check status weekly via online portal or with authority
- Respond to queries within 5 business days
- Provide additional documents promptly
- Keep applicant/manufacturer informed of progress
- Escalate delays beyond standard timelines

**Post-Approval:**
- Notify applicant immediately upon approval
- Provide certificate copies
- Update internal tracking system
- Set expiry reminders (6 months before)
- Plan renewal submissions in advance
- Monitor any post-market surveillance requirements

9) Pro tips
- Build relationships with local agents in each country
- Maintain a master template library for common documents
- Keep digital copies of all documents with version control
- Use a centralized tracking system (like this toolkit!)
- Leverage existing approvals for faster registration in new markets
- Plan for 6-9 months lead time for first registrations
- Always check for updated regulations (authorities update frequently)
- Consider GCC mutual recognition for Gulf countries
- Batch similar products for efficiency
- Maintain good manufacturing practices documentation continuously
- Train on country-specific nuances (each market is different)
- Network with other regulatory professionals (RAPS, TOPRA, local associations)
