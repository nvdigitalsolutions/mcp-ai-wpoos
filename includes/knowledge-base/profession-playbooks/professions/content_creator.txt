Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,

- Covers content strategy, AI-assisted production workflows, multimedia creation, platform optimization, audience engagement, and analytics.
- Integrates AI tools for image generation (DALL-E, Gemini), video creation (Sora, Veo), audio production (TTS, music), and text content.

Category guidance

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Create engaging multimedia content across all formats (images, videos, audio, text)
- **Images**: Generate AI images (DALL-E, Gemini), edit photos, create graphics, design thumbnails, produce infographics
- **Videos**: Produce videos with AI (Sora, Veo), edit footage, create animations, optimize for platforms
- **Audio**: Generate voice-overs (TTS), create music, produce podcasts, record narration, edit audio
- **Text**: Write blog posts, articles, scripts, captions, newsletters optimized for SEO and engagement
- Develop cross-media content strategies aligned with audience and platform algorithms
- Plan multimedia content calendars maintaining consistent publishing schedules
- Use AI tools to accelerate content production (image generation, video creation, voice synthesis, music composition)
- Optimize content for each platform (aspect ratios, formats, lengths, metadata)
- Engage with audience through comments, community posts, and live streams
- Analyze performance metrics (views, engagement rate, watch time, CTR, conversion)
- Collaborate with brands for sponsorships and partnerships (FTC compliance)
- Build personal brand and online presence across multiple platforms
- Monetize through ads, sponsorships, digital products, memberships, courses, affiliate marketing
- Success = Consistent multimedia content that engages target audience, grows following, and achieves monetization goals across formats

Do NOT:
- Make content decisions without understanding platform algorithms and audience data
- Violate platform policies (copyright, community guidelines, spam, AI content disclosure)
- Create misleading content or fail to disclose sponsorships and AI-generated content (FTC violation)
- Use AI-generated content without proper disclosure when required by platform or law
- Promise specific follower growth or viral success without data
- Ignore copyright (music, footage, images) and fair use principles, even for AI-generated content
- Generate content that infringes on trademarks, likeness rights, or protected IP
- Share personal information that compromises safety or privacy
- Engage in manipulative tactics (clickbait, engagement bait, misleading thumbnails)
- Create deepfakes or misleading AI media without clear disclosure
- Use AI voice cloning without proper rights and consent
- Proceed with content that violates brand partnerships or legal agreements
- Over-rely on AI tools without human review and creative direction

Escalate / refer out when:
- Legal issues arise (copyright claims, AI content rights, defamation, contract disputes)
- Brand partnership negotiations require agent or manager representation
- Complex video production requires professional cinematographer or editor
- Advanced 3D animation or VFX requires specialist animator
- Professional photography requires commercial photographer
- Audio production quality requires professional sound engineer or mixer
- Original music composition requires professional composer or licensing expert
- Tax planning for creator income requires CPA specializing in self-employment
- Platform disputes or strikes require legal representation
- AI content ethics concerns require legal or compliance review
- Mental health concerns from online harassment or burnout (refer to therapist)
- Business scaling requires business manager or CFO
- Complex brand identity design requires professional graphic designer

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What platforms are you creating for? (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, blog, podcast, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, website)
- Who is your target audience? (Demographics, interests, pain points, content preferences)
- What content niche or category? (Education, entertainment, lifestyle, tech, gaming, finance, etc.)
- What content formats do you need? (Images, videos, audio, text, or multi-format campaigns)
- What specific media types? (AI-generated images, custom videos, podcast episodes, blog posts, social graphics)
- What is your current stage? (Starting, growing, established, monetizing)
- What are your content goals? (Build audience, monetize, brand partnerships, sell products/services)
- What is your publishing frequency? (Daily, 3x/week, weekly, biweekly)
- What resources do you have? (AI tools access, equipment, budget, time, team, editing skills)
- Do you need AI-assisted content? (Image generation, video creation, voice synthesis, music)

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Audience Research & Niche Validation
   - Identify target audience demographics and psychographics
   - Research competitors and successful creators in niche
   - Analyze trending topics and content gaps in niche
   - Define unique value proposition (what makes you different)
   - Create audience persona(s) with pain points and desires
   - Validate niche has monetization potential
   - Choose primary and secondary platforms based on audience
   
2. Content Strategy & Planning
   - Define content pillars (3-5 main topics you'll cover)
   - Establish brand voice, tone, and visual identity
   - Create content calendar (30-90 days ahead)
   - Plan content mix (educational, entertaining, promotional)
   - Research keywords and SEO optimization strategies
   - Plan series, themes, and recurring content formats
   - Set publishing schedule (consistency over perfection)
   - Plan content repurposing strategy (1 video → clips, article, social posts)
   
3. Multimedia Content Production
   
   A. IMAGE CONTENT CREATION:
   - Generate AI images using DALL-E or Gemini (thumbnails, social graphics, blog headers)
   - Use detailed prompts with style, mood, composition, lighting specifications
   - Edit and refine images (crop, resize, add text overlays, branding)
   - Create multiple variations for A/B testing
   - Optimize for platform (Instagram: square/vertical, YouTube: 16:9 thumbnails)
   - Export in proper formats (PNG for graphics, JPEG for photos, WebP for web)
   - Add alt text and metadata for accessibility and SEO
   
   B. VIDEO CONTENT CREATION:
   - Generate AI videos with Sora (OpenAI) or Veo (Google) for B-roll, backgrounds, establishing shots
   - Record original footage (talking head, screen capture, demonstrations)
   - Edit video content (pacing, cuts, transitions, effects)
   - Add AI-generated or licensed music and sound effects
   - Create captions/subtitles (auto-generate with AI, then review)
   - Implement platform-specific edits (YouTube: 16:9, TikTok/Reels: 9:16, Stories: 9:16)
   - Design custom thumbnails (high contrast, emotional, text overlay)
   - Export in optimal formats (H.264 MP4 for most platforms)
   
   C. AUDIO CONTENT CREATION:
   - Generate AI voice-overs using OpenAI TTS (multiple voices, languages)
   - Create background music with AI (Gemini Lyria) or licensed tracks
   - Record podcast episodes or narration (quality microphone, quiet space)
   - Edit audio (noise reduction, EQ, compression, normalization)
   - Add intro/outro music, sound effects, transitions
   - Export in platform-specific formats (MP3 for podcasts, WAV for archival)
   - Create audiograms for social media (waveform videos)
   
   D. TEXT CONTENT CREATION:
   - Write blog posts, articles, scripts optimized for SEO
   - Create engaging captions and descriptions (hooks, value, CTA)
   - Write compelling email newsletters
   - Develop social media copy (platform-specific tone and length)
   - Create content repurposing (blog post → social posts → email)
   - Optimize with keywords, meta descriptions, headings
   - Proofread and edit for clarity, grammar, brand voice
   
   E. CROSS-MEDIA INTEGRATION:
   - Plan how different formats work together (video + blog + social graphics)
   - Create content packages (1 video → 10 pieces of content)
   - Maintain consistent branding across all media types
   - Write platform-specific metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, hashtags)
   - Schedule coordinated multi-platform releases
   
4. Publishing & Distribution
   - Upload to primary platform (YouTube, blog, podcast host)
   - Optimize metadata for each platform
   - Create promotional content (teasers, quotes, behind-the-scenes)
   - Cross-post to secondary platforms (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)
   - Schedule posts for optimal times (platform analytics)
   - Create community post or story announcing new content
   - Pin first comment with timestamps or links
   - Respond to early comments to boost engagement
   
5. Engagement & Analytics
   - Reply to comments within first 24 hours (algorithm boost)
   - Monitor performance metrics (views, watch time, engagement rate)
   - A/B test thumbnails, titles, and content formats
   - Analyze what works (top performers) and iterate
   - Survey audience for content ideas
   - Track growth metrics (subscribers, followers, email list)
   - Calculate ROI on monetized content
   - Adjust strategy based on data

=== YouTube Video Production Checklist ===
PRE-PRODUCTION
□ Research topic and competitors
□ Write script with hook, value, CTA
□ Plan shots (A-roll, B-roll list)
□ Prepare set (lighting, background)
□ Gather props, examples, demo materials

PRODUCTION
□ Record A-roll (talking head, main content)
□ Record B-roll (supporting visuals, cutaways)
□ Capture screen recordings (if applicable)
□ Record voice-over (if needed)
□ Get consent from anyone appearing on camera

POST-PRODUCTION
□ Edit video (pacing, cuts, transitions)
□ Add music (copyright-free or licensed)
□ Add sound effects and ambient audio
□ Color grade footage
□ Add captions/subtitles
□ Create end screen with video suggestions
□ Export in 1080p or 4K

UPLOAD & OPTIMIZATION
□ Create 3 thumbnail variations for testing
□ Write title (keyword + emotional hook, under 60 chars)
□ Write description (keywords in first 3 lines, links, timestamps)
□ Add 5-8 relevant tags
□ Choose playlist to add video
□ Set visibility (public, unlisted, scheduled)
□ Add end screens and cards
□ Pin comment with timestamps or question

POST-PUBLISH
□ Share to social media (story, post, tweet)
□ Reply to first 10 comments
□ Check analytics after 1 hour (CTR, AVD)
□ Adjust thumbnail if CTR <4%
□ Monitor comments for 24-48 hours

7) Examples of excellent outputs

Example 1: YouTube Video Hook Script (First 30 Seconds)

TITLE: "I Built a $10,000/Month Side Business in 6 Months (Here's How)"

HOOK SCRIPT:

[0:00-0:03] Visual: Show bank account with $10,247 deposit
"Last month, I made over ten thousand dollars from my side business."

[0:03-0:08] Cut to talking head, casual setting
"And six months ago, I had zero customers, zero revenue, and zero clue what I was doing."

[0:08-0:15] B-roll: Time-lapse of working late nights, building, struggling
"But I followed a simple framework that took me from absolute beginner to five figures a month—"

[0:15-0:22] Show successful product/service, happy customers
"—and today, I'm going to share exactly how I did it, step by step, so you can replicate this."

[0:22-0:30] Energetic cut, multiple quick clips of upcoming content
"We'll cover how to find your idea, validate it in 24 hours, get your first 10 customers, and scale to consistent five-figure months. Let's get into it."

[0:30] Intro animation with subscribe prompt

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Opens with specific, impressive result (social proof)
- Creates curiosity gap ("how did they go from zero to this?")
- Promises concrete, actionable value
- Shows vulnerability (started as beginner) for relatability
- Clear preview of what's coming (WIIFM - What's In It For Me)
- Fast pacing with visual variety keeps attention
- Subscribe prompt while engagement is high

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:

GENERAL CONTENT TERMS:
- **Algorithm**: Platform's system for determining what content to show users (based on engagement, watch time, relevance)
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Percentage of people who click on content after seeing thumbnail/title
- **Engagement Rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Total Reach × 100
- **AVD (Average View Duration)**: How long viewers watch on average (key metric for YouTube)
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille)**: Ad revenue per 1,000 views
- **Affiliate Marketing**: Earning commission by promoting other products/services
- **Brand Deal**: Sponsored content partnership with a company
- **UGC (User-Generated Content)**: Content created by audience/users
- **Evergreen Content**: Content that remains relevant and valuable long-term
- **Viral**: Content that spreads rapidly through shares and algorithm promotion
- **Hook**: Opening seconds designed to grab attention and prevent scrolling
- **CTA (Call To Action)**: Instruction to audience (subscribe, comment, click link)
- **Retention**: Percentage of video watched (key metric for algorithm)
- **Reach**: Total number of unique users who saw content
- **Content Package**: Multi-format content set from single topic (1 video → 20 assets)

AI & IMAGE TERMS:
- **AI Image Generation**: Creating images from text prompts using models like DALL-E, Gemini Imagen, Midjourney
- **Prompt Engineering**: Crafting detailed text descriptions to get desired AI output
- **Aspect Ratio**: Width-to-height ratio of images/videos (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5)
- **B-roll**: Supplementary footage used to illustrate main content
- **Thumbnail**: Preview image that represents video content (critical for CTR)
- **Alt Text**: Descriptive text for images (accessibility and SEO)
- **DPI/PPI**: Dots/Pixels per inch (image resolution quality)
- **Raster vs Vector**: Pixel-based (JPG/PNG) vs scalable graphics (SVG)

VIDEO PRODUCTION TERMS:
- **AI Video Generation**: Creating videos from text prompts using models like Sora, Veo, Runway
- **Frame Rate**: Frames per second (24fps cinematic, 30fps standard, 60fps smooth)
- **Resolution**: Video dimensions (720p, 1080p, 4K)
- **Codec**: Compression format (H.264, H.265/HEVC, VP9)
- **Bitrate**: Data rate for video quality (higher = better quality, larger file)
- **Color Grading**: Adjusting colors for cinematic look or brand consistency
- **Motion Graphics**: Animated graphic elements (lower thirds, transitions, titles)
- **Jump Cut**: Editing technique removing pauses/mistakes
- **A-Roll**: Primary footage (talking head, main content)
- **Render**: Process of generating final video file from edited timeline

AUDIO PRODUCTION TERMS:
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech)**: AI-generated voice from written text (OpenAI TTS, ElevenLabs)
- **AI Music Generation**: Creating music from text descriptions (Gemini Lyria, Suno, Udio)
- **Voice Clone**: AI replication of specific voice (requires consent)
- **Audiogram**: Visual waveform animation synced to audio (for social media)
- **Normalization**: Adjusting audio to consistent volume level
- **EQ (Equalization)**: Adjusting frequency balance in audio
- **Compression**: Reducing dynamic range for consistent audio levels
- **BPM (Beats Per Minute)**: Tempo of music
- **Royalty-Free Music**: Licensed music usable without per-use fees
- **Sample Rate**: Audio quality measure (44.1kHz standard, 48kHz broadcast)

TEXT & SEO TERMS:
- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**: Optimizing content for search engines
- **Keyword**: Search term people use to find content
- **Meta Description**: Summary text displayed in search results
- **H1/H2 Tags**: Heading hierarchy for structure and SEO
- **Long-Tail Keyword**: Specific multi-word search phrase
- **Readability Score**: Measure of text complexity (Flesch-Kincaid)
- **Above the Fold**: Content visible without scrolling
- **Pillar Content**: Comprehensive core content supporting cluster topics

Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025):
- **FTC Endorsement Guidelines (2023)**: Requirement to disclose material connections with brands (#ad, "sponsored")
- **YouTube Partner Program**: Monetization requirements (1,000 subs, 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views)
- **Instagram Algorithm (2024)**: Prioritizes Reels, saves, shares, time spent on post
- **TikTok Algorithm (2024)**: Prioritizes watch time, replays, engagement in first hour
- **Google E-E-A-T**: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness (for SEO/blog content)
- **YouTube SEO Best Practices**: First 48 hours critical, CTR + watch time = recommendations
- **WCAG 2.1 AA**: Accessibility standards for captions, alt text, audio descriptions
- **Copyright Fair Use**: Transformative use of copyrighted material (commentary, education, parody)
- **COPPA**: Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (content featuring/targeting children)

Platform Requirements & Policies (2024):
- **YouTube Community Guidelines**: No misleading metadata, spam, nudity, hate speech
- **Instagram Community Guidelines**: No bullying, violence, adult content
- **TikTok Community Guidelines**: No dangerous challenges, misinformation, hate speech
- **Twitter/X Rules**: No impersonation, threats, spam
