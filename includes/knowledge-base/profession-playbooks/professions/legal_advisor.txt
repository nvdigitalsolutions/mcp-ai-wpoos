Intent
- This file provides guidance for in-house counsel, corporate legal advisors, and business-aligned legal professionals.
- Focus on providing legal support to organizations while managing legal operations and strategic business partnerships.

Category guidance
- Balance legal rigor with business pragmatism; focus on risk management and enabling business objectives.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide legal advice and counsel to organizational stakeholders (executive team, departments, board)
- Review and negotiate contracts and commercial agreements
- Advise on regulatory compliance (corporate governance, securities, employment, data privacy, IP)
- Manage litigation and disputes (including selection and oversight of external counsel)
- Conduct legal risk assessments and develop mitigation strategies
- Draft and review corporate policies and procedures
- Support M&A transactions and corporate restructurings
- Manage legal department operations (budget, technology, vendors)
- Success = Legal support that enables business while minimizing risk and ensuring compliance

Do NOT:
- Provide legal services outside organizational scope (requires law firm engagement or separate attorney-client relationship)
- Guarantee legal outcomes or make business decisions on behalf of management
- Practice in areas requiring specialized licenses without credentials (patent prosecution, immigration representation)
- Create conflicts of interest between organization and personal interests
- Disclose confidential or privileged information without proper authority
- Ignore potential conflicts between duties to organization vs. individual employees
- Make unauthorized settlements or commit to positions without client approval

Escalate / refer out when:
- Matter requires specialized expertise beyond capabilities (patent lit, securities class actions, criminal)
- Conflict of interest exists (organization vs. individual employees, related party transactions)
- Significant litigation or regulatory investigation emerges (engage specialized outside counsel)
- Cross-border issues require local counsel in foreign jurisdictions
- Matter involves potential personal liability for officers/directors (independent counsel needed)
- Capacity constraints require additional resources (ALSPs, contract attorneys, law firms)
- Ethics questions arise about duties to organization vs. individuals

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What jurisdictions are involved? (Legal compliance requirements vary significantly by region)
- What is the business objective or problem you're trying to solve?
- What are the legal risks and potential liabilities involved?
- What is the timeline and business urgency (board meeting, deal closing, regulatory deadline)?
- What is the budget or cost constraint for legal support?
- Who are the key stakeholders and decision-makers?
- What contracts, regulations, or prior legal advice apply?
- Have outside counsel or other advisors been consulted?

1.5) Regional Variations - In-House Legal Practice

**NOTE**: In-house legal advisors must be familiar with jurisdictional legal systems (see lawyer playbook for details).

**United States:**
- Bar Admission: Must be licensed attorney in at least one state; corporate counsel rules allow limited multi-state practice
- Professional Conduct: Model Rules apply; duty to organization (not individuals); up-the-ladder reporting
- Privilege: Attorney-client privilege applies; work product doctrine protects strategic materials
- Regulatory: SEC (public companies), FTC, DOJ, state AGs, industry-specific regulators
- Corporate Governance: SOX compliance (public), Dodd-Frank, FCPA (anti-corruption), antitrust
- Employment Law: Federal (Title VII, FLSA, FMLA, ADA) + state employment laws (vary significantly)
- Contracts: State-specific contract law; UCC for commercial transactions
- IP: Federal (patents, trademarks, copyrights); state (trade secrets)
- Data Privacy: State laws (CCPA California, others); no comprehensive federal law
- Professional Org: Association of Corporate Counsel (ACC)

**United Kingdom:**
- Qualification: Solicitor (Law Society) or barrister; qualified lawyers from other jurisdictions can requalify
- Professional Conduct: SRA Code of Conduct (solicitors); in-house exemption from some requirements
- Privilege: Legal professional privilege (advice and litigation privilege)
- Regulatory: FCA (financial services), CMA (competition), ICO (data protection), HSE, sector regulators
- Corporate Governance: Companies Act 2006, UK Corporate Governance Code (listed companies)
- Employment Law: Employment Rights Act 1996, Equality Act 2010, TUPE, Working Time Regulations
- Contracts: English common law; some consumer protection EU-derived
- IP: UK IPO; European Patent Office; post-Brexit UK-specific regime
- Data Privacy: UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018
- Professional Org: The In-House Lawyers Group, Law Society In-House Division

**European Union:**
- Qualification: National bar admission; Lawyers' Services Directive allows cross-border practice
- Professional Conduct: CCBE Code of Conduct; national codes
- Privilege: Varies by member state; EU law recognizes lawyer-client confidentiality (limited)
- Regulatory: EU-level (competition, data protection, financial services) + national regulators
- Corporate Governance: Company Law Directive, Shareholder Rights Directive, national company laws
- Employment Law: EU directives (working time, equal treatment, collective redundancies) + national laws
- Contracts: National contract law varies (civil law codes)
- IP: EU trademarks, EU designs; European Patent Convention
- Data Privacy: GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) - directly applicable; national DPAs enforce
- Compliance: GDPR, Anti-Money Laundering Directives, sanctions compliance

**Canada:**
- Qualification: Provincial law society admission; typically qualify in province of employment
- Professional Conduct: Federation of Law Societies Model Code; provincial variations
- Privilege: Solicitor-client privilege protected; Wigmore test
- Regulatory: Federal (competition, securities (OSC), privacy) + provincial regulators
- Corporate Governance: CBCA (federal), provincial corporation acts, securities laws
- Employment Law: Federal (Canada Labour Code) for federally regulated; provincial employment standards
- Contracts: Provincial contract law (common law except Quebec civil law)
- IP: Federal (patents, trademarks, copyright, industrial designs)
- Data Privacy: PIPEDA (federal commercial), provincial laws (Quebec, BC, Alberta)
- Bilingual: Federal government and some provincially regulated entities require bilingual capacity

**Australia:**
- Qualification: State/territory admission; automatic national mutual recognition
- Professional Conduct: Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules; state-based regulation
- Privilege: Legal professional privilege (advice and litigation privilege)
- Regulatory: ASIC (corporations, financial services), ACCC (competition), OAIC (privacy), state/territory regulators
- Corporate Governance: Corporations Act 2001, ASX Corporate Governance Principles (listed)
- Employment Law: Fair Work Act 2009 (national system); state systems for some industries
- Contracts: Common law (state-based but nationally consistent)
- IP: Federal (IP Australia) for patents, trademarks, designs, copyright
- Data Privacy: Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles), Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
- Professional Org: Corporate Lawyers Association of Australia

**Key Regional Differences for In-House Counsel:**
- **Bar Admission**: US multi-state flexibility; others require qualification in jurisdiction
- **Privilege**: US strong attorney-client privilege; EU more limited; varies by jurisdiction
- **Regulatory Landscape**: US federal + 50 states complex; EU harmonized in some areas; varies
- **Data Privacy**: EU/UK GDPR comprehensive; US state-by-state patchwork; Canada PIPEDA; Australia Privacy Act
- **Employment Law**: Varies significantly (US at-will employment with exceptions; EU/UK stronger protections)
- **Corporate Governance**: US (SOX for public, state corporate law); UK (Companies Act); EU directives; Canada CBCA/provincial
- **Professional Obligations**: All have duty to organization, confidentiality; specifics vary

3) Default workflow
Use ACC standards for in-house legal management:

1. Business Intake & Issue Spotting
   - Meet with business stakeholder to understand request and context
   - Identify legal issues (contract, compliance, litigation, IP, employment, etc.)
   - Assess urgency, timeline, and business impact
   - Determine if matter requires outside counsel or can be handled internally
   - Check for conflicts of interest
   - Document scope, assumptions, and limitations
   - Estimate time and budget requirements

2. Legal Research & Analysis
   - Research applicable laws, regulations, and precedents
   - Review relevant contracts, policies, and prior advice
   - Analyze risks, liabilities, and compliance obligations
   - Consider best practices and industry standards
   - Identify alternative approaches and tradeoffs
   - Assess enforcement likelihood and potential damages
   - Benchmark against peers and market practices
   - Prepare risk matrix or decision framework

3. Strategic Counsel & Risk Management
   - Provide practical legal advice aligned with business objectives
   - Present options with pros, cons, and risk levels
   - Recommend preferred approach with clear rationale
   - Quantify legal and business risks where possible
   - Develop risk mitigation strategies and controls
   - Consider reputational, regulatory, and financial implications
   - Advise on disclosure obligations and reporting requirements
   - Document advice and recommendations in writing

4. Document Drafting & Negotiation
   - Draft or review contracts, policies, and legal documents
   - Negotiate terms with counterparties or their counsel
   - Protect company interests while enabling deal closure
   - Include appropriate risk allocation (indemnification, limitations of liability, warranties)
   - Ensure compliance with legal requirements and corporate policies
   - Use company-approved templates and playbooks when available
   - Obtain necessary internal approvals (business owner, CFO, CEO, board)
   - Maintain organized files and executed versions

5. Compliance & Risk Monitoring
   - Develop and implement compliance programs (SOX, GDPR, FCPA, etc.)
   - Create policies and training materials
   - Conduct compliance audits and risk assessments
   - Monitor regulatory changes and advise on implications
   - Manage internal investigations and whistleblower reports
   - Coordinate with compliance, audit, and risk management functions
   - Report to board audit or compliance committee as required
   - Document compliance efforts for regulatory examinations

6. Litigation & Dispute Management
   - Assess litigation risk and settlement value
   - Engage and supervise outside litigation counsel
   - Review pleadings, discovery, and motion strategy
   - Approve litigation budgets and fee arrangements
   - Participate in settlement negotiations
   - Coordinate with insurance carriers on coverage
   - Manage e-discovery and document hold processes
   - Keep leadership informed of significant developments

7. Legal Operations & Department Management
   - Manage legal department budget and forecast spend
   - Select and manage outside counsel and vendors (RFPs, rate negotiations, performance reviews)
   - Implement legal technology (CLM, matter management, e-billing, AI tools)
   - Track metrics and KPIs (spend, cycle time, SLAs, NPS)
   - Develop standardized processes and playbooks
   - Manage department staffing and professional development
   - Foster collaboration between legal and business teams
   - Benchmark against ACC standards and peer companies

8. Strategic Business Partnership
   - Participate in strategic planning and business development
   - Advise on M&A targets, due diligence, and integration
   - Support new product launches and market expansions
   - Advise on ESG initiatives and corporate social responsibility
   - Counsel board and executives on governance matters
   - Build relationships with business stakeholders
   - Translate legal issues into business language
   - Enable business while protecting company interests

4) Deliverables
- Legal opinions and memoranda
- Contract drafts and redlines (MSAs, NDAs, terms of service, SaaS agreements)
- Board resolutions and meeting minutes
- Compliance policies and training materials
- Due diligence reports and deal memos
- Litigation hold notices and document requests
- Regulatory filings and disclosures
- Risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Legal department metrics and benchmarking reports
- External counsel management reports (spend, performance)

Minimum viable: Clear legal advice + risk assessment + recommended action
Gold standard: Strategic counsel + practical solutions + documented analysis + proactive risk management

5) Quality checklist
Legal Analysis:
- [ ] Applicable laws and regulations identified correctly
- [ ] Relevant precedents and case law researched
- [ ] Contractual obligations and rights analyzed
- [ ] Legal risks quantified and prioritized
- [ ] Alternative approaches considered
- [ ] Advice consistent with prior guidance on similar issues

Business Alignment:
- [ ] Business objectives and constraints understood
- [ ] Legal advice is practical and actionable
- [ ] Cost-benefit tradeoffs explained clearly
- [ ] Timeline realistic for business needs
- [ ] Stakeholders identified and consulted
- [ ] Solutions enable business while managing risk

Risk Management:
- [ ] Key legal risks identified and assessed (likelihood x impact)
- [ ] Risk mitigation strategies developed
- [ ] Residual risks disclosed to decision-makers
- [ ] Insurance coverage reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Compliance obligations addressed
- [ ] Reputation and ESG risks considered

Documentation:
- [ ] Legal advice documented in writing (email or memo)
- [ ] Assumptions and limitations clearly stated
- [ ] Privileged/confidential markings applied
- [ ] Files organized and accessible
- [ ] Contracts executed and stored properly
- [ ] Version control maintained

Ethics & Professionalism:
- [ ] Conflicts of interest checked and resolved
- [ ] Attorney-client privilege protected
- [ ] Confidential information secured
- [ ] Professional conduct standards met (Model Rules)
- [ ] Independent judgment exercised
- [ ] Duty to organization (not individuals) maintained

7) Examples of excellent outputs

Example 1: Contract Negotiation Guidance
```
Re: SaaS Vendor Agreement - Key Issues

Business Team,

I've reviewed the proposed agreement with [Vendor]. Overall terms are market-standard, 
but three issues need resolution before we can sign:

1. DATA SECURITY (HIGH): Agreement lacks SOC 2 certification requirement and GDPR-compliant 
   data processing addendum. Given we'll share customer data, we need both. 
   → Proposed language attached. This is non-negotiable for GDPR compliance.

2. LIABILITY CAP (HIGH): Current cap is $10K but our annual spend is $100K. If their 
   service fails, our damages could exceed cap significantly.
   → Negotiate cap to 1x annual fees ($100K minimum) or 2x if possible.

3. TERMINATION (MEDIUM): 90-day notice required but they can terminate for convenience 
   with 30-day notice (asymmetric).
   → Ask for mutual 30-day or 60-day termination rights.

RECOMMENDATION: Negotiate items #1 and #2 (must-haves), push on #3 (nice-to-have). 
If they agree on data security and reasonable liability cap, I'll approve.

Redline attached. Let me know if you need me on the negotiation call.
```

Example 2: Risk Assessment for New Product
```
LEGAL RISK ASSESSMENT - AI-Powered Product Feature

OVERVIEW: Business proposes launching AI-powered feature that analyzes user data 
and provides personalized recommendations.

LEGAL RISKS:

HIGH RISK - Data Privacy (Likelihood: High | Impact: Severe)
- Feature requires processing personal data, potentially sensitive categories
- GDPR, CCPA require explicit consent, purpose limitation, data minimization
- Failure to comply: €20M fine (GDPR) + private litigation + reputational harm
→ MITIGATION: Conduct DPIA, update privacy policy, implement consent workflow, 
  data minimization by design

MEDIUM RISK - AI Bias & Discrimination (Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High)
- AI models may produce discriminatory outputs affecting protected classes
- EU AI Act (2024) classifies this as high-risk AI requiring conformity assessment
- Failure: Regulatory action, discrimination lawsuits, brand damage
→ MITIGATION: Bias testing pre-launch, ongoing monitoring, human review for 
  sensitive decisions, EU AI Act compliance roadmap

LOW RISK - IP Ownership (Likelihood: Low | Impact: Medium)
- Training data may include third-party IP
- AI-generated outputs ownership unclear in some jurisdictions
→ MITIGATION: Verify training data licenses, Terms of Service clarify output ownership

RECOMMENDATION:
Proceed with launch ONLY IF:
1. DPIA completed and approved by DPO
2. Privacy policy updated with user consent flow
3. Bias testing completed with acceptable results
4. EU AI Act compliance plan in place (6-month timeline)
5. Product liability insurance increased

Timeline: 4-6 weeks for legal workstream (parallel to product development).

Budget: €50K (DPIA consultant, AI audit, policy updates, insurance).
```

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **In-House Counsel**: Attorney employed by organization (vs. external law firm)
- **General Counsel (GC)**: Senior legal executive, often reports to CEO
- **Chief Legal Officer (CLO)**: GC with additional business/operational responsibilities
- **Legal Operations (Legal Ops)**: Function focused on department efficiency, technology, process improvement
- **Outside Counsel**: External law firms retained for specialized matters
- **ALSP (Alternative Legal Service Provider)**: Non-law firm providers (contract attorneys, legal tech, LPO)
- **Matter Management**: System and process for tracking legal work and spend
- **CLM (Contract Lifecycle Management)**: Software platform for contract drafting, negotiation, execution, renewal
- **Privilege**: Attorney-client privilege protecting confidential communications
- **Work Product Doctrine**: Protection for materials prepared in anticipation of litigation
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Situations where lawyer's duties to organization conflict with personal or other client interests

Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025):
- **ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct**: Ethical rules for attorneys (including in-house)
- **ACC (Association of Corporate Counsel) Law Department Management Benchmarking (2024-2025)**: Industry standards for legal department structure, spend, staffing
- **ACC Legal Department Operations Index**: Benchmarks for legal ops maturity
- **Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)**: Corporate governance and financial reporting (legal involvement in compliance)
- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**: EU data privacy law (applies to many US companies)
- **CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)**: California privacy law (model for other states)
- **EU AI Act (2024)**: Comprehensive AI regulation (compliance required for high-risk AI)
- **FCPA (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act)**: Anti-bribery and accounting provisions
- **Model Rule 1.13 (Organization as Client)**: Defines attorney duties when representing organizations

Industry Benchmarks (ACC 2024-2025):
- **Legal Spend**: Median 0.50% of company revenue
- **Outside Counsel Spend**: Median $1.8M annually
- **Legal Team Size**: Scales with revenue (median 4 for <$1B revenue, 158 for >$20B)
- **Preferred Law Firms**: Median company uses 10 firms (down from 14)
- **AI Adoption**: 52% of legal departments implemented AI tools (up from 34%)
- **Budget Allocation**: 5-10% to legal technology

Common Tools:
- **Legal Research**: Westlaw, LexisNexis, Bloomberg Law, Fastcase (in-house discount subscriptions)
- **Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)**: Ironclad, Docusign CLM, Icertis, Agiloft, LinkSquares
- **Matter Management**: Legal Tracker (Thomson Reuters), SimpleLegal, Legal Files
- **E-Billing**: Serengeti, Passport (Bellefield), TyMetrix 360
- **E-Discovery**: Relativity, Logikcull, Everlaw, Disco
- **Document Management**: iManage, NetDocuments, SharePoint
- **Entity Management**: Diligent Entities, Computershare, Athennian
- **AI & Legal Tech**: Kira Systems (due diligence), LawGeex (contract review), Harvey AI, LexisNexis Lexis+ AI
