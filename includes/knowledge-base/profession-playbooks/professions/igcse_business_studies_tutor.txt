Intent
- Playbook for IGCSE Business Studies tutoring (Cambridge 0450), covering business concepts,
  organizational functions, and analytical skills.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Tutor IGCSE Business Studies (0450) for all sections
- Explain business concepts with real-world examples
- Develop case study analysis skills
- Build exam technique for knowledge, application, analysis, and evaluation questions
- Success = Student develops business understanding, analytical skills, and achieves target grade

2) Quick intake questions
- Year, current/target grade?
- Understanding of business basics (strong, developing, weak)?
- Challenging topics (finance, marketing, operations, people)?
- Mock results, exam date?

3) Key Topics (Cambridge 0450)
**Section 1: Business Activity**
- Purpose and nature of business activity
- Types of business organizations (sole trader, partnership, company, franchise)
- Business objectives
- Stakeholders

**Section 2: People in Business**
- Recruitment and selection
- Training and development
- Motivation theory (Maslow, Herzberg)
- Leadership styles
- Communication
- Organizational structure

**Section 3: Marketing**
- Marketing mix (4Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion)
- Market research
- Product life cycle
- Pricing strategies
- Advertising and promotion

**Section 4: Operations Management**
- Production methods (job, batch, flow)
- Quality control and assurance
- Inventory management
- Location decisions

**Section 5: Financial Information**
- Cash flow forecasts
- Income statements (profit/loss)
- Balance sheets
- Break-even analysis
- Financial ratios

**Section 6: External Environment**
- Government influence
- Environmental and ethical issues
- Economic factors (inflation, unemployment, exchange rates)

4) Quality checklist
- [ ] Business terminology used accurately
- [ ] Real business examples integrated
- [ ] Case study details applied effectively
- [ ] Calculations practiced (break-even, ratios, profit margins)
- [ ] Analysis explains why/how (not just what)
- [ ] Evaluation makes judgments with justification
- [ ] Command words understood (define, explain, analyze, evaluate)

5) Exam Technique
- **Knowledge** (1-2 marks): Define, state, identify
- **Application** (2-3 marks): Apply to context/case study
- **Analysis** (3-4 marks): Explain why/how, show cause-effect
- **Evaluation** (4-6 marks): Make judgment, justify, consider both sides

Use connectives: Therefore, This means that, Consequently, However, On the other hand
