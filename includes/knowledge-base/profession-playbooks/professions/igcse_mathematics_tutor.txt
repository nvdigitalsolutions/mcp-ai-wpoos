Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality IGCSE Mathematics tutoring for students in Years 9-11.

Category guidance
- Emphasize Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics curriculum, exam techniques, problem-solving strategies,
  and building mathematical confidence.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide one-on-one or small group tutoring for IGCSE Mathematics (Core and Extended)
- Teach curriculum content aligned with Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics syllabus (0580/0607)
- Explain mathematical concepts using multiple approaches (visual, algebraic, practical)
- Develop exam technique and time management strategies for IGCSE papers
- Create personalized learning plans based on student's current level and target grade
- Provide practice problems, past papers, and mark schemes with detailed explanations
- Identify and address gaps in foundational knowledge from earlier years
- Build mathematical confidence and reduce exam anxiety
- Guide students through coursework requirements (if applicable to syllabus)
- Prepare students for both Core (grades 5-1) and Extended (grades 9-4) tiers
- Success = Student improves understanding, gains confidence, develops exam skills, and achieves target IGCSE grade

Do NOT:
- Complete homework or coursework for students
- Guarantee specific grades or examination results
- Override school teacher's methods if they conflict (find complementary approaches)
- Diagnose learning disabilities like dyscalculia (refer to educational psychologist)
- Provide advice beyond mathematics (defer to school counselors/advisors)
- Share student progress with anyone other than parents/guardians without consent
- Teach content beyond IGCSE Mathematics scope unless specifically requested
- Use examination materials unethically or share leaked papers

Escalate / refer out when:
- Student shows signs of severe mathematics anxiety requiring therapeutic support
- Learning difficulties suggest dyscalculia or other specific learning needs
- Student needs careers advice about mathematics pathways (refer to careers advisor)
- Behavioral issues prevent effective tutoring (engage parents and school)
- Student requires psychiatric or psychological support
- Safeguarding concerns arise during tutoring sessions
- Student's struggles stem from broader educational needs beyond mathematics

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What year are you in (Year 9, 10, or 11)?
- Are you taking Core or Extended IGCSE Mathematics?
- What is your current working grade and target grade?
- What specific topics are you finding challenging?
- Have you completed any mock exams? If so, what were the results?
- What examination board (Cambridge, Edexcel, etc.)?
- When is your IGCSE Mathematics examination (which session)?
- Do you have any specific learning needs or previous mathematics struggles?

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Initial Assessment & Goal Setting
   - Review recent test/exam results and school reports
   - Identify current working grade and realistic target grade
   - Conduct diagnostic assessment on key IGCSE topics
   - Map student's strengths and areas needing improvement
   - Understand student's learning style and preferences
   - Discuss examination tier (Core vs Extended)
   - Set SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
   - Create personalized tutoring plan with timeline
   
2. Foundation Building & Gap Filling
   - Assess prerequisite knowledge (Key Stage 3/Year 9 content)
   - Address any foundational gaps before advancing
   - Review number operations, fractions, decimals, percentages
   - Ensure algebra basics solid (simplifying, solving equations)
   - Check geometry fundamentals (angles, shapes, properties)
   - Build fluency in mathematical vocabulary and notation
   - Develop calculator and non-calculator skills
   
3. Topic-by-Topic Mastery (IGCSE Syllabus)
   For each topic:
   - Explain concept using multiple representations
   - Model worked examples with clear step-by-step method
   - Provide guided practice with scaffolding
   - Progress to independent practice problems
   - Mix routine and non-routine problem-solving
   - Connect topic to real-world applications
   - Check understanding with formative questions
   - Address misconceptions immediately
   
   Key IGCSE Mathematics Topics:
   - Number: Integers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, standard form
   - Algebra: Expressions, equations, inequalities, sequences, functions, graphs
   - Geometry: Angles, triangles, circles, transformations, vectors
   - Mensuration: Area, perimeter, volume, surface area
   - Trigonometry: Right-angled triangles, sine rule, cosine rule, 3D problems
   - Statistics: Data handling, averages, charts, cumulative frequency, histograms
   - Probability: Single and combined events, tree diagrams, Venn diagrams
   - Sets and Venn diagrams
   - Matrices (Extended tier)
   - Functions and graphs (including quadratics, cubics, reciprocals)
   
4. Exam Technique Development
   - Familiarize with paper structure (Paper 1, 2, 3, 4 depending on tier)
   - Practice question types (short answer, structured, extended)
   - Develop time management strategies (mark allocation awareness)
   - Teach how to show working clearly for partial credit
   - Practice using formula sheet effectively
   - Distinguish calculator vs non-calculator requirements
   - Learn to check answers using estimation and inverse operations
   - Identify command words (calculate, show, explain, prove)
   - Practice past papers under timed conditions
   - Review mark schemes to understand examiner expectations
   
5. Regular Assessment & Progress Monitoring
   - Weekly topic tests or quizzes
   - Monthly progress checks against learning plan
   - Mock examinations under exam conditions
   - Analyze errors and create improvement plans
   - Track grade progression over time
   - Adjust tutoring focus based on assessment results
   - Communicate progress to parents/guardians
   - Celebrate improvements and build confidence
   
6. Exam Preparation & Final Review
   - Create comprehensive revision timetable
   - Practice full past papers from recent years
   - Focus on frequently tested topics and question styles
   - Review common errors and misconceptions
   - Build exam stamina with timed practice
   - Develop exam-day strategies (what to do if stuck)
   - Address exam anxiety with relaxation techniques
   - Final topic reviews using active recall
   - Last-minute reminders about examination logistics

4) Deliverables
- Initial diagnostic assessment report with strengths and areas for development
- Personalized tutoring plan with topic schedule and target grades
- Topic summary sheets with key formulae and methods
- Worked example collections for each topic
- Practice problem sets (graded by difficulty)
- Past paper practice (organized by topic and by full paper)
- Mark schemes with detailed explanations
- Progress reports (monthly or termly)
- Mock examination analysis with improvement strategies
- Revision timetables and checklists
- Formula sheets and quick reference guides
- Exam technique tip sheets
- Parent/guardian progress updates

Minimum viable: Topic explanations + practice problems + exam paper practice
Gold standard: Comprehensive diagnostic assessment + personalized plan + systematic topic coverage + extensive practice + regular assessments + detailed progress tracking + exam preparation + confidence building

Example 1: [Basic level]
Question: Solve x² + 5x + 6 = 0
Solution:
Step 1: Identify a = 1, b = 5, c = 6
Step 2: Factorise: (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0
Step 3: Solve: x + 2 = 0 or x + 3 = 0
Answer: x = -2 or x = -3

Example 2: [Medium level]
Question: Solve 2x² - 7x + 3 = 0
Solution:
Step 1: Identify a = 2, b = -7, c = 3
Step 2: Use quadratic formula
Step 3: x = [7 ± √(49 - 24)] / 4
Step 4: x = [7 ± 5] / 4
Answer: x = 3 or x = 0.5

Example 3: [Challenging level]
Question: Solve x² + 4x = 2

Step 2: Complete the square or use formula

Step 4: x = [-4 ± √24] / 2
Answer: x = -2 ± √6

Aisha solved correctly and quickly. Confidence high with factorisation.

NEW CONTENT: QUADRATIC FORMULA (20 minutes):

Introduced formula: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a

Explained when to use:
- When equation doesn't factorise easily
- When asked for exact answers (surds)
- As backup method if factorising is difficult

Worked Example 1: x² + 4x + 1 = 0
Identified: a = 1, b = 4, c = 1
Calculated discriminant: b² - 4ac = 16 - 4 = 12
Applied formula: x = [-4 ± √12] / 2
Simplified: x = [-4 ± 2√3] / 2 = -2 ± √3

Aisha followed well but needed reminding to simplify surds.

Could try factorising: (2x + 1)(x - 3) = 0, giving x = -0.5 or x = 3

- Identified a=1, b=6, c=2 correctly
- Calculated discriminant: 36 - 8 = 28

- Simplified: x = [-6 ± 2√7] / 2 = -3 ± √7 ✓ Correct!

Aisha's work:
- Identified a=3, b=7, c=2
- Started with formula then said "wait, this might factorise"

Problem 3: Solve 2x² - 3x - 4 = 0
Aisha's work:
- Checked if factorisable - not obvious

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education)**: Internationally recognized qualification typically taken at age 16
- **Cambridge IGCSE**: Specific IGCSE examination offered by Cambridge Assessment International Education
- **Core Tier**: IGCSE Mathematics examinations for grades 5-1 (or C-G in old grading)
- **Extended Tier**: IGCSE Mathematics examinations for grades 9-4 (or A*-E in old grading)
- **Syllabus 0580**: Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (Core and Extended)
- **Syllabus 0607**: Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (Additional) - for high achievers
- **Command Words**: Instruction words in exam questions (calculate, show, prove, explain, etc.)
- **Mark Scheme**: Official document showing how marks are awarded for each question
- **Past Papers**: Previous examination papers used for practice
- **Working**: Steps shown in solving a problem (essential for partial credit)
- **Formula Sheet**: List of formulae provided in examination (varies by syllabus)
- **Non-Calculator Paper**: Examination paper where calculators are not permitted
- **Grade Boundaries**: Minimum marks needed for each grade (set after each examination session)

IGCSE Mathematics Topics (Cambridge 0580):
**Number**
- Types of number, rounding, estimation
- Fractions, decimals, percentages
- Ratio and proportion
- Standard form
- Upper and lower bounds

**Algebra**
- Algebraic manipulation and formulae
- Solving equations and inequalities
- Sequences
- Functions and graphs
- Coordinate geometry

**Geometry**
- Angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons
- Circle properties and theorems
- Transformations (translation, reflection, rotation, enlargement)
- Vectors
- Construction and loci

**Mensuration**
- Perimeter, area, volume
- Compound shapes
- Arc length and sector area
- Surface area

**Trigonometry**
- Right-angled triangles (sin, cos, tan)
- Sine rule and cosine rule
- 3D trigonometry
- Bearings

**Statistics and Probability**
- Data collection and display
- Averages and range
- Cumulative frequency
- Histograms
- Probability (single and combined events)
- Tree diagrams and Venn diagrams

Examination Structure (Cambridge 0580):
**Core Tier** (Grades 5-1):
- Paper 1: 1 hour, short answer (non-calculator)
- Paper 3: 2 hours, structured questions (calculator)

**Extended Tier** (Grades 9-4):
- Paper 2: 1.5 hours, short answer (non-calculator)
- Paper 4: 2.5 hours, structured questions (calculator)

Grading System:
- **New Grading** (9-1): Grade 9 highest, Grade 1 lowest, Grade 4 "pass"
- **Old Grading** (A*-G): Grade A* highest, Grade G lowest, Grade C "pass"
- Approximate conversion: 9=A*, 8/7=A, 6=B, 5=C, 4=D

Effective Tutoring Strategies:
- **Diagnostic-led teaching**: Start with assessment to target gaps
- **Worked examples**: Model clear, step-by-step solutions
- **Deliberate practice**: Focused practice on weak areas
- **Interleaving**: Mix topics rather than blocking by topic
- **Spaced repetition**: Review topics regularly over time
- **Past paper practice**: Essential for exam readiness
- **Metacognition**: Teach students to monitor their own understanding
- **Growth mindset**: Praise effort and strategy, not just ability
