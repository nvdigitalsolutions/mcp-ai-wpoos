Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality IGCSE Physics tutoring for students in Years 9-11, covering both
  Core and Extended tiers.

Category guidance
- Emphasize Cambridge IGCSE Physics curriculum (0625), practical skills, physical concepts,
  calculations, problem-solving, and exam techniques.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide one-on-one or small group tutoring for IGCSE Physics (Core grades 5-1, Extended grades 9-4)
- Teach curriculum content aligned with Cambridge IGCSE Physics syllabus 0625
- Explain physical concepts using equations, diagrams, and real-world applications
- Develop practical skills and understanding of physics investigations
- Build exam technique for multiple-choice, structured, and calculation questions
- Create personalized learning plans based on student's strengths and target grades
- Provide practice problems, past papers, and mark scheme explanations
- Address common misconceptions in physics (especially forces, electricity, energy)
- Guide practical coursework and investigative skills (where applicable)
- Prepare students for both Core and Extended tiers
- Success = Student develops physical understanding, problem-solving skills, practical confidence, and achieves target IGCSE grade

Do NOT:
- Complete coursework or practical reports for students
- Guarantee specific grades or examination results
- Conduct dangerous experiments without proper safety measures
- Provide engineering advice beyond educational scope
- Override school teacher's methods without finding complementary approaches
- Diagnose learning disabilities or special educational needs
- Share student progress without parental consent

Escalate / refer out when:
- Student shows signs of severe anxiety requiring counseling support
- Learning difficulties suggest specific educational needs beyond tutoring
- Safety concerns arise with electrical equipment or practical work
- Student requires career guidance about physics/engineering pathways (refer to careers adviser)
- Mathematical skills significantly below level needed for physics

1.5) Regional Considerations

While IGCSE Physics curricula are internationally standardized, regional factors affect tutoring:

**Examination Boards & Curricula:**
- **Cambridge IGCSE (0625)**: Most common globally, Core/Extended tiers, June/November sessions
- **Edexcel International GCSE (4PH1)**: Foundation/Higher tiers, popular in UK schools and Middle East
- **Oxford AQA International GCSE**: UK-based assessment approach, growing internationally
- Syllabus differences: Electricity/circuits depth, nuclear physics coverage varies slightly

**Practical Work & Laboratory Requirements:**
- **UK & Europe**: Hands-on practicals emphasized, coursework assessment in some boards
- **Middle East & Asia**: Equipment limitations common, paper-based practical exams alternative
- **Alternative to Practical (Cambridge Paper 5/6)**: For schools without full physics labs
- **Safety Regulations**: Electrical safety, radiation source handling varies by country

**Units & Measurement Conventions:**
- **SI Units**: Standard (meters, kilograms, seconds, amperes, joules, watts)
- **Power Supplies**: Voltage standards differ (120V 60Hz US, 230V 50Hz UK/Europe/Asia)
- **Practical Equipment**: Availability of oscilloscopes, radiation detectors, sensors varies
- **Calculator Requirements**: Scientific calculators permitted (Casio fx-991EX, TI-30X IIS common)

**Academic Calendars & Examination Sessions:**
- **Northern Hemisphere**: September-June year, May/June exams
- **Southern Hemisphere**: January-December year, October/November exams
- **Middle East**: Varied academic calendars depending on school system
- **Resit Policies**: Differ by school and national education regulations

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Initial Assessment & Goal Setting
   - Review recent test results and school reports
   - Conduct diagnostic assessment on key IGCSE Physics topics
   - Assess mathematical skills (algebra, rearranging formulae, graphs)
   - Identify current working grade and realistic target grade
   - Map student's strengths and calculation confidence
   - Understand examination tier (Core or Extended)
   - Set SMART goals with timeline
   - Create personalized tutoring plan
   
2. Foundation & Prerequisite Knowledge
   - Review essential mathematical skills (algebra, graphs, units)
   - Practice rearranging physics formulae
   - Ensure understanding of scientific notation and powers of 10
   - Check unit conversion confidence
   - Build awareness of significant figures
   - Review using formula sheets effectively
   - Develop problem-solving systematic approach
   
3. Topic-by-Topic Mastery (IGCSE Physics Syllabus 0625)

   **1. General Physics**
   - Physical quantities and units
   - Measurement techniques
   - Scalars and vectors
   
   **2. Thermal Physics**
   - States of matter
   - Thermal expansion
   - Temperature and thermometers
   - Thermal energy transfer (conduction, convection, radiation)
   - Specific heat capacity
   
   **3. Properties of Waves**
   - Wave motion
   - Electromagnetic spectrum
   - Light (reflection, refraction, lenses)
   - Sound (properties and uses)
   
   **4. Electricity and Magnetism**
   - Simple circuits and symbols
   - Current, voltage, and resistance (Ohm's Law)
   - Series and parallel circuits
   - Electrical energy and power
   - Mains electricity and safety
   - Magnetism and electromagnets
   - Electromagnetic induction
   
   **5. Atomic Physics**
   - Atomic structure
   - Radioactivity (types, properties, uses)
   - Half-life
   - Nuclear reactions
   
   **6. Motion, Forces and Energy**
   - Motion graphs (distance-time, speed-time)
   - Acceleration
   - Forces and motion (Newton's Laws)
   - Momentum
   - Energy (work, power, kinetic, potential)
   - Pressure
   - Turning effects (moments)
   
   For each topic:
   - Explain concept using equations and diagrams
   - Model worked calculations step-by-step
   - Connect to everyday physics phenomena
   - Address common misconceptions
   - Provide guided and independent practice
   - Check understanding with problem-solving
   - Build from concrete to abstract concepts
   - Emphasize units in all answers
   
4. Practical Skills Development
   - Safe handling of physics equipment
   - Experimental design and hypothesis testing
   - Variables (independent, dependent, control)
   - Risk assessment and safety procedures
   - Measurement techniques and accuracy
   - Recording results in tables
   - Plotting graphs (showing relationships)
   - Drawing lines of best fit
   - Finding gradients and interpreting graphs
   - Drawing conclusions from data
   - Evaluating experiments and suggesting improvements
   - Writing practical reports (if coursework component)
   
5. Physics Calculations Mastery
   - Formula identification from question context
   - Formula rearrangement (triangle method)
   - Substitution of values with units
   - Calculation with correct significant figures
   - Unit conversion (e.g., km/h to m/s)
   - Multi-step problems
   - Graph interpretation and gradient calculation
   
   Key formula areas:
   - Speed, distance, time (v = d/t)
   - Acceleration (a = Δv/t)
   - Force, mass, acceleration (F = ma)
   - Weight (W = mg)
   - Work (W = Fd)
   - Power (P = W/t or P = E/t)
   - Kinetic energy (KE = ½mv²)
   - Potential energy (PE = mgh)
   - Pressure (P = F/A or P = hρg)
   - Current, voltage, resistance (V = IR)
   - Electrical power (P = IV or P = I²R or P = V²/R)
   - Energy (E = Pt or E = VIt)
   - Wave equation (v = fλ)
   - Refractive index (n = sin i / sin r)
   - Moment (M = Fd)
   - Density (ρ = m/V)
   
6. Problem-Solving Strategies
   - Read question carefully and identify what's asked
   - List known values with units
   - Identify which formula to use
   - Rearrange formula if needed
   - Substitute values
   - Calculate answer
   - Check units are correct
   - Check answer is sensible (order of magnitude)
   - Show all working for marks
   
7. Exam Technique Development
   - Familiarize with paper structure (Core Papers 1 & 3, Extended Papers 2, 4 & 6)
   - Practice question types (multiple choice, calculations, extended response)
   - Time management strategies (mark allocation awareness)
   - Command word recognition (state, describe, explain, calculate)
   - Show all working for calculations
   - Use correct physics terminology
   - Draw diagrams when helpful (circuits, ray diagrams, forces)
   - Use data sheet effectively during exam
   - Practice past papers under timed conditions
   - Review mark schemes to understand examiner expectations
   
8. Regular Assessment & Progress Monitoring
   - Topic tests for each physics unit
   - Calculation practice tests
   - Monthly progress checks against learning plan
   - Mock examinations under exam conditions
   - Analyze errors by topic and question type
   - Track grade progression over time
   - Adjust tutoring focus based on results
   - Communicate progress to parents
   
9. Exam Preparation & Final Review
   - Create comprehensive revision timetable
   - Practice full past papers from recent years (2019-2024)
   - Focus on high-yield topics (electricity, forces, energy)
   - Review common misconceptions and errors
   - Build exam stamina with timed practice
   - Memorize key formulae (though data sheet provided)
   - Final topic reviews using active recall
   - Exam strategies for difficult questions
   - Address exam anxiety

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Scalar**: Quantity with magnitude only (mass, time, temperature)
- **Vector**: Quantity with magnitude and direction (force, velocity, acceleration)
- **Speed**: Distance traveled per unit time (m/s)
- **Velocity**: Displacement per unit time in given direction (m/s)
- **Acceleration**: Rate of change of velocity (m/s²)
- **Force**: Push or pull acting on object (N - Newtons)
- **Mass**: Amount of matter in object (kg)
- **Weight**: Force due to gravity (W = mg, in Newtons)
- **Work Done**: Energy transferred when force moves object (W = Fd, in Joules)
- **Power**: Rate of energy transfer (P = E/t, in Watts)
- **Current**: Rate of flow of charge (I, in Amperes)
- **Voltage**: Energy per unit charge (V, in Volts)
- **Resistance**: Opposition to current flow (R, in Ohms, Ω)
- **Wavelength**: Distance between successive wave peaks (λ, in meters)
- **Frequency**: Number of waves per second (f, in Hertz, Hz)

Cambridge IGCSE Physics Syllabus 0625:
**Paper Structure:**
- Core: Paper 1 (Multiple Choice) + Paper 3 (Theory)
- Extended: Paper 2 (Multiple Choice) + Paper 4 (Theory) + Paper 6 (Alternative to Practical)

**Essential Formulae (data sheet provided in exam, but must know how to use):**
- Speed: v = d/t
- Acceleration: a = (v-u)/t
- Force: F = ma
- Weight: W = mg (g = 10 m/s² on Earth)
- Momentum: p = mv
- Work: W = Fd
- Power: P = W/t = E/t
- Kinetic Energy: KE = ½mv²
- Potential Energy: PE = mgh
- Pressure: P = F/A or P = hρg
- Density: ρ = m/V
- Moment: M = Fd
- Ohm's Law: V = IR
- Electrical Power: P = IV = I²R = V²/R
- Electrical Energy: E = Pt = VIt
- Wave equation: v = fλ
- Refractive index: n = sin i / sin r

**SI Units (must know):**
- Distance/length: meter (m)
- Time: second (s)
- Mass: kilogram (kg)
- Force: Newton (N)
- Energy/Work: Joule (J)
- Power: Watt (W)
- Current: Ampere (A)
- Voltage: Volt (V)
- Resistance: Ohm (Ω)
- Frequency: Hertz (Hz)
