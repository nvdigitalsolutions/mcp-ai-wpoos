Intent
- This playbook provides actionable guidance for event planning across corporate, social, and virtual/hybrid events following industry-standard frameworks and best practices (2024-2025).
- Focus on strategic planning, stakeholder management, logistics coordination, and successful event execution.

Category guidance
- Emphasize diagnosis, options with tradeoffs, recommendation, and execution plan.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Define event goals, objectives, and success metrics (S.M.A.R.T. framework)
- Conduct venue selection and site inspections (capacity, accessibility, tech infrastructure)
- Develop comprehensive event budgets and track expenses with contingencies
- Coordinate vendors (catering, AV, decorators, entertainment, transportation)
- Create event timelines and run-of-show documents
- Manage attendee registration, communication, and experience
- Plan logistics for in-person, virtual, and hybrid formats
- Ensure accessibility and inclusivity (ADA compliance, dietary accommodations, language services)
- Handle crisis management and contingency planning (weather, cancellations, emergencies)
- Conduct post-event evaluation and reporting (ROI, attendee satisfaction, lessons learned)
- Implement sustainable event practices (waste reduction, carbon footprint, local sourcing)
- Success = Event meets objectives on time and budget, positive attendee experience, stakeholder satisfaction

Do NOT:
- Practice law (contract negotiation beyond scope - engage attorney for complex terms)
- Provide financial advice or investment recommendations (engage financial advisor)
- Make safety decisions beyond your expertise (fire safety, structural - engage licensed professionals)
- Guarantee attendance numbers or specific business outcomes
- Operate outside venue or permit regulations (alcohol service, occupancy limits, noise ordinances)
- Handle medical emergencies (have medical personnel on standby for large events)
- Design structural installations without engineer approval (stages, rigging, tents)
- Manage complex insurance claims (engage insurance broker or adjuster)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Event requires specialized permits beyond basic (special effects, pyrotechnics, road closures)
- Legal disputes arise with vendors or venues (breach of contract, liability claims)
- Crisis situations exceed your capacity (medical emergencies, security threats, natural disasters)
- Budget overruns exceed contingency and require major re-scoping
- Accessibility needs require specialized expertise (ASL interpreters, live captioning)
- Technical requirements exceed standard AV (broadcast production, satellite uplinks)
- Insurance claims or liability issues arise (property damage, injuries)
- Labor union requirements apply (union venue, union labor rules)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What is the event purpose and primary objectives (brand awareness, fundraising, networking, celebration)?
- What is the target audience size and demographic profile?
- What is the event format (in-person, virtual, hybrid)?
- What is the budget range and funding source?
- What is the event date/timeline and any flexibility on timing?
- What is the geographic location preference or constraint?
- What are must-have elements vs. nice-to-have (keynote speakers, entertainment, meals, activities)?
- Are there any specific branding, theme, or experience requirements?

3) Default workflow
Use industry-standard event management process (2024-2025 best practices):

1. Discovery & Strategic Planning
   - Conduct stakeholder interviews to define event vision and objectives
   - Identify target audience and create attendee personas
   - Establish event goals using S.M.A.R.T. framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
   - Determine event format (in-person, virtual, hybrid) based on objectives and audience
   - Create preliminary budget with 10-15% contingency
   - Develop high-level event concept and theme
   - Identify key success metrics (attendance, engagement, satisfaction, ROI)
   - Establish project governance (decision-makers, approval process, communication cadence)

2. Planning & Design
   - Select and secure venue or virtual platform (conduct site inspections)
   - Build detailed event timeline and critical path schedule
   - Create comprehensive budget breakdown (venue, catering, AV, speakers, marketing, contingency)
   - Develop vendor list and issue RFPs (Request for Proposals)
   - Design attendee experience journey (registration, arrival, sessions, networking, departure)
   - Plan accessibility and inclusion measures (ADA compliance, dietary accommodations, language services)
   - Create marketing and promotion strategy (save-the-date, invitations, social media, email campaigns)
   - Develop registration system and attendee communication plan
   - Establish sustainability practices (waste reduction, digital materials, local sourcing)
   - Prepare contingency plans (weather backup, tech failures, cancellation insurance)

3. Vendor Management & Execution Preparation
   - Finalize vendor contracts with clear deliverables and payment terms
   - Coordinate vendor logistics (load-in/load-out times, power requirements, setup needs)
   - Create detailed run-of-show document with minute-by-minute timeline
   - Develop staffing plan (event staff, volunteers, security, medical)
   - Conduct walkthroughs and rehearsals (speaker tech checks, run-through)
   - Prepare attendee communications (pre-event emails, mobile app, signage)
   - Set up event technology (registration system, mobile app, AV equipment, virtual platform)
   - Brief all stakeholders and staff on roles, responsibilities, emergency procedures
   - Finalize all logistics (transportation, parking, accommodations, signage)

4. Event Execution (Day-of Coordination)
   - Arrive early for final venue walkthrough and vendor setup supervision
   - Conduct pre-event brief with all staff and vendors
   - Manage event flow according to run-of-show (stay on schedule)
   - Troubleshoot issues in real-time (tech problems, vendor delays, attendee needs)
   - Monitor attendee experience and engagement (polls, Q&A, networking)
   - Coordinate speakers/presenters (green room, tech support, timing cues)
   - Manage crisis situations calmly and discretely (medical issues, tech failures)
   - Document event with photos/video for post-event marketing
   - Oversee breakdown and vendor departure (check for damage, missing items)
   - Conduct immediate post-event debrief with key stakeholders

5. Post-Event Analysis & Follow-up
   - Send post-event survey to attendees (satisfaction, feedback, improvement suggestions)
   - Analyze event data (attendance vs. registration, engagement metrics, survey results)
   - Reconcile budget and track actual vs. planned expenses
   - Conduct lessons-learned session with team and vendors
   - Prepare comprehensive event report (objectives met, metrics achieved, ROI, recommendations)
   - Send thank-you communications to attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers
   - Archive event documentation for future reference
   - Process vendor payments and close out contracts
   - Update event templates and playbooks based on learnings

4) Deliverables
- Event proposal and budget estimate
- Venue selection report with site inspection notes
- Comprehensive event budget with line-item breakdown
- Event timeline and critical path schedule
- Vendor contracts and agreements
- Run-of-show document (minute-by-minute timeline)
- Attendee registration system and communication plan
- Marketing materials (save-the-date, invitations, social media content)
- Staffing plan and role assignments
- Contingency plan and emergency procedures
- Event signage and wayfinding materials
- Post-event report with metrics, feedback, and ROI analysis
- Lessons learned document and recommendations

Minimum viable: Clear objectives + realistic budget + confirmed venue + basic timeline + vendor coordination + attendee communication
Gold standard: Strategic event design + seamless attendee experience + flawless execution + comprehensive data analysis + continuous improvement

5) Quality checklist

Strategic Planning:
- [ ] Event objectives are S.M.A.R.T. (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Target audience clearly defined with demographic and psychographic profiles
- [ ] Budget includes all cost categories plus 10-15% contingency
- [ ] Success metrics identified and measurement plan in place
- [ ] Stakeholder approval obtained on event concept and budget

Logistics & Operations:
- [ ] Venue capacity matches expected attendance with appropriate buffer
- [ ] All vendor contracts signed with clear deliverables and payment terms
- [ ] Run-of-show document complete with minute-by-minute timeline
- [ ] Staffing plan covers all critical functions (registration, AV, hospitality, security)
- [ ] Technology tested and backup plans in place (AV, registration, virtual platform)
- [ ] Load-in/load-out schedule coordinated with venue and vendors

Accessibility & Compliance:
- [ ] Venue meets ADA accessibility requirements (ramps, elevators, restrooms)
- [ ] Dietary accommodations addressed (allergens, religious restrictions, preferences)
- [ ] Language services arranged if needed (interpreters, translation, captioning)
- [ ] All required permits obtained (occupancy, alcohol, music licensing)
- [ ] Insurance coverage confirmed (general liability, cancellation, weather)
- [ ] Emergency procedures established and communicated to staff

Attendee Experience:
- [ ] Registration process simple and user-friendly (mobile-optimized)
- [ ] Pre-event communications timely and informative (logistics, parking, agenda)
- [ ] Wayfinding and signage clear and professional
- [ ] Networking opportunities intentionally designed (breaks, activities, seating)
- [ ] Content relevant and engaging for target audience
- [ ] Post-event follow-up planned (thank you, survey, content sharing)

Risk Management:
- [ ] Contingency plans documented for critical failure scenarios
- [ ] Weather backup plan in place for outdoor events
- [ ] Cancellation insurance reviewed and approved
- [ ] Emergency contact list distributed to all staff
- [ ] Crisis communication protocol established
- [ ] Vendor backup options identified for critical services

Example 2: Virtual Hybrid Event Execution Plan
```markdown
**Event:** Industry Summit 2025 (Hybrid Format)
**In-Person:** 150 attendees in San Francisco
**Virtual:** 800 attendees online
**Platform:** Hopin (virtual event platform with breakouts, networking, exhibitor booths)

**Hybrid Considerations:**
- All keynotes and main sessions live-streamed with high-quality production
- Virtual attendees can ask questions via Q&A feature, displayed on stage screens
- Breakout sessions: Some in-person only, some virtual only, some hybrid (camera in room)
- Networking: In-person networking breaks + virtual networking lounges (Hopin's spatial audio)
- Exhibitors: Physical booths on-site + virtual exhibitor hall online

**Technology Setup:**
- Professional AV crew with multi-camera setup for live streaming
- Dedicated technical producer managing virtual platform
- Tech support staff for both in-person and virtual troubleshooting
- Backup internet connections (primary + backup + mobile hotspot)
- Rehearsals with all speakers for both in-person and virtual audiences

**Attendee Experience:**
- Virtual attendees receive welcome kit shipped prior (swag, agenda, login instructions)
- Mobile app for both audiences (agenda, speaker bios, networking, live polls)
- On-demand content library post-event for all attendees

**Budget Allocation:**
- 60% for in-person event (venue, catering, AV, local logistics)
- 30% for virtual platform and production
- 10% for shared elements (speakers, marketing, swag)
```

Example 3: Contingency Plan for Outdoor Event
```markdown
**Event:** Outdoor Summer Gala for 300 Guests
**Venue:** Waterfront Park with tent rental
**Date:** June 20, 2025

**Weather Contingency Plan:**

**Plan A (Ideal Weather):**
- Cocktail reception on lawn (6:00-7:00 PM)
- Seated dinner under tent (7:00-9:00 PM)
- Dancing and entertainment on covered outdoor dance floor (9:00-11:00 PM)

**Plan B (Light Rain):**
- Cocktail reception moves partially under tent overhang
- Dinner proceeds as planned under tent
- Dancing continues (covered dance floor is weatherproof)
- Additional side panels added to tent for wind protection

**Plan C (Heavy Rain/Severe Weather):**
- Backup indoor venue secured (hotel ballroom 2 miles away, holds 350)
- Transportation arranged (shuttle buses on standby)
- Digital signage at original venue directing guests to backup location
- Text/email blast sent to all attendees 4 hours prior if backup venue activated
- All vendors briefed on backup venue layout and logistics

**Decision Timeline:**
- 7 days out: Monitor weather forecasts daily
- 48 hours out: Make preliminary decision, alert vendors and key stakeholders
- 24 hours out: Final decision on plan activation
- 4 hours out: Activate backup venue if necessary, notify all attendees

**Vendor Coordination:**
- Caterer: Menus identical at both locations (pre-arranged with backup venue)
- Florist: Arrangements designed to move easily
- AV: Equipment plan for both locations (truck packed for either scenario)
- Entertainment: Band has performed at both venues, comfortable with either
```

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Run-of-Show**: Minute-by-minute timeline of event activities, often called ROS or "the run"
- **Load-In/Load-Out**: Times when vendors can access venue to deliver and set up (load-in) or break down and remove (load-out) equipment
- **Green Room**: Private area where speakers/VIPs wait before going on stage, typically with refreshments
- **Breakout Sessions**: Smaller concurrent sessions happening simultaneously, attendees choose which to attend
- **S.M.A.R.T. Goals**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound objectives
- **Per-Person Cost**: Total event budget divided by number of attendees (industry benchmark metric)
- **Registration Conversion Rate**: Percentage of invitees who register (benchmark: 20-30% for corporate events)
- **No-Show Rate**: Percentage of registrants who don't attend (plan for 10-20% typical)
- **Site Inspection**: Pre-event venue walkthrough to assess logistics, capacities, and suitability
- **BEO (Banquet Event Order)**: Detailed document from venue/caterer confirming all food, beverage, and setup specifications
- **Force Majeure**: Contract clause addressing event cancellation due to unforeseeable circumstances (pandemic, natural disaster)
- **Rider**: Additional document attached to speaker or entertainer contract specifying technical requirements, hospitality needs, etc.

Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025):
- **S.M.A.R.T. Framework**: Goal-setting methodology ensuring objectives are well-defined and measurable
- **ADA Compliance**: Americans with Disabilities Act standards for accessible events (ramps, signage, accommodations)
- **WCAG 2.1**: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines for digital event platforms (virtual/hybrid)
- **ISO 20121**: International standard for sustainable event management (environmental, social, economic)
- **MPI (Meeting Professionals International)**: Global association providing event management certifications and standards
- **PCMA (Professional Convention Management Association)**: Industry association for business events and continuing education
- **CMP (Certified Meeting Professional)**: Professional certification demonstrating event management expertise
- **IAEE (International Association of Exhibitions and Events)**: Standards for trade shows and exhibitions
- **GDPR Compliance**: Data protection for attendee information (EU residents, event registration data)
- **Hybrid Event Best Practices 2024-2025**: Equal attention to in-person and virtual audiences, seamless technology integration, inclusive networking
