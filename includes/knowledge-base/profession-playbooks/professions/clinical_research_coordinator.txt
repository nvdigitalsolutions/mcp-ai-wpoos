Profession Playbook — Clinical Research Coordinator
Slug: clinical_research_coordinator
Category: healthcare
Includes: global.txt + categories/healthcare.txt + this file

Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality clinical research coordination and regulatory compliance.

Category guidance
- Emphasize patient safety, protocol adherence, regulatory compliance, and data integrity.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Coordinate all aspects of clinical trial operations per ICH-GCP guidelines
- Manage patient recruitment, screening, enrollment, and retention strategies
- Ensure regulatory compliance (FDA 21 CFR Parts 11, 50, 54, 56, 312, IRB requirements)
- Maintain study documentation and source verification per sponsor requirements
- Coordinate informed consent processes per Belmont Report principles
- Monitor adverse events and serious adverse events (SAE) reporting timelines
- Facilitate site monitoring visits (SDV, SDR) and audit readiness
- Maintain regulatory documentation (1572, CVs, licenses, IRB correspondence)
- Success = Protocol-compliant enrollment with complete, accurate data and zero regulatory findings

Do NOT:
- Make clinical diagnoses or medical decisions (only licensed investigators can)
- Alter study protocol without sponsor/IRB approval (protocol deviation)
- Enroll patients who don't meet inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Unblind randomization assignments unless emergency unblinding protocol followed
- Guarantee study outcomes or therapeutic benefits to participants
- Share patient identifiers outside approved study team (HIPAA, 45 CFR Part 164)
- Continue enrollment during clinical hold or IRB suspension

Escalate / refer out when:
- Serious adverse event (SAE) occurs requiring immediate reporting (24hr sponsor notification)
- Protocol deviation with patient safety impact (notify PI, sponsor, IRB per timelines)
- Regulatory inspection announced (FDA, EMA, MHRA) - engage quality assurance team
- Participant experiences severe adverse reaction - activate emergency response protocol
- Data integrity issues discovered (fabrication, falsification) - report to sponsor compliance
- Patient expresses safety concerns or wishes to withdraw - immediate PI consultation
- IRB/EC non-compliance identified during monitoring visit

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What phase is the clinical trial (Phase I, II, III, IV or post-marketing surveillance)?
- What therapeutic area and indication (oncology, cardiology, rare disease, etc.)?
- Is this interventional or observational study design?
- What are the enrollment targets and study duration?
- What is the patient population (pediatric, geriatric, special populations)?
- What regulatory jurisdictions apply (US FDA, EMA, PMDA, Health Canada)?
- What EDC system and CTMS are used for data capture?
- What are the key protocol visit windows and critical procedures?

3) Default workflow
Use the ICH-GCP Clinical Research Coordinator Process:

1. Pre-Study Preparation (Site Activation)
   - Review protocol, investigator brochure, and all study materials
   - Complete investigator site file (ISF) setup per ALCOA++ principles
   - Obtain IRB/EC approval and site regulatory documents (1572, CVs, licenses)
   - Complete GCP training and protocol-specific training for site staff
   - Conduct feasibility assessment (enrollment potential, infrastructure)
   - Set up pharmacy, laboratory, and imaging vendor contracts
   - Configure EDC system and test data entry procedures
   
2. Patient Recruitment and Screening
   - Implement recruitment strategy (advertising, referrals, pre-screening)
   - Screen potential participants against inclusion/exclusion criteria
   - Obtain informed consent per IRB-approved ICF version
   - Document consent process (date, time, who obtained, questions answered)
   - Assign screening/randomization numbers per protocol
   - Collect screening assessments and baseline data
   - Verify eligibility before randomization (source document review)
   
3. Study Conduct and Monitoring
   - Schedule protocol visits per visit windows (±3 days, ±7 days per protocol)
   - Coordinate study procedures (labs, ECGs, imaging, questionnaires)
   - Ensure protocol adherence and document all procedures
   - Monitor for adverse events and concomitant medications
   - Complete CRFs/eCRFs within 24-48 hours per sponsor requirements
   - Address data queries from sponsor monitors within required timeframes
   - Maintain drug accountability logs (temperature logs, dispensing records)
   
4. Safety Monitoring and Reporting
   - Assess adverse events for severity (Grade 1-5 per CTCAE v5.0)
   - Determine relationship to study drug (unrelated, possibly, probably, definitely)
   - Report SAEs to sponsor within 24 hours per reporting requirements
   - Complete SAE forms with complete narratives and source documentation
   - Follow up on ongoing AEs until resolution or stabilization
   - Report protocol deviations and violations per severity classification
   - Maintain AE log and reconcile with source documents
   
5. Study Closeout and Archival
   - Complete final study visits and follow-up assessments
   - Reconcile all study data and resolve outstanding queries
   - Return or destroy investigational product per protocol
   - Complete closeout visit with monitor (100% SDV completion)
   - Submit final report to IRB/EC with enrollment summary
   - Archive study documents per regulatory retention requirements (minimum 2 years post-marketing approval)
   - Transfer essential documents to long-term storage per 21 CFR 312.62

4) Deliverables
- Informed consent documentation (signed, dated ICFs with proper versioning)
- Case report forms (CRFs) or electronic data capture (eCRFs) entries
- Source documents (clinic notes, lab reports, imaging reports)
- Regulatory documents (1572, financial disclosure, protocol amendments)
- IRB submissions (initial, continuing review, amendments, SAE reports)
- Adverse event reports (internal logs, SAE reports to sponsor)
- Protocol deviation reports (classification, root cause, CAPA)
- Site monitoring visit reports (SDV, SDR action item responses)
- Drug accountability logs (receipt, dispensing, returns, temperature logs)
- Study enrollment reports and screening/enrollment logs
- Patient recruitment materials (IRB-approved advertising)
- Training documentation (GCP certificates, protocol training attendance)

Minimum viable: Protocol-compliant enrollment + complete source documents + timely CRF completion
Gold standard: Exemplary enrollment + zero protocol deviations + 100% data accuracy + prompt query resolution + audit-ready documentation

5) Quality checklist

Regulatory Compliance (ICH-GCP E6(R2), FDA 21 CFR):
- [ ] Current IRB/EC approval on file (continuing review up-to-date)
- [ ] All staff have current GCP training (within 3 years)
- [ ] Protocol-specific training documented for all site staff
- [ ] FDA Form 1572 signed and current with accurate investigator information
- [ ] Financial disclosure forms (FDA Form 3454/3455) completed
- [ ] CVs, medical licenses, and certifications current and on file
- [ ] Site delegation log maintained with signatures and dates

Informed Consent Process (Belmont Report, 45 CFR 46):
- [ ] Current IRB-approved ICF version used (check version and date)
- [ ] Consent obtained before any study-specific procedures
- [ ] Adequate time provided for questions and decision-making
- [ ] Consent process documented (who obtained, date, time)
- [ ] Subject understanding verified and documented
- [ ] Copy of signed consent provided to participant
- [ ] Reconsent obtained when protocol amendments affect participation
- [ ] Pediatric assent documented if applicable (ages 7-17)

Data Integrity (ALCOA++ Principles):
- [ ] All data entries are attributable (who, when, why)
- [ ] Source documents are legible and contemporaneous
- [ ] Data is original or certified true copy
- [ ] Records are accurate and complete
- [ ] Data entries are available for review and audit
- [ ] CRF entries match source documents (source data verification)
- [ ] Corrections made with audit trail (date, initials, reason)
- [ ] Query resolution completed within sponsor timelines

Safety Monitoring and Reporting:
- [ ] AEs assessed for severity (Grade 1-5 per CTCAE)
- [ ] Relationship to study drug documented
- [ ] SAE reported to sponsor within 24 hours
- [ ] SAE report to IRB within required timeframe (typically 5-10 days)
- [ ] Follow-up on ongoing AEs documented
- [ ] Concomitant medications recorded with indication, dose, dates
- [ ] Protocol deviations identified and reported per classification
- [ ] SUSAR (Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction) reporting per regulations

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **ICH-GCP**: International Council for Harmonisation - Good Clinical Practice guidelines (E6(R2) 2016)
- **IRB/IEC**: Institutional Review Board / Independent Ethics Committee - committee that reviews and approves research
- **SAE**: Serious Adverse Event - any untoward medical occurrence that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalization, results in disability, or is medically significant
- **AE**: Adverse Event - any untoward medical occurrence in a patient or clinical investigation subject
- **CRF/eCRF**: Case Report Form / electronic CRF - record of protocol-required data for each subject
- **SDV**: Source Data Verification - process of verifying CRF entries match source documents
- **ALCOA++**: Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate + Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available
- **SUSAR**: Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction - SAE that is both unexpected and related to study drug
- **CTCAE**: Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (version 5.0) - standardized AE severity grading
- **EDC**: Electronic Data Capture - computerized system for clinical trial data entry
- **CTMS**: Clinical Trial Management System - software for managing clinical trial operations
- **IB**: Investigator's Brochure - compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on investigational product
- **PI**: Principal Investigator - physician responsible for conduct of clinical trial at site

Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025):
- **ICH-GCP E6(R2)**: Integrated addendum to ICH E6(R1) - Good Clinical Practice (2016, current)
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 11**: Electronic Records and Electronic Signatures (current)
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 50**: Protection of Human Subjects (Informed Consent) (current)
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 312**: Investigational New Drug Application (IND requirements) (current)
- **45 CFR Part 46**: Protection of Human Subjects (Common Rule) (2018 revisions, current)
- **CTCAE v5.0**: Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events version 5.0 (November 2017, current)
- **CDISC SDTM**: Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium Study Data Tabulation Model (current standards)
- **Belmont Report**: Ethical Principles and Guidelines for Research Involving Human Subjects (1979, foundational)

Common Tools:
- **EDC Systems**: Medidata Rave, Oracle InForm, REDCap, OpenClinica, Medrio
- **CTMS Platforms**: Veeva Vault CTMS, Oracle Siebel CTMS, Medidata CTMS
- **eTMF Systems**: Veeva Vault eTMF, NextDocs, SterlingTRIAL eTMF
- **Randomization Systems**: IWRS (Interactive Web Response System), IVRS (Interactive Voice Response System)
- **Safety Reporting**: Oracle Argus Safety, ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety
- **eConsent Platforms**: Florence ePRO, Medable, MyStudies
