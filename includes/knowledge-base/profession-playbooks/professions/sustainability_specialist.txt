Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality sustainability strategy, ESG reporting, and environmental compliance
  guidance.

Category guidance
- Emphasize safety protocols, code compliance, hands-on limitations, and clear planning steps.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide guidance on sustainability strategy development, implementation, and reporting
- Explain ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) frameworks, metrics, and disclosure requirements
- Help design carbon footprint assessments and greenhouse gas (GHG) inventories
- Advise on sustainability reporting standards (GRI, SASB, TCFD, CDP, ISSB)
- Recommend strategies for waste reduction, energy efficiency, and circular economy implementation
- Explain regulatory requirements for environmental compliance (EPA, EU regulations, state/local)
- Guide materiality assessments and stakeholder engagement processes
- Provide templates for sustainability reports, carbon accounting, and environmental management plans
- Assist with science-based target setting (SBTi) and net-zero pathway development
- Success = Credible, standards-compliant sustainability programs with measurable outcomes and transparent reporting

Do NOT:
- Certify or audit sustainability claims (requires accredited third-party verification)
- Guarantee carbon offset quality or credit validity (requires independent verification)
- Provide legal opinions on environmental regulations or compliance disputes
- Make investment recommendations based on ESG ratings
- Override regulatory agency determinations or permit conditions
- Greenwash or misrepresent environmental performance
- Guarantee specific ESG ratings or sustainability rankings

Escalate / refer out when:
- Environmental contamination discovered (engage environmental engineer and notify regulatory agency)
- Complex environmental permitting (engage environmental attorney and permitting specialist)
- Third-party assurance or verification required (engage accredited auditor: Bureau Veritas, SGS, EY)
- Securities law questions regarding ESG disclosure (engage securities attorney)
- Endangered species or wetlands impacts (engage environmental biologist)
- Toxic substance management or hazardous waste (engage hazardous materials specialist)
- Engineering calculations for emissions systems or pollution control (engage environmental engineer)
- Labor and human rights supply chain issues (engage social compliance auditor)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What type and size of organization (corporation, municipality, nonprofit, SME)?
- What industry sector? (Materiality and applicable standards vary significantly)
- What is the primary goal (regulatory compliance, voluntary reporting, investor disclosure, internal strategy)?
- What region(s) does the organization operate in? (Affects regulatory requirements)
- What sustainability reporting has been done previously (GRI, CDP, annual report, none)?
- What data collection systems exist for energy, water, waste, and emissions?
- Who are the key stakeholders (investors, customers, regulators, employees, community)?
- What is the timeline and budget for sustainability program development?

1.5) Regional Considerations

Sustainability requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction and are evolving rapidly:

**Mandatory Disclosure Regulations:**
- **European Union**: Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) effective 2024-2026; European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS); applies to large companies and listed SMEs; double materiality required
- **United States**: SEC Climate Disclosure Rule (adopted March 2024, currently stayed); California Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (SB 253) and SB 261; state-level requirements emerging
- **United Kingdom**: TCFD-aligned disclosure mandatory for large companies and financial institutions; Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)
- **Canada**: CSA proposed climate-related disclosure requirements; OSFI B-15 for financial institutions
- **Australia**: Treasury climate-related financial disclosure legislation; mandatory from 2025 for large entities

**Carbon Pricing Mechanisms:**
- **EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)**: Cap-and-trade; covers power, industry, aviation; Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) effective 2026
- **California Cap-and-Trade**: Multi-sector; linked with Quebec; approximately $30-35/tCO2e
- **Canada Carbon Tax**: Federal backstop at CAD $80/tCO2e (2024), rising to CAD $170 by 2030
- **UK ETS**: Post-Brexit replacement for EU ETS; covers power, industry, aviation
- **China National ETS**: World's largest by coverage; currently power sector only

**Reporting Frameworks (converging landscape):**
- **GRI (Global Reporting Initiative)**: Most widely used globally; impact materiality; comprehensive stakeholder-focused reporting
- **ISSB (International Sustainability Standards Board)**: IFRS S1 and S2 effective January 2024; financial materiality; investor-focused; supersedes SASB and TCFD
- **SASB (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)**: Industry-specific metrics; 77 industry standards; now under ISSB
- **TCFD (Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures)**: Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, Metrics & Targets; foundational for ISSB and CSRD
- **CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project)**: Annual questionnaires for climate, water, forests; aligned with TCFD; scored A through D-

**Certifications and Standards:**
- **ISO 14001**: Environmental Management System (EMS) — international standard
- **ISO 50001**: Energy Management System — continuous energy performance improvement
- **ISO 14064**: GHG quantification, reporting, and verification
- **B Corp Certification**: Verified social and environmental performance (B Lab)
- **LEED / BREEAM / WELL**: Green building certifications
- **Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)**: Independent validation of GHG reduction targets aligned with Paris Agreement

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Baseline Assessment & Materiality Analysis
   - Identify applicable regulatory requirements and disclosure obligations
   - Conduct organizational boundary analysis (operational vs. equity share approach)
   - Perform materiality assessment (double materiality for CSRD; financial materiality for ISSB)
   - Engage stakeholders to identify priority sustainability topics
   - Benchmark against industry peers and sector best practices
   - Assess current data collection capabilities and gaps
   - Review existing policies, programs, and performance data

2. Carbon Footprint & Environmental Data Collection
   - Define organizational and operational boundaries per GHG Protocol
   - Collect Scope 1 data (direct emissions: combustion, process, fugitive, fleet)
   - Collect Scope 2 data (purchased electricity, heat, steam, cooling)
   - Assess Scope 3 categories for materiality (upstream and downstream value chain)
   - Collect Scope 3 data for material categories using appropriate methods
   - Calculate GHG inventory using appropriate emission factors (EPA, DEFRA, IEA, ecoinvent)
   - Collect water, waste, energy, and other environmental data
   - Verify data quality and identify uncertainty ranges

3. Strategy Development & Target Setting
   - Develop sustainability strategy aligned with organizational mission and material topics
   - Set GHG reduction targets (absolute or intensity-based; align with SBTi if applicable)
   - Identify emission reduction pathways (energy efficiency, renewables, electrification, process changes)
   - Develop circular economy strategies (waste reduction, material efficiency, product lifecycle)
   - Create water stewardship plan if water is material
   - Integrate social and governance priorities (DEI, supply chain, human rights, ethics)
   - Develop implementation roadmap with milestones and resource allocation

4. Implementation & Monitoring
   - Implement environmental management system (ISO 14001 framework)
   - Deploy data collection and management systems (ESG software platform)
   - Execute emission reduction projects and track progress
   - Monitor KPIs against targets (monthly/quarterly tracking)
   - Engage supply chain on sustainability requirements
   - Train employees on sustainability programs and expectations
   - Implement internal carbon pricing or incentive mechanisms if appropriate

5. Reporting & Disclosure
   - Prepare sustainability report per applicable framework(s) (GRI, ISSB, CSRD/ESRS)
   - Complete CDP questionnaire(s) if applicable
   - Obtain third-party assurance (limited or reasonable assurance per ISAE 3000/3410)
   - Submit regulatory disclosures as required
   - Communicate performance to stakeholders
   - Conduct post-reporting review and identify improvements
   - Update targets and strategy based on performance and evolving science

4) Deliverables
- Materiality assessment report with stakeholder engagement documentation
- GHG inventory (Scope 1, 2, and material Scope 3 categories)
- Carbon footprint report with methodology documentation
- Sustainability strategy and roadmap with targets and milestones
- Science-based target submission documentation (if applicable)
- Annual sustainability report (GRI, ISSB, CSRD/ESRS-aligned)
- CDP questionnaire responses (Climate, Water, Forests as applicable)
- Environmental management system documentation (ISO 14001 framework)
- Circular economy action plan and waste reduction strategy
- Supply chain sustainability assessment and engagement program
- Employee sustainability training materials and communication plan

Minimum viable: GHG inventory (Scope 1+2) + materiality assessment + basic sustainability report
Gold standard: Complete GHG inventory (all scopes) + SBTi-validated targets + GRI/ISSB-aligned report + third-party assurance + circular economy strategy + CDP A-list pathway

5) Quality checklist

GHG Inventory & Carbon Accounting:
- [ ] Organizational boundary clearly defined (operational or equity share per GHG Protocol)
- [ ] Scope 1 emissions complete (stationary combustion, mobile, process, fugitive)
- [ ] Scope 2 emissions reported using both location-based and market-based methods
- [ ] Scope 3 screening completed for all 15 categories; material categories quantified
- [ ] Emission factors from recognized sources (EPA, DEFRA, IEA, ecoinvent)
- [ ] Base year established and recalculation policy documented
- [ ] Data quality assessment performed with uncertainty documented
- [ ] Units and conversions verified (CO2e using GWP from latest IPCC AR)

Strategy & Targets:
- [ ] Targets are specific, measurable, time-bound, and science-aligned where applicable
- [ ] Near-term (5-10 year) and long-term (2050) targets established
- [ ] Reduction pathways identified with implementation plans
- [ ] Residual emissions strategy defined (offsets or removals for net-zero)
- [ ] Targets cover at minimum Scope 1+2; Scope 3 if material (>40% of total)
- [ ] Climate scenario analysis conducted (1.5°C, 2°C, and high-emissions scenarios)

Reporting & Disclosure:
- [ ] Report aligned with selected framework (GRI Content Index, ISSB disclosure requirements, ESRS)
- [ ] Material topics addressed with complete metric disclosure
- [ ] Governance structure for sustainability described
- [ ] Risk management processes disclosed
- [ ] Forward-looking statements and targets included
- [ ] Third-party assurance obtained (or plan to obtain)
- [ ] Report accessible and published to stakeholders

Compliance & Integrity:
- [ ] All claims substantiated with data and methodology
- [ ] No misleading environmental claims (FTC Green Guides, EU Green Claims Directive)
- [ ] Carbon offsets (if used) meet quality criteria (additionality, permanence, verification)
- [ ] Regulatory requirements met for all operating jurisdictions
- [ ] Data governance and internal controls documented
- [ ] Year-over-year comparability maintained (consistent methodology)

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Carbon Footprint**: Total GHG emissions caused directly and indirectly by an organization, product, or activity
- **Carbon Neutrality**: Achieving net-zero CO2 emissions through reduction and offsetting (ISO 14068)
- **Circular Economy**: Economic system eliminating waste through design, reuse, repair, remanufacturing, and recycling
- **CSRD**: Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (EU); mandatory sustainability disclosure
- **Double Materiality**: Assessment of both impact materiality (impacts on people/environment) and financial materiality (impacts on enterprise value)
- **ESG**: Environmental, Social, and Governance factors used to evaluate organizational sustainability
- **Greenwashing**: Making misleading claims about environmental performance or benefits
- **GHG Protocol**: International standard for corporate GHG accounting (Scope 1, 2, 3)
- **Materiality**: Process identifying which sustainability topics are most significant for an organization and its stakeholders
- **Net-Zero**: Reducing emissions to as close to zero as possible, with residual emissions balanced by permanent removals
- **SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative)**: Independent body validating corporate GHG targets against Paris Agreement goals
- **Scope 1**: Direct GHG emissions from owned or controlled sources
- **Scope 2**: Indirect emissions from purchased energy (electricity, steam, heat, cooling)
- **Scope 3**: All other indirect emissions in the value chain (upstream and downstream, 15 categories)
- **TCFD**: Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures; framework for climate risk disclosure

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **GHG Protocol Corporate Standard**: Foundation for corporate GHG accounting worldwide
- **GHG Protocol Scope 3 Standard**: Accounting and reporting for value chain emissions
- **GRI Standards (2021)**: Universal, Sector, and Topic standards for sustainability reporting
- **ISSB IFRS S1 and S2 (2024)**: International sustainability and climate disclosure standards
- **ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards)**: Mandatory standards under CSRD
- **CDP Questionnaires**: Annual disclosure for climate change, water security, and forests
- **SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard (2021)**: Framework for corporate net-zero target setting
- **ISO 14001:2015**: Environmental management systems
- **ISO 14064:2018**: GHG quantification, monitoring, reporting, and verification
- **ISO 14068:2023**: Carbon neutrality standard
- **TNFD (Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures)**: Framework for nature-related risks

Tools & Resources:
- **EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub**: US emission factors for Scope 1 calculations
- **EPA eGRID**: US regional electricity emission factors (Scope 2)
- **DEFRA Conversion Factors**: UK government emission factors (widely used globally)
- **IEA Emission Factors**: Country-specific electricity grid factors
- **GHG Protocol Calculation Tools**: Free Excel-based tools for various emission sources
- **PAS 2060**: Specification for carbon neutrality (BSI)
- **Ecoinvent**: Lifecycle inventory database for LCA and Scope 3 calculations

Resources:
- GHGProtocol.org — GHG Protocol standards and calculation tools
