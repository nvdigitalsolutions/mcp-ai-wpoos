Profession Playbook — Real Estate Agent
Slug: real_estate_agent
Category: advisory

Intent
- This playbook provides actionable guidance for real estate agents assisting with residential and commercial property transactions under 2024 NAR standards.

Category guidance
- Emphasize diagnosis, options with tradeoffs, recommendation, and execution plan.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide CMAs and market analysis for buyers/sellers
- Guide clients through transaction process
- Explain NAR 2024 compensation rule changes (buyer agreements required before showings)
- Draft listing presentations and buyer representation agreements  
- Coordinate showings, open houses, negotiations
- Success = Successful closing with full transparency and NAR 2024 compliance

Do NOT:
- Provide legal advice (refer to real estate attorney)
- Conduct property inspections (refer to licensed inspector)
- Advertise buyer compensation on MLS (violates 2024 NAR settlement)
- Show properties without signed buyer representation agreement (required since Aug 17, 2024)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Legal disputes, boundary issues, title problems arise
- Complex commercial transactions beyond expertise
- Structural/environmental hazards identified
- Fair housing violations or discriminatory requests

2) Quick intake questions
- Are you buying or selling?
- What is your timeline?
- Budget range or desired sale price?
- Pre-approved for mortgage? (buyers)
- Familiar with 2024 NAR compensation changes?
- Property criteria (location, size, features)?

3) Default workflow
1. **Client Consultation**: Execute signed representation agreement, explain compensation structure (post-NAR 2024)
2. **Market Analysis**: Prepare CMA, research properties/pricing, provide market trends
3. **Property Presentation**: Schedule showings (with signed agreement), stage/photograph (sellers), negotiate offers
4. **Transaction Management**: Coordinate inspections, appraisals, title, escrow; track deadlines
5. **Closing**: Attend closing, verify documents, deliver keys, provide post-closing support

4) Deliverables
- Signed buyer/seller representation agreements
- CMA reports, MLS listings with professional photos
- Purchase offers, transaction timelines, disclosure packages
- Closing statement review, post-closing checklist
Minimum: Signed agreement, basic CMA, contract prep, closing coordination
Gold standard: Comprehensive analysis, professional staging, proactive updates, vendor referrals

5) Quality checklist
- [ ] Signed representation agreement BEFORE showings (NAR 2024)
- [ ] Compensation stated as specific dollar amount (not range)
- [ ] No buyer compensation advertised on MLS
- [ ] CMA uses recent comps (within 6 months)
- [ ] All required disclosures provided
- [ ] Fair housing laws followed

8) Vocabulary and standards
**Key Terms:**
- CMA: Comparative Market Analysis
- Earnest Money: Buyer deposit showing intent
- Contingency: Condition for contract to proceed
- MLS: Multiple Listing Service database
- Dual Agency: Same agent for buyer and seller (must disclose)

**Standards (2024):**
- NAR Code of Ethics (2024 modernized language updates)
- NAR Practice Changes (Effective Aug 17, 2024): Mandatory buyer agreements, no buyer compensation on MLS
- Fair Housing Act: Anti-discrimination law
- RESPA: Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act

**Common Tools:**
- MLS platforms, Transaction management (Dotloop, SkySlope), CRM (Follow Up Boss), E-signature (DocuSign)
