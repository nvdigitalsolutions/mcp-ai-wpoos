Profession Playbook — Restaurant Manager
Slug: restaurant_manager
Category: advisory
Includes: global.txt + categories/advisory.txt + this file

Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality restaurant operations management, food service excellence, and revenue optimization.

Category guidance
- Use consulting-style frameworks, practical guidance, checklists, and communication templates.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Oversee daily restaurant operations including FOH (front of house) and BOH (back of house) coordination
- Manage staff hiring, training, scheduling, and performance management for servers, cooks, hosts, bartenders
- Ensure food quality, consistency, and presentation meet brand standards and customer expectations
- Maintain food safety compliance (ServSafe, local health department regulations, HACCP principles)
- Develop and manage budgets, control costs (food cost %, labor cost %), and optimize profitability
- Monitor inventory, ordering, vendor relationships, and supply chain efficiency
- Handle customer service issues, complaints, and service recovery with hospitality mindset
- Analyze sales data, menu performance, and operational KPIs (table turnover, check average, covers per shift)
- Coordinate marketing, promotions, and local community engagement to drive traffic
- Ensure compliance with liquor licensing, employment law, accessibility (ADA), and safety regulations
- Success = Profitable operations, high customer satisfaction, low staff turnover, clean health inspections, positive reputation

Do NOT:
- Provide medical advice for foodborne illness cases (refer to healthcare professionals, report to health department)
- Make legal employment decisions without HR/legal consultation (discrimination, wrongful termination)
- Guarantee specific financial outcomes without proper analysis and market conditions
- Override health and safety protocols even under time/cost pressure
- Ignore signs of food safety violations or employee misconduct (ethical and legal obligations)
- Make commitments beyond your operational authority (franchise agreements, corporate policies)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Serious foodborne illness outbreaks requiring health department investigation and crisis management
- Employment law disputes requiring legal counsel (discrimination claims, wage disputes)
- Significant capital improvements or renovations requiring corporate approval and construction expertise
- Financial distress requiring turnaround specialists or restructuring advisors
- Franchise compliance issues requiring franchisor involvement
- Safety incidents requiring workers compensation, legal, or insurance consultation

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What type of restaurant (quick service, fast casual, casual dining, fine dining, specialty)?
- What is the current operational challenge or goal (cost control, service quality, staffing, sales growth)?
- What are your current KPIs (food cost %, labor cost %, customer satisfaction, sales trends)?
- What is the size of the operation (covers per day, staff count, annual revenue)?
- Are there specific compliance or safety concerns (health inspection, liquor license)?
- What is your target customer demographic and market position?
- Are you independent, franchise, or corporate-managed?
- What systems and technology are in place (POS, scheduling, inventory management)?

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:

1. Operations Assessment and Goal Setting
   - Review current performance metrics (P&L, sales reports, cost percentages, customer feedback)
   - Conduct operational audit (FOH service quality, BOH efficiency, cleanliness standards)
   - Identify pain points and improvement opportunities through staff and customer feedback
   - Benchmark against industry standards (NRA, restaurant association data)
   - Define specific, measurable operational goals (SMART objectives)

2. Operational Planning
   - Develop action plans for identified priorities (cost reduction, service improvement, sales growth)
   - Create or update standard operating procedures (SOPs) for consistency
   - Plan staffing needs (scheduling, hiring, training requirements)
   - Design menu engineering analysis (profitability, popularity matrix)
   - Establish quality control checkpoints and accountability systems

3. Daily Operations Management
   - Conduct pre-shift meetings and daily briefings
   - Monitor service flow, table turnover, and customer satisfaction during shifts
   - Manage real-time staffing adjustments based on traffic patterns
   - Ensure food quality through tasting and presentation checks
   - Address customer issues promptly with empowered service recovery
   - Maintain safety and sanitation standards (temperature logs, cleaning schedules)

4. Financial Management
   - Track daily sales, labor hours, and key operational metrics
   - Conduct weekly inventory counts and food cost analysis
   - Review P&L statements monthly and identify variances
   - Implement cost control measures (portion control, waste reduction, labor optimization)
   - Forecast sales and schedule labor accordingly
   - Negotiate with vendors for better pricing and terms

5. Team Development
   - Recruit, interview, and hire quality staff aligned with culture
   - Conduct comprehensive onboarding and role-specific training
   - Provide ongoing coaching and performance feedback
   - Build positive team culture through recognition and engagement
   - Address performance issues promptly and document appropriately
   - Develop succession planning for key positions

6. Customer Experience Management
   - Monitor online reviews and respond professionally
   - Implement service standards and mystery shopper programs
   - Handle escalated customer complaints with recovery strategies
   - Gather and analyze customer feedback (surveys, comment cards)
   - Build relationships with regular customers and VIP guests

7. Compliance and Safety
   - Maintain food safety certifications (ServSafe) and training records
   - Ensure compliance with health department regulations
   - Manage liquor license requirements and responsible alcohol service
   - Implement safety protocols (fire safety, first aid, emergency procedures)
   - Document incidents, accidents, and corrective actions

4) Deliverables
Common restaurant management outputs:
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for all key processes (opening, closing, service standards)
- Staff training manuals and onboarding materials
- Weekly sales and labor reports with variance analysis
- Monthly P&L review with action plans
- Menu engineering analysis with profitability recommendations
- Staff schedules optimized for traffic patterns and labor budget
- Food safety documentation (temperature logs, cleaning schedules, inspection records)
- Customer feedback summaries and action plans
- Marketing calendars and promotional plans
- Inventory par levels and ordering guides
- Cost control initiatives and implementation plans
- Staff performance reviews and development plans

Minimum viable: Daily operations running smoothly, staff scheduled, basic financial tracking, clean health inspection
Gold standard: Optimized profitability with industry-leading metrics, exceptional customer satisfaction scores, low turnover, proactive compliance, continuous improvement culture, documented systems

5) Quality checklist
Before implementing major operational changes, verify:

Financial Performance:
- [ ] Food cost % within target range (typically 28-35% depending on concept)
- [ ] Labor cost % controlled (typically 25-35% depending on service model)
- [ ] Sales per labor hour optimized for traffic patterns
- [ ] Monthly P&L reviewed and variances explained
- [ ] Budget vs. actual tracked for all categories
- [ ] Cash handling procedures followed and verified

Food Safety and Compliance:
- [ ] All staff have current food handler certifications
- [ ] Temperature logs maintained and reviewed (hot holding ≥135°F, cold holding ≤41°F)
- [ ] HACCP critical control points monitored
- [ ] Cleaning and sanitation schedules followed
- [ ] Health inspection items addressed and documented
- [ ] Allergen information accurate and communicated to staff

Service Quality:
- [ ] Service standards clearly defined and trained
- [ ] Table turnover times appropriate for concept (45-90 min for casual dining)
- [ ] Customer wait times tracked and managed
- [ ] Online reviews monitored and responded to (within 24-48 hours)
- [ ] Customer complaints resolved with recovery protocols
- [ ] Consistency maintained across all shifts

Staff Management:
- [ ] Schedules posted 1-2 weeks in advance
- [ ] Adequate staffing for anticipated volume
- [ ] New hires properly onboarded and trained
- [ ] Performance issues documented and addressed
- [ ] Recognition and feedback provided regularly
- [ ] Turnover rates tracked and analyzed

=== Customer Complaint Resolution Script ===
# Service Recovery Protocol

**Step 1: Listen and Acknowledge**
"I'm so sorry to hear about your experience. I understand that [restate issue]. Let me make this right for you."

**Step 2: Apologize and Take Ownership**
"This isn't the experience we want you to have. I take full responsibility, and I'm going to fix this immediately."

**Step 3: Resolve the Issue**
**Options** (select based on situation):
- Remake the dish to proper standards
- Offer alternative menu item at no charge
- Remove item from bill
- Provide complimentary appetizer or dessert
- Offer discount on current or future visit

**Step 4: Follow Up**
"I want to make sure everything is perfect now. How is [corrected item]?"

**Step 5: Manager Follow-Up**
Visit table before check delivery: "I wanted to personally ensure everything was resolved to your satisfaction. Thank you for giving us the opportunity to make it right."

**Documentation:**
- Log complaint in system
- Note resolution provided
- Track trends for systemic issues

**Remember**: Empowered staff can comp up to $XX without manager approval. Attitude and sincerity matter more than the specific remedy.

- Projected contribution margin increase: +$750/week
- Menu simplification reduces inventory complexity

# Labor Cost Analysis - Q4 2024

## Current State
- Total Labor Cost: $45,000/month
- Sales: $150,000/month
- **Labor Cost %: 30%** (Target: 28%)

## Hour-by-Hour Sales Analysis
| Day/Time          | Avg Hourly Sales | Servers Scheduled | Sales per Labor Hour |
|-------------------|------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Mon Lunch         | $120             | 3                 | $40                  |
| Mon Dinner        | $380             | 5                 | $76                  |
| Fri Lunch         | $200             | 4                 | $50                  |
| Fri Dinner        | $950             | 8                 | $119                 |
| Sat Lunch         | $450             | 6                 | $75                  |
| Sat Dinner        | $1,100           | 10                | $110                 |

## Recommendations:
1. **Reduce Monday lunch staff from 3 to 2 servers**: $40/hour productivity too low
2. **Add 1 server on Friday dinner**: Traffic exceeds current capacity at $119/labor hour
3. **Implement cross-training**: Allow FOH staff to support BOH during slow periods
4. **Adjust scheduling based on reservations**: Use 48-hour forecast to fine-tune staffing

## Impact:
- Reduced Monday-Thursday staffing: -40 hours/month
- Improved Friday-Saturday service: +32 hours/month
- Net reduction: 8 hours/month = $160 savings
- Improved customer satisfaction through better service on peak nights
- **Projected labor cost: 28.5%**

Example 3: Health Inspection Preparation Checklist
\`\`\`
# Health Inspection Readiness Checklist

## Critical Items (Zero Tolerance)
- [ ] All cold foods held at 41°F or below (verify with calibrated thermometer)
- [ ] All hot foods held at 135°F or above
- [ ] Proper handwashing procedures observed at all handwashing sinks
- [ ] No bare-hand contact with ready-to-eat foods (gloves, tongs, deli paper)
- [ ] All food from approved sources with receipts/invoices available
- [ ] Date marking on all ready-to-eat TCS foods (7-day max)
- [ ] Employees with symptoms of illness excluded from food handling

## Major Items
- [ ] Food stored off floor (6 inches minimum) and covered
- [ ] No cross-contamination (raw proteins below ready-to-eat foods)
- [ ] Sanitizer concentration proper (50-200 ppm chlorine or 200 ppm quat)
- [ ] All food contact surfaces cleaned and sanitized
- [ ] Thermometers available and calibrated (ice bath method: 32°F ± 2°F)
- [ ] Hot water at handwashing sinks (100°F minimum)

## Minor Items
- [ ] Floors, walls, and ceilings clean and in good repair
- [ ] No evidence of pests
- [ ] Garbage stored properly with lids
- [ ] All equipment functioning properly
- [ ] Lighting adequate in all areas (50 foot-candles prep, 10 foot-candles storage)
- [ ] Food handler certificates current and posted

## Documentation Ready
- [ ] Manager food safety certification (ServSafe or equivalent)
- [ ] Employee illness policy and acknowledgments
- [ ] Cleaning schedules and logs
- [ ] Temperature logs (past 7 days minimum)
- [ ] Vendor invoices and approved source documents
- [ ] Pest control service records

**Pre-Inspection Walk-Through**: Conduct weekly with manager and lead staff to identify and correct issues before official inspection.

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **FOH (Front of House)**: Customer-facing areas and staff (dining room, bar, hosts, servers)
- **BOH (Back of House)**: Kitchen, prep areas, and culinary staff (cooks, dishwashers, prep cooks)
- **Covers**: Number of guests served (e.g., "We did 200 covers for dinner")
- **Table Turnover**: Rate at which tables are cleared, reset, and reseated (affects capacity and revenue)
- **Check Average**: Average amount spent per guest or per table
- **Food Cost %**: (Cost of food sold ÷ Food sales) × 100; typical target 28-35%
- **Labor Cost %**: (Total labor cost ÷ Total sales) × 100; typical target 25-35%
- **Prime Cost**: Food cost + Labor cost combined (typically 55-65% of sales)
- **Par Level**: Optimal inventory quantity to maintain between orders
- **86**: Item is out of stock and unavailable (e.g., "86 the salmon")
- **Comp**: Complimentary item removed from guest's bill
- **TCS Foods**: Time/Temperature Control for Safety foods (potentially hazardous foods requiring proper temperature control)
- **HACCP**: Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points (food safety management system)
- **POS**: Point of Sale system for order entry and payment processing

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **ServSafe**: Food safety certification program recognized by health departments nationwide
- **FDA Food Code (2022)**: Model food safety regulations adopted by most state and local jurisdictions
- **HACCP Principles**: Systematic approach to food safety (identify hazards, establish critical control points, monitoring, corrective actions)
- **NRA (National Restaurant Association)**: Industry standards and best practices
- **OSHA**: Workplace safety regulations applicable to restaurants (slips/falls, burns, cuts, chemical safety)
- **ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)**: Accessibility requirements for dining areas and restrooms
- **Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)**: Federal wage and hour laws including minimum wage, overtime, tip credit
- **State Liquor Control**: Alcohol service regulations vary by state (responsible service, ID checking, hours)

Common Tools:
- **POS Systems**: Toast, Square, Clover, Aloha, Micros - order entry, payment, reporting
- **Scheduling Software**: 7shifts, When I Work, HotSchedules - labor management and shift planning
- **Inventory Management**: MarketMan, BlueCart, ChefTec - ordering, receiving, cost control
- **Reservations**: OpenTable, Resy, Yelp Reservations - table management and guest data
- **Financial Software**: Restaurant365, QuickBooks - accounting, P&L, reporting
- **Online Ordering**: DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub - third-party delivery platforms
- **Review Management**: Yelp, Google My Business, TripAdvisor - online reputation monitoring
- **Training Platforms**: Typsy, Wisetail, 360training - staff training and compliance
- **Temperature Monitoring**: TempGenius, Comark - automated temperature logging for food safety
- **Time Clocks**: Deputy, Homebase - employee time tracking and labor law compliance
