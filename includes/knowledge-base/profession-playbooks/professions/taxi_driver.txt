Profession Playbook — Taxi Driver
Slug: taxi_driver
Category: transportation
Includes: global.txt + categories/other.txt + this file

Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook for taxi and rideshare drivers, containing actionable instructions,
  SOPs, checklists, and templates for safe passenger transport, professional customer service, and licensing compliance.

Category guidance
- Emphasize passenger safety, professional conduct, customer service excellence, and local regulatory compliance.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Transport passengers safely to requested destinations via the most efficient route
- Maintain valid taxi/rideshare license, driver's license, and vehicle inspections per local jurisdiction
- Conduct pre-trip vehicle safety checks (brakes, tires, lights, cleanliness)
- Operate taximeter or app accurately, provide clear fare estimates and receipts
- Maintain professional demeanor (courteous, respectful, helpful)
- Ensure passenger comfort and security (clean vehicle, safe driving, privacy)
- Handle payment transactions securely (cash, card, contactless, app-based)
- Assist passengers with accessibility needs (wheelchair-accessible vehicles, service animals)
- Report incidents and safety concerns to dispatch or platform immediately
- Success = Safe, courteous, efficient passenger transport with positive customer reviews and regulatory compliance

Do NOT:
- Operate without valid taxi license, commercial driver's license, or proper permits
- Refuse service based on discrimination (race, religion, disability, destination - illegal under equality laws)
- Drive under influence of alcohol or drugs (zero tolerance)
- Exceed safe speeds or engage in reckless driving behaviors
- Overcharge passengers or manipulate fares/meters
- Share passenger information or violate privacy (addresses, phone numbers, travel patterns)
- Engage in inappropriate conversation or behavior with passengers
- Operate vehicle with safety defects (brake issues, tire problems, lighting failures)
- Transport unaccompanied minors without proper authorization and protocols

Escalate / refer out when:
- Passenger exhibits threatening or violent behavior (contact police, ensure personal safety first)
- Medical emergency occurs (call 911, provide first aid if trained, document incident)
- Accident with injuries or significant property damage (secure scene, call police, exchange information)
- Vehicle mechanical breakdown requires towing or roadside assistance
- Passenger refuses to pay or disputes fare (document details, report to company, do not escalate conflict)
- Suspected human trafficking or illegal activity (discreetly contact law enforcement hotline)
- Lost property valuable or perishable (secure item, report immediately to dispatch/platform)
- Accessibility accommodation beyond vehicle capability (wheelchair van needed, specialized medical transport)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What type of taxi service (traditional cab, rideshare app, private hire, airport shuttle)?
- What licensing jurisdiction applies (city/county taxi permit, state commercial license requirements)?
- What vehicle type and accessibility features (sedan, van, wheelchair-accessible)?
- What payment methods are accepted (cash, card, app-based, account billing)?
- What are the fare calculation methods (meter, flat rate, zone-based, app algorithm)?
- What are the safety and security protocols (GPS tracking, emergency button, camera)?
- What customer service standards apply (greeting protocol, route preferences, tipping expectations)?
- What incident reporting procedures exist (accidents, passenger complaints, safety concerns)?

3) Default workflow
Use industry best practices and local taxi licensing requirements:

1. Pre-Shift Preparation
   - Verify valid taxi/rideshare license and driver's license (check expiration dates)
   - Review daily vehicle assignment and ensure proper insurance coverage
   - Conduct pre-trip vehicle inspection (walk-around check)
     * Tires (inflation, tread depth, damage)
     * Lights (headlights, brake lights, turn signals, hazards)
     * Brakes (test pedal feel and response)
     * Interior cleanliness (seats, floor mats, air freshener)
     * Safety equipment (first aid kit, fire extinguisher if required, protective screen)
   - Set up payment systems (card reader, app login, change for cash transactions)
   - Review GPS/navigation system functionality
   - Stock supplies (hand sanitizer, tissues, water for extreme weather, phone charger)

2. Passenger Pickup & Greeting
   - Verify passenger identity (name confirmation for pre-booked rides)
   - Greet courteously ("Good morning, are you [Name]? Heading to [destination]?")
   - Assist with luggage loading (offer help, handle carefully)
   - Confirm destination and preferred route ("Would you like to take the highway or surface streets?")
   - Provide fare estimate if requested ("The trip should be approximately $[X] based on current traffic")
   - Ensure passenger safely seated before departing (wait for door to close)
   - Adjust climate control and ask if passenger prefers music/silence

3. Safe Driving & Navigation
   - Follow GPS or use local knowledge for most efficient route
   - Obey all traffic laws (speed limits, stop signs, traffic signals)
   - Practice defensive driving (maintain following distance, anticipate hazards)
   - Avoid hard braking and sharp turns for passenger comfort
   - Provide smooth acceleration and deceleration
   - Communicate route changes ("There's construction ahead, I'll take an alternate route")
   - Monitor traffic conditions and adjust route in real-time
   - Respect passenger preferences (conversation level, music, temperature)

4. Customer Service During Trip
   - Be friendly but respectful of personal space and privacy
   - Avoid controversial topics (politics, religion) unless passenger initiates
   - Provide local information if requested (landmarks, restaurants, directions)
   - Handle phone calls briefly and professionally (hands-free only)
   - Maintain clean and comfortable environment (no strong odors, trash removed)
   - Respect passenger's need for quiet (reading, working, resting)
   - Accommodate reasonable requests (route preferences, quick stops, multiple drop-offs)

5. Drop-Off & Conclusion
   - Announce arrival ("We've arrived at [destination]")
   - Clearly state fare and payment options ("Your fare is $[X], I accept cash or card")
   - Process payment accurately, provide receipt immediately
   - Assist with luggage unloading
   - Thank passenger and wish them well ("Thank you, have a great day!")
   - Ensure passenger has all belongings before departing
   - Check vehicle for lost items immediately after drop-off
   - Log trip details (for record-keeping and lost property tracking)

6. Post-Shift & Maintenance
   - Clean vehicle interior (remove trash, vacuum if needed, wipe surfaces)
   - Check for lost property and report immediately
   - Refuel vehicle if required
   - Report any mechanical issues or damage to dispatch/management
   - Submit trip logs, receipts, and payment reconciliation
   - Review customer feedback and ratings (address any concerns)

4) Deliverables
- Trip logs with pickup/drop-off locations, times, and fares
- Payment receipts (printed or digital) for each trip
- Vehicle inspection reports (pre-shift and post-shift)
- Incident reports (accidents, passenger issues, safety concerns)
- Lost property documentation with photos and descriptions
- Customer service feedback responses (addressing complaints or commendations)
- License renewal and insurance documentation
- Fuel and maintenance expense records

Minimum viable: Safe, courteous passenger transport with accurate fares, clean vehicle, no complaints
Gold standard: Consistent 5-star ratings, passenger commendations, exemplary safety record, local route expertise

5) Quality checklist

Safety & Compliance:
- [ ] Valid taxi/rideshare license and driver's license current
- [ ] Vehicle inspection passed (brakes, tires, lights functional)
- [ ] Insurance coverage verified and current
- [ ] No driving under influence (zero alcohol/drugs)
- [ ] All traffic laws followed (speed limits, signals, right-of-way)
- [ ] GPS tracking and emergency button functional (if equipped)

Customer Service Excellence:
- [ ] Courteous greeting and destination confirmation
- [ ] Professional appearance and demeanor
- [ ] Clean, comfortable vehicle interior
- [ ] Smooth, safe driving (no harsh braking/acceleration)
- [ ] Respectful of passenger preferences (conversation, music, temperature)
- [ ] Accurate fare calculation and clear receipt provided

Passenger Safety & Security:
- [ ] Passenger identity verified for pre-booked rides
- [ ] No discrimination in service provision
- [ ] Privacy maintained (no sharing of passenger information)
- [ ] Lost property handled promptly and securely
- [ ] Incidents reported immediately to proper authorities
- [ ] Accessibility needs accommodated (service animals, mobility devices)

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Taxi License**: Municipal or county permit required to operate taxi/cab service legally
- **Private Hire**: Pre-booked passenger transport (not street hail) requiring separate licensing
- **Rideshare/TNC**: Transportation Network Company (Uber, Lyft) with app-based booking and payment
- **Taximeter**: Device measuring distance/time to calculate fares (required for metered taxis)
- **Fare Estimate**: Pre-trip price quote based on distance, time, and current traffic conditions
- **Protective Screen**: Safety barrier between driver and passenger compartment
- **Accessibility Requirements**: Legal obligations under ADA/Equality Act (wheelchair access, service animals)
- **Street Hail**: Passenger flagging down taxi on street (vs. pre-booked dispatch/app)
- **Zone-Based Pricing**: Flat fares for specific geographic zones (common at airports)
- **No-Show Fee**: Charge applied when passenger fails to appear for pre-booked ride
- **Surge Pricing**: Higher fares during peak demand periods (rideshare apps)
- **GPS Tracking**: Real-time vehicle location monitoring for safety and dispatch efficiency

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **Local Taxi Licensing Ordinances**: Municipal/county regulations for taxi permits, vehicle standards
- **ADA Title III (Americans with Disabilities Act)**: Accessibility requirements for passenger services
- **Equality Act 2010 (UK equivalent)**: Non-discrimination requirements for taxi/PHV services
- **DOT Taxi Accessibility**: Federal guidance on accessible taxi service provision
- **GAO Ridesharing Safety Standards (2024)**: Background check and safety requirements for TNCs
- **PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)**: Card payment security requirements
- **State/Local Traffic Codes**: Speed limits, traffic laws, commercial vehicle regulations
- **Insurance Requirements**: Commercial auto liability coverage (varies by jurisdiction)
- **Background Check Standards**: Criminal history and driving record requirements for licensing
- **Vehicle Inspection Standards**: Annual or periodic safety inspections (brakes, tires, emissions)

Common Tools:
- Navigation/GPS: Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps, dedicated taxi GPS systems
- Payment Processing: Square, Stripe, PayPal, integrated taxi card readers
- Rideshare Apps: Uber Driver, Lyft Driver, local taxi dispatch apps
- Dash Cameras: Vantrue, BlackVue, Garmin (for security and incident documentation)
- Taxi Meters: Centrodyne, Digitax, Pulsar (traditional meter systems)
- Communication: Two-way radio, mobile data terminal, smartphone
- Safety Equipment: Protective screens (Pro-gard), emergency buttons, first aid kits
