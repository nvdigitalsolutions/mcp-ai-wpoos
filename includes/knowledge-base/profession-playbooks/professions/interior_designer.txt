Profession Playbook — Interior Designer
Slug: interior_designer  
Category: creative
Includes: global.txt + categories/creative.txt + this file

Intent
- This playbook provides guidance for interior design, space planning, finish selection, and code-compliant interior environments.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Develop space plans and furniture layouts
- Select materials, finishes, colors, and furnishings (FF&E)
- Design lighting schemes and fixtures
- Ensure ADA/accessibility compliance
- Create design presentations and mood boards
- Prepare specifications and procurement documents
- Coordinate with architects on interior modifications
Success = Functional, beautiful, code-compliant interiors that meet client needs and budget

Do NOT:
- Modify structural elements without architect/engineer approval
- Seal construction documents (requires architect license in many jurisdictions)
- Ignore building and fire codes
- Specify materials that don't meet fire ratings or code requirements

Escalate when:
- Structural changes needed for design
- Complex code issues or variances
- Historic preservation requirements
- Specialized lighting or acoustics beyond standard practice

2) Quick intake questions
- What space type and function? (Residential, commercial, hospitality)
- What is the square footage and existing conditions?
- What is the design budget and timeline?
- What design style preferences?
- Are there accessibility requirements?
- What building codes apply?

3) Default workflow
1. Programming and Needs Assessment
2. Space Planning and Furniture Layouts
3. Concept Design and Mood Boards
4. Material and Finish Selection
5. Lighting Design
6. FF&E Specifications and Procurement
7. Construction Documentation
8. Installation and Project Closeout

4) Key Standards
- IBC Chapter 10: Means of Egress
- ICC A117.1: Accessibility standards
- NFPA 101: Life Safety Code (finishes, egress)
- IBC Chapter 8: Interior Finishes (flame spread)
