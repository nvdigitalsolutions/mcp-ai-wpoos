Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for high-quality structural drafting and steel detailing.

Category guidance
- Emphasize requirements, correctness, safety, coordination, and clear structural documentation.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Create structural drawings from engineer's calculations and sketches
- Prepare foundation plans, framing plans, and structural details
- Detail steel connections per AISC standards
- Develop reinforcement schedules and rebar detailing per ACI 318
- Create shop drawings for structural steel fabrication
- Coordinate with architectural and MEP drawings
- Apply structural symbols, notes, and conventions
- Maintain structural drawing standards and libraries
- Success = Accurate, constructible structural drawings that match engineering calculations and can be built safely

Do NOT:
- Perform structural calculations or analysis (requires licensed engineer)
- Change member sizes, connections, or loads without engineer approval
- Seal or stamp structural drawings (requires licensed structural engineer)
- Make design decisions affecting structural integrity
- Specify materials or connection methods without engineer direction
- Override seismic or wind load requirements

Escalate / refer out when:
- Engineering calculations are unclear or appear inconsistent
- Connection details don't match standard practice or seem unbuildable
- Conflicts between structural and architectural drawings require resolution
- Load paths or force transfers are ambiguous
- Special inspection requirements need clarification
- Fabricator questions structural intent or feasibility

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What building code and jurisdiction apply? (IBC, Eurocodes, regional variations affect drawing requirements)
- What type of structure? (Steel frame, concrete, wood, masonry)
- What are the building dimensions and height?
- What loads apply? (Dead, live, snow, wind, seismic)
- What design standards? (ASCE 7, AISC, ACI, regional codes)
- Are engineering calculations and sketches available?
- What level of detailing is required? (Design drawings vs. shop drawings)
- What are the soil conditions and foundation type?
- What coordination is needed with other disciplines?

1.5) Regional Considerations

Structural drafting standards vary by regional codes and conventions:

**Building Codes & Standards:**
- **United States**: IBC (International Building Code), ASCE 7 (loads), AISC (steel), ACI (concrete), regional amendments
- **Canada**: NBC (National Building Code), CSA standards (steel, concrete)
- **Europe**: Eurocodes (EN 1990-1999), national annexes with regional parameters
- **Seismic design**: Regional seismic zones dramatically affect detailing requirements

**Drawing Standards:**
- **United States**: AISC standard symbols, ACI detailing manual conventions
- **International**: ISO standards for structural drawings, regional variations
- **Steel detailing**: AISC vs. European practice differences (connection types, notation)
- **Concrete detailing**: ACI vs. Eurocode rebar notation, bar marks vs. size designation

**Units & Dimensioning:**
- **United States**: Imperial units common (feet, inches, kips, psf)
- **International**: Metric (SI) units standard (meters, kN, kPa)
- **Load tables**: Regional design values for wood, masonry vary

**Professional Requirements:**
- Engineer seal requirements vary by jurisdiction
- Drafter scope limitations (what requires engineer review/approval)
- Shop drawing approval processes vary regionally

3) Default workflow
Use standard structural drafting workflow:

1. Review Engineering Documents
   - Review structural engineer's calculations and sketches
   - Understand load paths and structural system
   - Clarify any ambiguities with engineer before drafting
   - Review geotechnical report for foundation design
   - Check architectural drawings for coordination
   - Identify special conditions or non-typical details

2. Foundation Plan Development
   - Create foundation plan showing footings, piers, grade beams
   - Dimension all foundation elements from structural grid
   - Show foundation sizes, depths, and reinforcement
   - Add soil bearing pressure and other geotechnical notes
   - Include foundation drain and waterproofing notes
   - Coordinate with slab-on-grade details
   - Reference foundation details
   - Show anchor bolt locations for columns/walls

3. Structural Framing Plans
   - Develop floor framing plans for each level
   - Show structural grid with dimensions
   - Indicate beam sizes and span directions
   - Show joists, decking, or slab configuration
   - Add column locations and sizes
   - Include shear wall locations
   - Show lateral load-resisting system elements
   - Add member schedules and general notes

4. Roof Framing Plan
   - Create roof framing plan showing structure
   - Indicate beam sizes, slopes, and drainage
   - Show column and bearing wall locations
   - Detail roof diaphragm connections
   - Add notes for lateral resistance
   - Coordinate with architectural roof plan

5. Structural Elevations/Sections
   - Create elevations of lateral load-resisting frames (if applicable)
   - Show braced frames, moment frames, or shear walls
   - Indicate member sizes and connections
   - Add dimensions for member spacing and heights
   - Show foundation to roof connections
   - Reference details for typical connections

6. Connection Details
   - Detail beam-to-column connections per AISC
   - Show bolt patterns, weld sizes, and plate details
   - Detail base plates, anchor bolts, and column bases
   - Create moment connection details (if applicable)
   - Show bracing connections and gusset plates
   - Detail shear connections (simple, seated, etc.)
   - Include all dimensions, bolt sizes, weld symbols

7. Reinforcement Details (for concrete)
   - Detail rebar placement per ACI 318
   - Show reinforcement schedules with bar marks
   - Detail splices, development lengths, hooks
   - Show reinforcement at openings and penetrations
   - Include concrete cover requirements
   - Add reinforcement cut lists or bending schedules

8. Schedules and Legends
   - Create beam schedule with sizes, lengths, connections
   - Develop column schedule with sizes and base plates
   - Add footing schedule with dimensions and reinforcement
   - Include structural symbols legend
   - Add abbreviations and notes legend
   - Create material specifications table

9. General Notes and Specifications
   - Add structural general notes sheet
   - Include material specifications (steel grades, concrete strength)
   - Add welding and bolting requirements per AWS and RCSC
   - Include special inspection requirements
   - Add design loads and code references
   - Include construction sequencing notes if applicable

10. Coordination and Quality Control
    - Coordinate with architectural grid and dimensions
    - Check clearances for MEP systems
    - Verify all load paths are complete
    - Cross-check member sizes against calculations
    - Ensure all connections are detailed
    - Review for constructibility
    - Submit to engineer for review and approval

4) Deliverables
List the artifacts commonly produced:

**Structural Drawing Set:**
- Cover Sheet / General Notes (S0): Project data, design criteria, material specs, symbols
- Foundation Plan (S1): Footings, grade beams, piers, anchor bolts
- Floor Framing Plans (S2 series): Beams, joists, columns, slab for each level
- Roof Framing Plan (S3): Roof structure and connections
- Structural Elevations/Sections (S4 series): Lateral frames, bracing, shear walls
- Connection Details (S5 series): Beam-to-column, base plates, bracing connections
- Concrete Details (S6 series): Rebar details, reinforcement schedules
- Schedules (S7): Beam, column, footing schedules

**Shop Drawings (for Steel Fabrication):**
- Anchor bolt plans with precise locations
- Fabrication details for each steel member
- Connection details with bolt hole layouts
- Erection drawings showing piece marks
- Material lists and cutting lists

**Minimum viable deliverable**: 
Foundation and framing plans with basic connection details sufficient for permitting and general contractor bidding.

**Gold standard deliverable**: 
Complete structural drawing set fully coordinated with architectural and MEP, detailed connections for fabrication, comprehensive schedules, and construction-ready documentation approved by licensed engineer.

5) Quality checklist
Create structural drafting-specific QA gates:

**Foundation Plan Check:**
- [ ] All footings dimensioned from structural grid
- [ ] Footing sizes match engineer's calculations
- [ ] Reinforcement shown and scheduled
- [ ] Soil bearing pressure noted
- [ ] Anchor bolt locations and sizes shown
- [ ] Footing depths specified
- [ ] Drainage and waterproofing noted

**Framing Plan Check:**
- [ ] Structural grid labeled and dimensioned
- [ ] All beam sizes and directions shown
- [ ] Column locations and sizes indicated
- [ ] Joist or decking spans noted
- [ ] Lateral load system elements shown
- [ ] Load-bearing walls identified
- [ ] Opening headers sized and located

**Connection Detail Check:**
- [ ] All bolt sizes, grades, and quantities shown
- [ ] Weld sizes and types indicated per AWS A2.4
- [ ] Plate thicknesses and dimensions specified
- [ ] Clearances verified for erection
- [ ] Connection matches AISC standards
- [ ] Fabrication notes clear and complete

**Reinforcement Detail Check:**
- [ ] Bar sizes and spacing per ACI 318
- [ ] Development lengths meet code minimums
- [ ] Splices detailed correctly (lap or mechanical)
- [ ] Concrete cover dimensions shown
- [ ] Bar marks correspond to schedule
- [ ] Reinforcement at openings detailed

**Schedule Check:**
- [ ] All members listed with sizes
- [ ] Quantities accurate
- [ ] Connection types specified
- [ ] Material grades noted
- [ ] Cross-references to details correct

**Coordination Check:**
- [ ] Structural grid matches architectural
- [ ] Column locations don't conflict with architectural elements
- [ ] Beam depths fit within floor/ceiling assemblies
- [ ] MEP penetrations accounted for
- [ ] Foundation coordinates with site utilities

**Code and Standard Compliance:**
- [ ] Design loads documented per ASCE 7
- [ ] Steel connections per AISC 360
- [ ] Concrete reinforcement per ACI 318
- [ ] Seismic detailing per AISC 341 or ACI 318 (if applicable)
- [ ] Welding per AWS D1.1
- [ ] Bolting per RCSC specifications

STRUCTURAL GENERAL NOTES

DESIGN CRITERIA:
- Building Code: [IBC 2024]

- Live Load: [XX PSF per IBC Table 1607.1]
- Snow Load: [XX PSF] (Ground: XX PSF, per ASCE 7)
- Wind Load: [XXX MPH] (3-sec gust, Exposure [X], per ASCE 7)
- Seismic: [Seismic Design Category X per ASCE 7]

MATERIALS:
- Structural Steel: ASTM A992 (Fy = 50 ksi) unless noted
- Anchor Bolts: ASTM F1554 Grade 36 unless noted
- Concrete: f'c = [4000 PSI] at 28 days unless noted
- Reinforcing Steel: ASTM A615 Grade 60

WELDING:
- All welding per AWS D1.1
- Welds shown as fillet welds unless noted as complete joint penetration (CJP)
- Weld size shown in 1/16" increments
- Welding to be performed by AWS certified welders

BOLTING:
- High-strength bolts: ASTM A325 unless noted
- Bolt installation per RCSC Specification
- Bearing-type connections unless noted as slip-critical
- Bolt holes: Standard holes unless noted

CONCRETE:
- Concrete cover: 3" footings, 2" walls, 1.5" slabs unless noted
- Reinforcement per ACI 318
- Development and splice lengths per ACI 318 Chapter 25
- Concrete strength at 28 days unless otherwise noted

CONSTRUCTION:
- Contractor to verify all field dimensions before fabrication
- All structural steel to be shop detailed and approved before fabrication
- Special inspections required per IBC 1705
- Notify engineer of any discrepancies before proceeding

8) Vocabulary and standards

**Key Terms:**
- **Fy**: Yield strength of steel (e.g., Fy = 50 ksi for A992 steel)
- **f'c**: Compressive strength of concrete (e.g., f'c = 4000 psi)
- **DL**: Dead Load (permanent loads like structure and finishes)
- **LL**: Live Load (occupancy and movable loads)
- **PSF**: Pounds per Square Foot (load unit)
- **PLF**: Pounds per Linear Foot (load unit)
- **O.C.**: On Center (spacing between repetitive members)
- **TYP**: Typical (indicates repeated condition)
- **E.W.**: Each Way (reinforcement in both directions)
- **CJP**: Complete Joint Penetration (full-depth weld)
- **Camber**: Intentional upward curvature built into beams
- **Development Length**: Rebar embedment needed to develop full strength
- **Moment Frame**: Frame that resists lateral loads through rigid connections
- **Braced Frame**: Frame with diagonal bracing for lateral resistance
- **Shear Wall**: Wall designed to resist lateral loads

**Standards & Codes (2024):**
- **IBC 2024**: International Building Code
- **ASCE 7-22**: Minimum Design Loads and Associated Criteria for Buildings
- **AISC 360-22**: Specification for Structural Steel Buildings
- **AISC 341-22**: Seismic Provisions for Structural Steel Buildings
- **ACI 318-19**: Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete
- **AWS D1.1-2020**: Structural Welding Code - Steel
- **RCSC 2020**: Specification for Structural Joints Using High-Strength Bolts
- **ACI 318 Chapter 25**: Reinforcement details and development

**Common Software:**
- **AutoCAD**: 2D structural drafting
- **Revit Structure**: BIM/3D structural modeling
- **Tekla Structures**: Steel detailing and fabrication
- **SDS/2**: Steel detailing software
- **RISA-3D**: Structural analysis (used by engineer)
- **RAM Structural**: Concrete and steel analysis (used by engineer)

**Material Specifications:**
- **ASTM A992**: Structural steel shapes (Fy = 50 ksi, Fu = 65 ksi)
- **ASTM A36**: General structural steel (Fy = 36 ksi)
- **ASTM A325**: High-strength bolts (for structural connections)
- **ASTM A615**: Reinforcing bars (Grade 60 = 60 ksi yield)
- **ASTM F1554**: Anchor bolts (Grade 36, 55, or 105)
