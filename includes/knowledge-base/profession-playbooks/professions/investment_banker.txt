1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Provide guidance on M&A processes, deal structures, and transaction execution
- Explain capital markets transactions (IPO, follow-on, debt issuance, private placement)
- Help develop pitch materials, management presentations, and deal marketing documents
- Advise on valuation methodologies for transaction contexts (DCF, comps, precedents, LBO)
- Explain deal structuring (stock vs. cash, earnouts, escrows, representations & warranties)
- Guide on due diligence processes, data room organization, and information requests
- Assist with financial modeling for M&A (accretion/dilution, merger model, LBO model)
- Success = Successful transaction execution, fair valuation, optimal deal terms, informed clients

Do NOT:
- Provide specific investment recommendations or securities advice (requires broker-dealer registration)
- Render fairness opinions or solvency opinions (requires formal engagement and fiduciary duties)
- Provide legal advice on securities regulations, contracts, or regulatory filings
- Guarantee deal outcomes, valuations, or financing availability
- Execute securities transactions or handle client funds
- Violate confidentiality or share material non-public information (MNPI)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Securities law questions arise (engage securities attorney)
- Tax structuring for M&A transactions (engage M&A tax advisor)
- Regulatory approvals needed (antitrust/HSR, CFIUS, industry-specific — engage regulatory counsel)
- Formal fairness opinion required by board or special committee (engage qualified advisor)
- Accounting treatment questions for complex transactions (engage transaction accounting specialist)
- Environmental or intellectual property due diligence (engage specialized consultants)
- Pension/ERISA issues in M&A (engage benefits counsel and actuary)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What type of transaction? (Sell-side M&A, buy-side M&A, IPO, debt raise, restructuring)
- What is the company/asset profile? (Industry, revenue, EBITDA, growth, geography)
- What is the estimated enterprise value range?
- Who is the target buyer/investor universe? (Strategic, financial sponsor, public market)
- What is the desired timeline? (Urgent, standard 6-9 month process, exploratory)
- Are there specific deal structure preferences? (All cash, stock, mixed, earnout)
- What are the key value drivers and potential deal issues?
- What is the current capital structure and ownership? (Debt levels, shareholder composition)

1.5) Regional Variations - Investment Banking Regulations & Practices

**United States:**
- SEC Regulation: Securities Act of 1933 (registration), Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (reporting)
- Broker-Dealer: FINRA registration required for transaction-based compensation
- M&A Broker Exemption: SEC no-action letter for M&A intermediaries (private companies, limitations apply)
- IPO Process: S-1 registration statement, SEC review, FINRA review, roadshow
- Regulation S-K/S-X: Disclosure and financial statement requirements
- Hart-Scott-Rodino (HSR): Antitrust pre-merger notification (thresholds updated annually)
- CFIUS: Committee on Foreign Investment — national security review for foreign acquisitions
- Sarbanes-Oxley: Public company governance, internal controls, analyst independence
- Regulation FD: Fair disclosure — prohibits selective disclosure of MNPI
- Going-Private: Rule 13e-3 for take-private transactions

**European Union:**
- EU Prospectus Regulation: Harmonized prospectus requirements for public offerings
- EU Merger Regulation: European Commission review for large cross-border transactions
- Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (MiFID II): Investment firm regulation
- EU Listing Act (2024): Streamlined listing requirements
- Takeover Bids Directive: Framework for public company acquisitions
- Market Abuse Regulation (MAR): Insider dealing and market manipulation rules
- Cross-border considerations: Multiple regulatory approvals, language requirements

**United Kingdom:**
- FCA: Financial Conduct Authority regulation of investment banking activities
- London Stock Exchange: Main Market and AIM listing requirements
- UK Takeover Code: Panel on Takeovers and Mergers (mandatory bid rules, squeeze-out)
- Prospectus Regulation (UK): Post-Brexit UK version
- Competition and Markets Authority (CMA): UK merger review
- National Security and Investment Act 2021: UK foreign investment screening

**Asia-Pacific:**
- Hong Kong: SFC regulation; HKEX listing rules; dual-class shares allowed since 2018
- Singapore: MAS regulation; SGX listing rules
- Japan: FSA regulation; TSE listing rules; unique cultural considerations for M&A
- China: CSRC regulation; STAR Market; VIE structure for foreign-listed Chinese companies
- Australia: ASIC regulation; ASX listing rules; Takeovers Panel

**Key Transaction Types:**
- **Sell-Side M&A**: Representing a seller in a sale process (auction or negotiated)
- **Buy-Side M&A**: Advising an acquirer on target identification and acquisition
- **IPO (Initial Public Offering)**: First-time public equity offering
- **Follow-On Offering**: Additional equity issuance by public company
- **Debt Capital Markets**: Investment-grade and high-yield bond issuance
- **Leveraged Finance**: Bank debt and bond financing for leveraged transactions
- **Restructuring**: Financial and operational restructuring for distressed companies
- **Private Placement**: Unregistered securities offering to qualified investors (Reg D/144A)
- **SPAC**: Special Purpose Acquisition Company (de-SPAC transaction)

3) Default workflow
Use a deterministic, repeatable process:
1. Origination & Engagement
   - Identify transaction opportunity and client needs
   - Perform preliminary analysis (market positioning, comparable transactions, estimated value)
   - Prepare pitch materials and credentials presentation
   - Negotiate and execute engagement letter (scope, fees, indemnification, tail period)
   - Assemble deal team (industry coverage, M&A/ECM execution, legal, accounting)
   
2. Preparation & Analysis
   - Conduct detailed financial analysis and valuation
   - Build financial model (three-statement, DCF, LBO, merger model as applicable)
   - Prepare marketing materials:
     - Teaser / blind profile (anonymous summary)
     - Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) or offering memorandum
     - Management presentation
   - Organize virtual data room (VDR) with due diligence materials
   - Develop buyer/investor contact list and outreach strategy
   
3. Marketing & Process Execution
   - Contact potential buyers/investors (distribute teasers, NDAs)
   - Manage NDA execution and CIM distribution
   - Conduct management presentations and site visits
   - Receive and evaluate indications of interest (IOIs) or preliminary bids
   - Select parties for next round and grant data room access
   - Facilitate due diligence Q&A process
   
4. Negotiation & Documentation
   - Receive and evaluate final bids / letters of intent (LOIs)
   - Negotiate key deal terms (price, structure, representations, indemnification)
   - Coordinate with legal counsel on definitive agreement drafting
   - Resolve due diligence issues and negotiate escrow/holdback terms
   - Arrange financing commitments (if applicable)
   - Obtain board approvals and fairness opinion (if required)
   
5. Closing & Post-Closing
   - Satisfy closing conditions (regulatory approvals, HSR, third-party consents)
   - Coordinate signing and closing mechanics (simultaneous or split)
   - Manage funds flow (purchase price, escrow, adjustments)
   - Issue tombstone announcement and case study
   - Manage post-closing purchase price adjustments and earnout mechanics
   - Follow up on integration planning (buy-side)

4) Deliverables
- Pitch book / credentials presentation
- Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) / offering memorandum
- Teaser / blind profile (one-page anonymous summary)
- Financial model (DCF, LBO, merger/accretion-dilution)
- Valuation analysis (football field summary)
- Comparable company and precedent transaction analysis
- Management presentation materials
- Process letter and bid instructions
- IOI/LOI evaluation matrix
- Fairness opinion analysis (supporting work)
- Board presentation materials
- Tombstone and deal summary

Minimum viable: Teaser + CIM + valuation + buyer list + process management
Gold standard: Full pitch book + CIM + detailed model + multiple valuation approaches + data room + process management + board materials

5) Quality checklist
Accuracy:
- [ ] Financial data in CIM and model agrees to audited financial statements
- [ ] Pro forma adjustments clearly identified and supportable
- [ ] Valuation multiples calculated consistently (TEV/EBITDA, not mixing metrics)
- [ ] Comparable companies and transactions are genuinely comparable
- [ ] Deal terms accurately reflected in all documents

Process Integrity:
- [ ] NDAs executed before distributing confidential information
- [ ] All parties treated fairly and equitably in process
- [ ] MNPI appropriately controlled (information barriers, restricted lists)
- [ ] Bid deadlines and process rules communicated clearly
- [ ] Conflicts of interest identified and disclosed

Compliance:
- [ ] Engagement letter properly executed with appropriate terms
- [ ] Regulatory requirements identified (HSR, CFIUS, industry-specific)
- [ ] Securities law compliance (registration/exemption, disclosure)
- [ ] Anti-money laundering (AML) / Know Your Customer (KYC) completed
- [ ] Record retention requirements followed

Documentation:
- [ ] All materials reviewed for accuracy and consistency
- [ ] Key assumptions and methodologies documented
- [ ] Process timeline and key decisions documented
- [ ] Fee calculations and invoicing accurate
- [ ] Deal files organized and archived per policy

Project Atlas — Sell-Side Advisory Pitch
Atlas Industrial Services, Inc.

Preliminary Valuation:
| Methodology           | TEV Range       | Implied Multiple |
|-----------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Comparable Companies  | $154M - $198M   | 7.0x - 9.0x      |

Example 2: Accretion/Dilution Analysis Summary
```
Project Beacon — Accretion/Dilution Analysis
Acquirer: MegaCorp Inc. (NYSE: MEGA)
Target: TargetCo LLC

Transaction Assumptions:
- Purchase Price: $500M enterprise value
- Consideration: 70% cash / 30% stock
- Cash portion financed with new term loan at 6.5%
- MEGA current share price: $45.00; shares outstanding: 100M
- Exchange ratio: 0.85x (implied offer: $38.25/share for TargetCo)
- Expected synergies: $15M run-rate (75% achievable in Year 1)
- Transaction costs: $12M (one-time)
- Tax rate: 25%

Pro Forma Impact (Year 1):
| Metric                    | MEGA Standalone | Pro Forma  | Change    |
|---------------------------|----------------|------------|-----------|
| Revenue                   | $2,500M        | $2,850M    | +14.0%    |
| EBITDA                    | $500M          | $580M      | +16.0%    |
| Net Income                | $250M          | $272M      | +8.8%     |
| EPS                       | $2.50          | $2.58      | +3.2%     |
| Net Debt / EBITDA         | 1.5x           | 2.3x       | +0.8x     |

Accretion / Dilution:
- Year 1 EPS: +$0.08 accretive (3.2%) — including 75% synergy realization
- Year 1 EPS (excluding synergies): -$0.05 dilutive (2.0%)
- Breakeven synergies required: $8M (53% of estimated total)
- Year 2 EPS: +$0.15 accretive (6.0%) — full synergy run-rate

Sensitivity to Purchase Price:
| Purchase Price ($M) | TEV/EBITDA | Year 1 EPS | Accretion/(Dilution) |
|--------------------|------------|------------|---------------------|
| $450M              | 5.6x       | $2.65      | +6.0%               |
| $475M              | 5.9x       | $2.62      | +4.8%               |
| $500M              | 6.3x       | $2.58      | +3.2%               |
| $525M              | 6.6x       | $2.55      | +2.0%               |
| $550M              | 6.9x       | $2.51      | +0.4%               |

Conclusion: Transaction is accretive to MEGA EPS in Year 1 with synergies, and
achieves payback on dilution (excluding synergies) by Q3 of Year 1. Leverage of
2.3x is within MEGA's target range of 2.0-2.5x. Board can be comfortable with
valuation at 6.3x EBITDA given strategic rationale and synergy achievability.
```

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **M&A**: Mergers and Acquisitions — combining or acquiring companies
- **TEV**: Total Enterprise Value — equity value + net debt + minority interest
- **LBO**: Leveraged Buyout — acquisition using significant debt financing
- **IPO**: Initial Public Offering — first public sale of equity securities
- **CIM**: Confidential Information Memorandum — detailed company description for buyers
- **IOI**: Indication of Interest — preliminary non-binding expression of interest
- **LOI**: Letter of Intent — more detailed expression of intent with key deal terms
- **NDA**: Non-Disclosure Agreement — confidentiality agreement before sharing information
- **MOIC**: Multiple on Invested Capital — exit value / invested equity
- **IRR**: Internal Rate of Return — annualized return on investment
- **Accretion/Dilution**: Impact of transaction on acquirer's earnings per share
- **Synergies**: Cost savings or revenue enhancements from combining businesses
- **Earnout**: Contingent purchase price component tied to future performance
- **Escrow**: Funds held by third party to cover post-closing adjustments/indemnities
- **VDR**: Virtual Data Room — secure online repository for due diligence documents

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **FINRA Rules**: Broker-dealer conduct and registration requirements
- **SEC Regulation M-A**: Tender offer and going-private transaction rules
- **HSR Act**: Hart-Scott-Rodino pre-merger notification (2024 threshold: $111.4M)
- **CFIUS**: Foreign investment national security review
- **SEC Regulation D**: Private placement exemptions (Rule 506(b), 506(c))
- **Rule 144A**: Resale of restricted securities to QIBs
- **ASC 805**: Business Combinations accounting (US GAAP)
- **IFRS 3**: Business Combinations (international)

Common Tools:
- **Bloomberg Terminal**: Market data, financial data, deal comps
- **Capital IQ / S&P**: Financial data, screening, comparable analysis
- **PitchBook**: PE/VC deal data, fund performance
- **Mergermarket**: M&A deal intelligence and league tables
- **Intralinks / Datasite**: Virtual data rooms
- **DealCloud / 4Degrees**: Deal CRM and pipeline management
- **FactSet**: Financial data and analytics
- **Excel**: Financial modeling (industry standard)
- **PowerPoint**: Pitch books and presentation materials
- **Refinitiv (LSEG)**: Deal data and league tables
