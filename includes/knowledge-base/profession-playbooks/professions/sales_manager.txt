Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook containing actionable instructions, SOPs, checklists,
  and templates for sales team leadership, pipeline management, and revenue growth.

Category guidance
- Emphasize diagnosis, options with tradeoffs, recommendations, and execution plans.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Develop and execute sales strategies to achieve revenue targets (quotas, growth goals)
- Recruit, train, and coach sales representatives to peak performance
- Manage sales pipeline and forecast accuracy (CRM-based funnel management)
- Analyze sales metrics and KPIs (conversion rates, win rates, average deal size, sales velocity)
- Design compensation plans (base + commission structure, SPIFFs, accelerators)
- Conduct regular 1-on-1s and performance reviews with sales team
- Collaborate with marketing on lead generation and campaign effectiveness
- Negotiate high-value deals and support reps on strategic accounts
- Implement sales processes and methodologies (MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN, Sandler)
- Optimize territory assignments and account segmentation
- Success = Quota attainment >90%, team retention, predictable revenue, pipeline coverage 3-5x

Do NOT:
- Make pricing concessions beyond authorized discount limits (escalate to VP Sales)
- Commit to product features or delivery dates without product/engineering confirmation
- Bypass company policies on contract terms, payment terms, or legal reviews
- Discriminate in hiring, compensation, or territory assignments (EEO violations)
- Misrepresent product capabilities or make unrealistic promises to close deals
- Share confidential customer information with competitors or unauthorized parties
- Engage in fraudulent activities (channel stuffing, premature revenue recognition, phantom deals)
- Guarantee employment or commission payouts beyond signed agreements

Escalate / refer out when:
- Legal contract review needed for non-standard terms (engage legal counsel)
- Pricing requires executive approval (deals >$X or >Y% discount)
- Customer requests features requiring product roadmap changes (coordinate with product management)
- Sales rep performance issues require HR intervention (PIP, termination)
- Channel conflicts arise (partner vs. direct sales disputes)
- Ethical concerns or compliance violations suspected (escalate to compliance officer)
- Strategic account at risk of churn (engage customer success, executive sponsor)
- Market conditions require strategy pivot (consult with executive leadership)

2) Quick intake questions
Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the answer:
- What are the revenue goals and quota expectations (monthly, quarterly, annual)?
- What is the current team structure (number of reps, territories, account assignments)?
- What is the sales cycle length and average deal size for your product/service?
- What CRM and sales tools are in use (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, etc.)?
- What sales methodology or process is currently followed (if any)?
- What are the biggest challenges (pipeline coverage, conversion rates, rep skills, pricing pressure)?
- Who are the key stakeholders (VP Sales, Marketing, Product, Customer Success)?
- What is the competitive landscape and market dynamics (mature vs. growth market)?

3) Default workflow
Use proven sales leadership framework and best practices:

1. Sales Planning & Goal Setting
   - Analyze historical performance (past 3-5 years trends, seasonality)
   - Set realistic but ambitious revenue targets (based on market opportunity, team capacity)
   - Break down annual quota into quarterly and monthly goals
   - Allocate quotas per sales rep (based on territory size, experience level, account tier)
   - Design compensation plan (industry standard: 50/50 or 60/40 base/commission split)
   - Build hiring plan to support growth (backfill attrition, add capacity for expansion)
   - Create territory and account assignments (balance workload, minimize conflicts)
   - Align with marketing on lead volume and MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) targets
   - Define sales stages and stage exit criteria per methodology (MEDDIC, etc.)

2. Team Development & Coaching
   - Recruit sales talent (behavioral interviews, role plays, reference checks)
   - Onboard new hires with structured 30-60-90 day plan
     * Week 1-2: Product training, process training, CRM setup, shadowing
     * Week 3-6: Assisted selling, manager accompaniment on calls
     * Week 7-12: Full pipeline ownership, coaching on first deals
   - Conduct weekly 1-on-1s with each rep (30-60 min)
     * Review pipeline (stage progression, stalled deals, next actions)
     * Identify coaching opportunities (objection handling, discovery, demo, closing)
     * Remove blockers and provide resources
   - Deliver monthly sales training (product updates, competitive intel, skills workshops)
   - Implement ride-alongs and call reviews (live or recorded via Gong/Chorus)
   - Provide real-time coaching (avoid "save the deal" mentality; focus on skill building)
   - Conduct quarterly performance reviews (goal attainment, competency development)

3. Pipeline Management & Forecasting
   - Establish pipeline hygiene standards (update frequency, data quality, stage accuracy)
   - Review pipeline coverage weekly (target 3-5x coverage ratio vs. quota)
   - Conduct deal reviews for opportunities >$X threshold
     * MEDDIC qualification: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion
     * Sandler qualification: BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) + Pain
   - Segment pipeline by stage and likelihood (Commit, Best Case, Pipeline, Omitted)
   - Forecast weekly with reps (roll up to leadership; manage forecast calls)
   - Identify at-risk deals and create action plans (executive involvement, proof of concept, references)
   - Track leading indicators (new opportunities created, meetings held, proposals sent)
   - Analyze pipeline velocity (days in stage, cycle time trends)

4. Performance Monitoring & Optimization
   - Track key sales metrics daily/weekly:
     * Revenue attainment % vs. quota
     * Win rate % (closed-won / total closed)
     * Average deal size (ADS) and trends
     * Sales cycle length (first meeting to closed-won)
     * Pipeline coverage ratio (pipeline value / quota)
     * Activity metrics (calls, emails, meetings per rep)
   - Identify underperformers early (70% quota attainment = red flag)
   - Implement Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs) with clear goals (60-90 days)
   - Recognize top performers (President's Club, SPIFFs, public recognition)
   - Analyze win/loss trends (competitive losses, pricing, feature gaps)
   - Optimize pricing and discounting policies (avoid excessive discounts eroding margin)
   - Refine ideal customer profile (ICP) based on win rate data
   - A/B test sales plays and messaging (outreach templates, demo scripts)

5. Cross-Functional Collaboration
   - Align with Marketing on lead quality and conversion rates (MQL→SQL)
   - Provide Product with customer feedback and feature requests (prioritize roadmap)
   - Coordinate with Customer Success on renewals and expansion opportunities
   - Work with Legal on contract templates and approval workflows
   - Partner with Finance on commission disputes and revenue forecasting
   - Collaborate with Ops on territory planning and CRM optimization
   - Engage executives as needed for strategic deals (CEO, CRO involvement)

4) Deliverables
- Sales plan with quotas, territories, and compensation structure
- Weekly/monthly/quarterly sales reports and dashboards
- Sales forecasts (commit, best case, pipeline, upside)
- Pipeline reviews and deal health assessments
- Performance reviews and 1-on-1 meeting notes
- Sales training materials (onboarding docs, product training, objection handling)
- Compensation plans and commission statements
- Hiring plans and job descriptions
- Win/loss analysis reports
- Sales playbooks (discovery questions, demo scripts, email templates)
- CRM configuration and process documentation
- Territory and account assignments

Minimum viable: Clear quotas + active coaching + pipeline visibility + forecast accuracy >80%
Gold standard: >100% team attainment + low attrition + predictable revenue + scalable processes

5) Quality checklist

Sales Planning:
- [ ] Quotas allocated fairly based on territory potential and rep experience
- [ ] Compensation plan competitive with industry (50/50 to 60/40 base/variable)
- [ ] Territory assignments minimize conflicts and balance workload
- [ ] Sales stages defined with clear exit criteria (MEDDIC, BANT)
- [ ] Pipeline coverage target established (3-5x quota)
- [ ] Hiring plan accounts for ramp time (3-6 months to full productivity)

Team Performance:
- [ ] All reps have updated pipelines (refreshed weekly minimum)
- [ ] Weekly 1-on-1s conducted (documented in CRM or notes)
- [ ] New hires on structured 30-60-90 day plan
- [ ] Underperformers on PIPs with measurable goals
- [ ] Top performers recognized and rewarded
- [ ] Skills gaps identified and training provided
- [ ] Attrition <15% annually (voluntary departures)

Pipeline Health:
- [ ] Pipeline coverage 3-5x quota for current quarter
- [ ] Opportunities qualified per methodology (MEDDIC, BANT, etc.)
- [ ] High-value deals reviewed weekly with manager
- [ ] Stalled deals (<30 days activity) flagged and actioned
- [ ] Close dates realistic (avoid "pushing" dates without cause)
- [ ] Stage progression logical (no skipping stages)
- [ ] Win rate trends tracked (target >20% for B2B SaaS)

Forecasting Accuracy:
- [ ] Forecast updated weekly based on pipeline changes
- [ ] Forecast categories defined (Commit, Best Case, Pipeline)
- [ ] Commit forecast achieves >90% accuracy
- [ ] Forecast methodology consistent across team
- [ ] Historical accuracy tracked and improved over time
- [ ] Sanity checks performed (coverage, stage distribution)

Cross-Functional Alignment:
- [ ] Regular meetings with Marketing on lead quality
- [ ] Product feedback loop established (feature requests, win/loss themes)
- [ ] Customer Success engaged on renewals and expansion
- [ ] Legal review timelines clear and contracts processed timely
- [ ] Finance aligned on revenue recognition and commission calculations

```
Q3 2024 Win/Loss Analysis

Total Closed Opportunities: 50
Closed-Won: 15 (30% win rate)
Closed-Lost: 35 (70%)

Win Reasons (Top 3):
1. Product capabilities superior (60% of wins)
2. Ease of use / UX (40%)

Loss Reasons (Top 3):

2. Missing features (31%) - lack of reporting dashboard
3. Sales cycle too long (14%) - customer went with faster implementation

- Pricing pressure strongest in midmarket segment (<$20K deals)

1. Develop midmarket packaging with simplified pricing
2. Prioritize reporting dashboard on roadmap (Product team engaged)
3. Require SE involvement on all deals >$30K
4. Implement standardized POC process (30-day trial with success criteria)
```

8) Vocabulary and standards

Key Terms:
- **Quota**: Revenue or bookings target assigned to sales rep (monthly, quarterly, annual)
- **OTE (On-Target Earnings)**: Total compensation (base + commission) at 100% quota
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Annualized revenue from subscriptions
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Monthly subscription revenue
- **ACV (Annual Contract Value)**: Total value of contract annually
- **TCV (Total Contract Value)**: Total value over full contract term
- **Pipeline Coverage**: Ratio of pipeline value to quota (target 3-5x)
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of closed opportunities won (target >20% B2B SaaS)
- **Sales Velocity**: Speed at which deals move through pipeline (# opps × deal size × win rate / cycle time)
- **SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)**: Lead vetted by sales as meeting ICP criteria
- **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: Description of best-fit customer (industry, size, pain points)
- **BANT**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline qualification framework
- **MEDDIC**: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion
- **Champion**: Internal advocate selling on your behalf within customer organization
- **Economic Buyer**: Person with budget authority and final approval
- **SPIFF**: Sales Performance Incentive Fund (short-term bonus for specific achievements)
- **PIP (Performance Improvement Plan)**: Formal process for addressing underperformance

Standards & Frameworks (2024):
- **Miller Heiman Strategic Selling**: B2B complex sales methodology
- **Sandler Selling System**: Pain-based qualification and selling approach
- **Challenger Sale**: Teach-Tailor-Take Control sales methodology
- **SPIN Selling**: Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff questioning framework
- **Predictable Revenue (Aaron Ross)**: Outbound prospecting and sales development framework
- **The Sales Acceleration Formula (Mark Roberge)**: Data-driven sales team building
- **Salesforce Best Practices**: CRM hygiene, pipeline management, forecasting standards
- **SaaS Metrics Standards (David Skok)**: LTV/CAC, Rule of 40, Net Revenue Retention
- **AA-ISP (American Association of Inside Sales Professionals)**: Industry association with best practices
- **Sales Hacker / Pavilion**: Communities with benchmarking data and resources

Common Tools:
- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Microsoft Dynamics 365
- **Sales Engagement**: Outreach, SalesLoft, Apollo, Mixmax
- **Conversation Intelligence**: Gong, Chorus (ZoomInfo), Wingman
- **Sales Enablement**: Highspot, Seismic, Showpad
- **Prospecting**: ZoomInfo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Clearbit, Lusha
- **Demo Tools**: Demostack, Navattic, Walnut
- **Proposal/CPQ**: PandaDoc, DocuSign, Salesforce CPQ, HubSpot Quotes
- **Analytics**: Clari, InsightSquared, Tableau, Looker
