Intent
- Playbook for IGCSE Geography tutoring (Cambridge 0460), covering physical and human geography,
  case studies, and geographical skills.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Tutor IGCSE Geography (0460) for all themes
- Teach case studies and geographical concepts
- Develop map skills, graph interpretation, and fieldwork understanding
- Build exam technique for short answer and structured questions
- Success = Student develops geographical knowledge, skills, and achieves target grade

2) Quick intake questions
- Year, tier, current/target grade?
- Which themes studied (Population, Settlement, Environment, Economic)?
- Case studies covered?
- Challenging areas (physical geography, human geography, skills)?
- Mock results, exam date?

3) Key Topics (Cambridge 0460)
**Theme 1: Population and Settlement**
- Population distribution, density, growth
- Migration (types, causes, effects)
- Settlement (types, hierarchy, functions)
- Urbanization (causes, challenges, solutions)

**Theme 2: Natural Environment**
- Earthquakes and volcanoes (causes, effects, responses)
- Rivers (processes, landforms, flooding, management)
- Coasts (processes, landforms, management)
- Weather and climate (types, factors, climate change)

**Theme 3: Economic Development**
- Development indicators and variations
- Industry (types, location factors, changes)
- Tourism (growth, impacts, sustainability)
- Energy (sources, sustainability)
- Agriculture (types, factors, changes)

**Geographical Skills:**
- Map skills (scale, distance, direction, symbols, contours, grid references)
- Graph interpretation (line, bar, pie, scatter, choropleth)
- Photograph analysis
- Statistical skills

4) Quality checklist
- [ ] Case studies learned with specific details (names, dates, statistics, places)
- [ ] Geographical terminology used correctly
- [ ] Answers structured with PEEL paragraphs
- [ ] Map and graph skills practiced regularly
- [ ] Causes and effects explained clearly
- [ ] Sustainable management discussed
- [ ] Past papers practiced under timed conditions

5) Exam Technique
- Use case study details (specific names, places, data)
- Structure answers: Point, Evidence, Explain, Link
- Use geographical terminology throughout
- For "Explain" questions: Give reasons/causes
- For "Describe" questions: State what you see/observe
- Draw diagrams when helpful (river cross-section, settlement patterns)
