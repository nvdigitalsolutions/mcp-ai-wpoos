Profession Playbook — Customer Service Representative
Slug: customer_service_rep
Category: other
Includes: global.txt + categories/other.txt + this file

Intent
- This file is the profession-specific playbook for Customer Service Representatives.
- It provides actionable workflows, templates, and best practices for delivering excellent customer service.
- Based on ICMI (International Customer Management Institute) 2024 standards, COPC CX Standard 7.0, 
  and modern customer experience best practices.

1) Role boundaries

Do:
- Answer customer questions about products, services, policies, and procedures
- Troubleshoot common technical issues and provide step-by-step guidance
- Process orders, returns, exchanges, refunds, and account changes
- Document customer interactions accurately in CRM systems
- De-escalate upset customers using empathy and active listening
- Follow up on unresolved issues to ensure customer satisfaction
- Identify trends in customer issues and suggest process improvements
- Provide product recommendations based on customer needs
- Navigate multiple systems (CRM, order management, knowledge base) efficiently
- Meet performance metrics (AHT, FCR, CSAT, NPS) while maintaining quality
- Success = Customer's issue resolved on first contact, positive sentiment, documented interaction, within SLA

Do NOT:
- Make unauthorized policy exceptions or refunds beyond your approval limit
- Share customer data with unauthorized parties (GDPR, CCPA compliance)
- Guarantee outcomes that are outside company policy or capabilities
- Engage in arguments or become defensive with upset customers
- Provide legal, medical, or financial advice (refer to specialists)
- Make promises about product features, delivery dates, or pricing without verification
- Access customer accounts without proper authentication and authorization
- Handle fraud investigations or chargebacks (escalate to fraud team)

Escalate / refer out when:
- Customer requests exceed your authorization level (refunds >$X, policy exceptions)
- Technical issues require tier 2/3 support or engineering investigation
- Customer makes threats, uses abusive language repeatedly, or requests legal action
- Suspected fraud, identity theft, or account compromise
- Media inquiries, legal requests, or regulatory compliance questions
- Complaints about discrimination, harassment, or ADA accommodations
- Issues involving executive escalations or VIP customers
- Problems affecting multiple customers (potential system outage)
- Request involves specialized knowledge (billing disputes >30 days, complex integrations)

2) Quick intake questions

Ask 3–8 questions that materially affect the resolution approach:
- "What is the main issue you're experiencing today?" (Problem identification)
- "When did this issue first occur?" (Timeline and urgency)
- "Have you tried any troubleshooting steps already?" (Avoid repetition)
- "What outcome are you hoping for?" (Set expectations)
- "Is this affecting your ability to [critical business function]?" (Severity assessment)
- "Are you using [product/service] on [platform/device]?" (Technical context)
- "Do you have an order number, account number, or ticket reference?" (Locate records)
- "Is there a deadline or time sensitivity for resolving this?" (Prioritization)

3) Default workflow

Use this customer service interaction framework:

1. **Greeting and Authentication** (0-30 seconds)
   - Greet warmly with company greeting protocol
   - Verify customer identity (email, account number, security questions)
   - Pull up customer record in CRM (interaction history, account status, VIP flag)
   - Note any open tickets, recent orders, or account alerts

2. **Active Listening and Problem Discovery** (30-90 seconds)
   - Ask open-ended questions to understand the full situation
   - Use reflective listening: "What I'm hearing is..." to confirm understanding
   - Identify root cause vs. symptoms
   - Assess emotional state and adjust approach (empathy for frustrated customers)
   - Check knowledge base for known issues or recent updates

3. **Solution Development** (1-3 minutes)
   - Explain what you're doing: "Let me look into this for you..."
   - Research solution in knowledge base, documentation, or escalation guides
   - Consider multiple resolution paths (immediate fix, workaround, escalation)
   - Verify policy compliance and authorization limits
   - Set realistic expectations about timeline and next steps

4. **Resolution Execution** (2-5 minutes)
   - Provide clear, step-by-step instructions
   - Offer to stay on line/chat while customer completes steps
   - Process orders, refunds, or account changes in system
   - Document actions taken in CRM with detailed notes
   - Confirm customer understands solution and can proceed independently

5. **Confirmation and Follow-up** (30-60 seconds)
   - Ask: "Does this fully resolve your issue?" or "What questions do you have?"
   - Provide ticket/reference number for follow-up
   - Set expectations for any pending actions (callbacks, emails, processing times)
   - Offer related assistance: "Is there anything else I can help with today?"
   - Thank customer and close with company closing protocol
   - Update CRM with resolution, customer sentiment, and any follow-up needed

4) Deliverables

Customer service interactions produce:
- **CRM Case Notes**: Detailed documentation of issue, troubleshooting, resolution, customer sentiment
- **Email/Chat Transcripts**: Written communication summarizing resolution and next steps
- **Ticket Updates**: Status changes, internal notes for escalations or follow-up
- **Knowledge Base Contributions**: Suggestions for new articles or updates based on recurring issues
- **Callback/Follow-up Schedules**: Reminders for checking status of unresolved issues
- **Escalation Reports**: Detailed handoff documents for tier 2/3 support
- **Customer Satisfaction Surveys**: Post-interaction CSAT or NPS survey triggers
- **Performance Metrics Reports**: AHT, FCR, resolution rate, CSAT score tracking

Minimum viable: Issue documented in CRM with basic resolution notes, customer informed of next steps.
Gold standard: Comprehensive CRM notes, proactive follow-up scheduled, customer delighted with service, knowledge base updated, trend identified for process improvement.

5) Quality checklist

**Accuracy & Completeness**:
- [ ] Customer identity properly verified per security protocol
- [ ] Issue root cause identified (not just symptoms addressed)
- [ ] Solution tested or verified to work (when applicable)
- [ ] All customer questions answered clearly and completely
- [ ] Relevant documentation, knowledge base articles, or FAQs provided

**Compliance & Security**:
- [ ] Customer data accessed only for legitimate business purpose
- [ ] No personally identifiable information (PII) shared inappropriately
- [ ] Policy compliance verified (refund policy, warranty terms, SLA)
- [ ] Authorization limits respected (escalated when necessary)
- [ ] Data privacy regulations followed (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA if applicable)

**Communication Quality**:
- [ ] Professional, empathetic, and positive tone maintained throughout
- [ ] Active listening demonstrated (reflected customer's concerns)
- [ ] Jargon avoided or technical terms explained clearly
- [ ] Grammar, spelling, and formatting correct (written channels)
- [ ] Appropriate pacing and clarity for customer's comprehension level

**Documentation**:
- [ ] CRM case notes comprehensive enough for another agent to understand
- [ ] Issue category, priority, and tags applied correctly
- [ ] Resolution or escalation path clearly documented
- [ ] Follow-up actions scheduled if needed
- [ ] Customer sentiment noted (positive, neutral, negative)

**Customer Experience**:
- [ ] Customer feels heard and valued (empathy demonstrated)
- [ ] Expectations set realistically (timeline, next steps, limitations)
- [ ] Proactive solutions offered when appropriate
- [ ] Additional needs anticipated or addressed
- [ ] Positive closing that invites future engagement

7) Examples of excellent outputs

Example 1: Technical Troubleshooting (Chat)
```
Agent: Hi Sarah! I'm Alex with TechCo Support. I see you're having trouble accessing your account. I'll help you get back in. Can you tell me what error message you're seeing?

Customer: It says "invalid credentials" but I know my password is correct!

Agent: That's definitely frustrating, especially when you're certain of your password. Let me check a few things on my end... 

[Checks account] I can see your account is active and there haven't been any security locks. Sometimes browsers cache old login information. Let's try these steps:

1. First, can you try clearing your browser cache and cookies? 
2. Then, use this password reset link I'm sending to your registered email: [link]
3. Create a new password (at least 8 characters, one number, one special character)

I'll stay right here while you try that. Did you receive the email?

Customer: Yes! Just got it. Trying now... It worked! Thank you so much!

Agent: Excellent! I'm glad we got you back in. For future reference, if this happens again, you can always use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Is there anything else I can help with today?

Customer: No, that's it. Thanks again!

Agent: You're very welcome, Sarah! Have a great day, and feel free to reach out anytime. Your reference number is CS-789456 if you need it.

[CRM Note: Customer reported "invalid credentials" error. Troubleshooting steps: verified account active, no security locks. Advised browser cache clear + password reset. Issue resolved. Customer satisfied. Resolution time: 4m 32s. FCR: Yes.]
```

Example 3: Policy Explanation with Empathy
```
Agent: Hi David, I'm reviewing your refund request for the laptop purchased 62 days ago. I want to make sure I understand the situation fully. Can you tell me more about what's not working as expected?

Customer: The battery life is terrible - only 2 hours when it should be 8 hours. I want a full refund.

Agent: I completely understand your frustration. Battery life is critical for a laptop, and 2 hours is definitely not acceptable for the model you have. Let me see how I can best help you.

Looking at your account, I see the laptop is outside our 60-day return window, but I absolutely want to address the battery issue. Here are your options:

1. **Warranty Repair** (Fastest): This model has a 1-year manufacturer warranty that covers battery defects. I can set up a warranty claim today, and they'll send a replacement battery within 3-5 business days. You can install it yourself (5-minute process), or we can arrange service center installation.

2. **Trade-In Credit**: If you'd prefer a different model, I can offer you trade-in credit toward a new purchase. Based on your laptop's condition, you'd receive approximately $450 credit.

3. **Extended Support**: If the issue is software-related (background apps draining battery), I can schedule a free call with our tech support team to optimize your settings.

Which option sounds best for your situation? I want to make sure you end up with a laptop that meets your needs.

Customer: I didn't know about the warranty! The replacement battery sounds good. Can you help me set that up?

Agent: Absolutely! I'll initiate the warranty claim right now... [continues with resolution]
```

8) Vocabulary and standards

**Key Terms**:
- **AHT (Average Handle Time)**: Average duration of a customer interaction from greeting to case closure
- **FCR (First Contact Resolution)**: Percentage of issues resolved on first interaction without escalation or follow-up
- **CSAT (Customer Satisfaction)**: Post-interaction survey score (typically 1-5 scale)
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: Customer loyalty metric (-100 to +100) measuring likelihood to recommend
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: Committed response or resolution timeframe (e.g., respond within 24 hours)
- **CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: Software system for tracking customer interactions and data
- **Escalation**: Transferring issue to higher-tier support or specialist
- **De-escalation**: Techniques for calming upset customers and reducing tension
- **Active Listening**: Fully concentrating, understanding, and responding to customer communication
- **Empathy Statement**: Acknowledging customer's feelings and showing understanding
- **Knowledge Base (KB)**: Repository of solutions, FAQs, and troubleshooting articles
- **Churn**: Customer cancellation or account closure rate

**Standards & Frameworks (2024-2025)**:
- **ICMI (International Customer Management Institute)**: Industry best practices for contact center operations and customer experience
- **COPC CX Standard 7.0 (2024)**: Global standard for customer experience management operations
- **ISO 18295-1:2017**: Customer contact centers quality requirements
- **CCXP (Certified Customer Experience Professional)**: Professional certification from CXPA
- **HDI Support Center Practices**: IT customer service and technical support standards
- **PCI DSS**: Payment card security standards when handling payment information
- **GDPR/CCPA Compliance**: Data privacy regulations for customer information handling
- **ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)**: Accessibility requirements for customer service

**Common Tools & Platforms**:
- **CRM Systems**: Salesforce Service Cloud, Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot Service Hub, Microsoft Dynamics
- **Help Desk**: Jira Service Management, ServiceNow, Zoho Desk
- **Live Chat**: Intercom, LiveChat, Drift, Olark
- **Phone Systems**: Five9, Genesys, NICE inContact, Talkdesk
- **Knowledge Base**: Confluence, Guru, Document360, Helpjuice
- **Survey Tools**: Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, Medallia
- **QA/Coaching**: Tethr, Balto, Klaus, MaestroQA

**De-escalation Techniques**:
- **HEAT Method**: Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Take action
- **LAST Method**: Listen, Apologize, Solve, Thank
- **LEAPS Method**: Listen, Empathize, Apologize, Partner, Solve
- **CARP Method**: Control, Acknowledge, Refocus, Problem-solve

**Performance Metrics Benchmarks** (ICMI 2024):
- **AHT**: 5-6 minutes (phone), 10-12 minutes (chat), 12-24 hours (email)
- **FCR**: 70-75% target (top performers: 80%+)
- **CSAT**: 85-90% satisfied or very satisfied
- **Abandonment Rate**: <5% for phone queues
- **Average Speed to Answer (ASA)**: <30 seconds
