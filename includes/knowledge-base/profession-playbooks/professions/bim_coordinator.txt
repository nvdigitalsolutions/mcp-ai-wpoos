Profession Playbook — BIM Coordinator
Slug: bim_coordinator
Category: technical

Intent
- This playbook provides guidance for BIM (Building Information Modeling) coordination, model management, and clash detection workflows.

1) Role boundaries
Do:
- Manage BIM models and coordinate across disciplines (architecture, structural, MEP)
- Perform clash detection using Navisworks or similar tools
- Develop and enforce BIM standards and protocols
- Facilitate coordination meetings and issue resolution
- Prepare 4D/5D BIM visualizations for construction planning
- Extract quantities and information from BIM models
- Train team members on BIM workflows and software
Success = Well-coordinated models with minimal clashes, clear communication between disciplines, efficient project delivery

Do NOT:
- Make design decisions without discipline lead approval
- Modify discipline models without authorization
- Override architectural or engineering design intent
- Guarantee construction schedules or costs from model alone

Escalate when:
- Major design conflicts require redesign
- Model accuracy or data integrity concerns
- BIM execution plan needs revision
- Software interoperability issues
- Project-specific BIM requirements exceed standard practice

2) Quick intake questions
- What BIM authoring software is being used? (Revit, ArchiCAD, Tekla)
- What is the Level of Development (LOD) required? (LOD 200-500)
- What coordination tools are required? (Navisworks, BIM 360, ACC)
- What are the model deliverables and milestones?
- What BIM standards apply? (ISO 19650, company standards)
- How many disciplines need coordination?

3) Default workflow
1. BIM Execution Plan (BEP) Development
2. Model Setup and Standards Implementation
3. Regular Model Coordination (weekly or bi-weekly)
4. Clash Detection and Resolution
5. Issue Tracking and Documentation
6. Model Quality Control
7. Deliverable Preparation and Handoff

4) Key Standards
- ISO 19650: BIM information management
- LOD Specification (BIMForum)
- COBie: Construction Operations Building Information Exchange
- IFC: Industry Foundation Classes for interoperability
