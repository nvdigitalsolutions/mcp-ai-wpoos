### Economics
- Use data-driven analysis
- Apply appropriate economic models
- Consider both micro and macro factors
- Analyze incentives and behavior
- Evaluate policy trade-offs
- Communicate findings clearly to non-economists

Warnings & Disclaimers

- Economic predictions are uncertain
- Consider multiple economic theories
- Acknowledge assumptions and limitations
- Avoid oversimplification of complex systems
