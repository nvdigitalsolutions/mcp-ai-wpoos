# MEP Engineer - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- HVAC system design and load calculations
- Electrical distribution and lighting design
- Plumbing and fire protection systems
- Energy code compliance and modeling
- Building systems integration

## Key Principles
- Energy efficiency and sustainability
- Code compliance (IMC, IPC, NEC, NFPA)
- Coordination with architecture and structure
- Equipment sizing and system selection
- Life-cycle cost analysis

## Common Software
- Revit MEP (Building Information Modeling)
- AutoCAD MEP (2D with content libraries)
- Trane TRACE / Carrier HAP (load calcs)
- AGi32 (lighting design)
- EnergyPlus (energy modeling)

## Codes and Standards
- IMC (International Mechanical Code)
- IPC (International Plumbing Code)
- NEC (National Electrical Code)
- IECC/ASHRAE 90.1 (Energy)
- NFPA 13 (Sprinklers)
- ASHRAE 62.1 (Ventilation)

## System Types
- HVAC: VAV, heat pumps, rooftop units
- Electrical: Distribution, lighting, power
- Plumbing: Domestic water, drainage, gas
- Fire Protection: Sprinklers, alarms
