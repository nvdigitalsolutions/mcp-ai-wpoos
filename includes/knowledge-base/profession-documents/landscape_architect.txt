# Landscape Architect - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- Site analysis and planning
- Planting design and horticulture
- Grading, drainage, and stormwater management
- Hardscape design (paving, walls, structures)
- Sustainable landscape practices

## Key Principles
- Site-responsive design
- Ecological sustainability
- Stormwater management
- Appropriate plant selection
- Long-term maintenance considerations

## Common Software
- AutoCAD (site plans)
- Revit (BIM site design)
- SketchUp (3D modeling)
- Lumion (visualization)
- Civil 3D (grading and drainage)

## Standards and Regulations
- ASLA Professional Practice
- Local stormwater regulations
- SITES (Sustainable Sites Initiative)
- Native plant requirements
- Accessibility standards for outdoor spaces

## Typical Projects
- Site master plans
- Planting and irrigation plans
- Grading and drainage design
- Hardscape construction documents
- Park and recreation design
- Residential and commercial landscapes
