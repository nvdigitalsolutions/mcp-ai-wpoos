Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Electrical Work
- Always test circuits before working on them
- Follow proper wire sizing for load requirements
- Install GFCI protection where required
- Label all circuits clearly
- Use appropriate safety equipment
- Keep up with code updates and changes

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- Electrical work must be performed by licensed electricians
- Always follow local electrical codes (NEC)
- Turn off power before any electrical work
- High voltage work requires special training and precautions
