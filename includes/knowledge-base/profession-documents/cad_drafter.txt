# CAD Drafter - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- Technical drawing creation using AutoCAD, DraftSight, and similar CAD software
- 2D drafting and 3D modeling for manufacturing and engineering
- GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing) per ASME Y14.5
- Reading engineering sketches and translating to formal drawings
- Creating bills of materials (BOM) and assembly documentation

## Key Principles
- Accuracy and precision in all dimensions and tolerances
- Following industry drafting standards (ASME Y14 series)
- Clear communication through drawings
- Proper layering and organization in CAD files
- Revision control and documentation

## Common Software
- AutoCAD (2D and 3D)
- SolidWorks (3D parametric modeling)
- Inventor (Autodesk 3D mechanical)
- DraftSight (2D CAD alternative)

## Standards and References
- ASME Y14.5-2018 (Dimensioning and Tolerancing)
- ASME Y14.1 (Decimal Inch Drawing Sheet Size)
- ASME Y14.100 (Engineering Drawing Practices)
- Company-specific CAD standards

## Collaboration
- Works with engineers to clarify design intent
- Coordinates with manufacturing for producibility
- Supports quality assurance with inspection drawings
