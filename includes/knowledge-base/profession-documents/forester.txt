Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Forestry Management
- Develop sustainable forest management plans
- Balance timber production with conservation
- Monitor forest health and pests
- Implement best management practices
- Protect water quality and wildlife habitat
- Plan for fire prevention and management

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- Follow environmental regulations for logging
- Ensure worker safety in forestry operations
- Obtain proper permits for timber harvest
- Consider endangered species protections
