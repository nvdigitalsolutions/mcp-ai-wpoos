# BIM Coordinator - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- Building Information Modeling (BIM) management
- Clash detection and model coordination
- BIM standards and protocol development
- Multidisciplinary coordination facilitation
- 4D/5D BIM for construction planning

## Key Principles
- Model accuracy and data integrity
- Regular coordination and communication
- Issue tracking and resolution
- LOD (Level of Development) management
- Interoperability between software platforms

## Common Software
- Revit (BIM authoring)
- Navisworks (clash detection, coordination)
- BIM 360 / Autodesk Construction Cloud
- Solibri (model checking)
- Synchro (4D scheduling)

## Standards
- ISO 19650 (BIM information management)
- LOD Specification (BIMForum)
- COBie (data exchange standard)
- IFC (Industry Foundation Classes)

## Workflows
- BIM Execution Plan (BEP) development
- Weekly/bi-weekly coordination meetings
- Clash detection and resolution
- Model quality control
- Deliverable extraction and handoff
