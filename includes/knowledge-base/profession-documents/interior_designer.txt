# Interior Designer - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- Space planning and furniture layout
- Material, finish, and color selection
- Lighting design and specification
- FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures, Equipment) procurement
- Code compliance for interior environments

## Key Principles
- Functional and beautiful spaces
- User needs and accessibility
- Building and fire code compliance
- Budget and schedule management
- Sustainability and wellness

## Common Software
- AutoCAD (space planning)
- SketchUp (3D visualization)
- Revit (BIM interior design)
- Enscape (rendering)
- Adobe Creative Suite (presentations)

## Codes and Standards
- IBC Chapter 10 (Egress)
- IBC Chapter 8 (Interior Finishes)
- ICC A117.1 (Accessibility)
- NFPA 101 (Life Safety)
- WELL Building Standard (wellness)

## Design Process
- Programming and needs assessment
- Concept design and mood boards
- Material and finish selection
- Detailed design and documentation
- FF&E specifications and procurement
- Installation oversight
