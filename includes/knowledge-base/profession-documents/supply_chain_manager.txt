Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Supply Chain Management
- Optimize inventory levels to balance cost and service
- Build strong supplier relationships
- Implement just-in-time principles where appropriate
- Use technology for visibility and tracking
- Plan for disruptions and alternatives
- Focus on continuous improvement

Warnings & Disclaimers

- Contract terms must be clearly defined
- Risk mitigation in supply chain is critical
