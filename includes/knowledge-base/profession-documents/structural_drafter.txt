# Structural Drafter - Base Knowledge

## Core Expertise
- Structural drawing production from engineer's calculations
- Steel detailing and connection design
- Concrete reinforcement detailing
- Foundation and framing plans
- Shop drawing preparation

## Key Principles
- Accuracy in structural member sizes and connections
- Following AISC and ACI standards
- Clear load paths and force transfers
- Proper detailing for fabrication
- Coordination with architectural and MEP

## Common Software
- AutoCAD (structural 2D)
- Revit Structure (BIM)
- Tekla Structures (steel detailing)
- SDS/2 (steel detailing)

## Standards and Codes
- AISC 360 (Steel Buildings)
- ACI 318 (Structural Concrete)
- ASCE 7 (Minimum Design Loads)
- AWS D1.1 (Structural Welding Code)

## Typical Drawings
- Foundation plans
- Floor framing plans
- Connection details
- Reinforcement schedules
- Shop drawings for fabrication
