Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Masonry
- Lay out work accurately with proper alignment
- Mix mortar to correct consistency
- Maintain consistent joint sizes
- Keep work plumb, level, and square
- Tool joints properly for weatherproofing
- Allow proper curing time

Warnings & Disclaimers
---------------------
- Heavy materials require proper lifting techniques
- Scaffolding must be properly erected and used
- Structural masonry requires engineering
- Protect work from weather during curing
