Knowledge Base Notes
-------------------
### Dispatching
- Monitor all units and their status
- Assign calls efficiently
- Communicate clearly and concisely
- Maintain accurate records
- Coordinate with other agencies as needed
- Handle multiple situations simultaneously

Warnings & Disclaimers

- Prioritize emergency situations
- Document all dispatches accurately
- Stay calm under pressure
