# Starter catalogue licence

SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT

The professions in this directory are a reduced sample shipped with the
`nvoos/laravel` Composer package under the package's MIT licence. They exist so
the package is usable — and testable — without a catalogue.

They are **not** the full catalogue. The complete NV oOS profession catalogue
(260 base professions) is proprietary and ships with the NV oOS Laravel
application. Point `oos.professions.path` at it, or at any catalogue you are
licensed to use, to replace this sample.

The package's own manifest (`manifest.json`) declares `catalogue_version`; a
deployment catalogue replaces both files.
