NV oOS Site Bridge

A ChatGPT and Codex plugin from NV Digital Solutions that connects to a WordPress site running NV oOS (Open Operator System) through its native MCP bridge — site operations, content publishing, and WooCommerce store management with the connected account's own permissions.

About the plugin

The plugin bundles three skills — site operations, content research & publishing, and store management — over the site's streamable-HTTP MCP endpoint. You link your site account with OAuth, and every tool call runs with your account's permissions.
