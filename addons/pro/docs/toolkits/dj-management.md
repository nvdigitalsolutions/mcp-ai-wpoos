# DJ Management Toolkit

> Entertainment-business management for DJs and event entertainers: clients, bookings,
> contracts, music libraries, playlists (with AI generation and BPM analysis), equipment
> tracking and maintenance, and event timelines. 18 tools.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Activation setting** | `enable_dj_management_toolkit` |
| **Admin location** | NV oOS → Settings → Pro Features → DJ Management |
| **Tools** | 18 |

---

## Tool categories

- **Clients & bookings:** `create_client_profile`, `create_event_booking`,
  `update_event_details`, `client_communication_log`, `send_event_confirmation`
- **Music & playlists:** `manage_music_library`, `create_playlist`, `generate_playlist_ai`,
  `analyze_track_bpm`, `mix_transition_planner`
- **Equipment:** `add_equipment_item`, `reserve_equipment`, `track_equipment_maintenance`,
  `equipment_inventory_report`
- **Money:** `generate_dj_contract`, `send_client_invoice`, `track_event_payments`
- **Event ops:** `generate_event_timeline`

Tool source: `addons/pro/includes/tools/dj-management/`.

---

## Activation

1. Activate the Pro add-on.
2. Toggle **DJ Management** under **NV oOS → Settings → Pro Features**.
