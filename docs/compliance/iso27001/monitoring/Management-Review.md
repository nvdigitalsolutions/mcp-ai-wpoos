## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the process for conducting management reviews of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for the NV oOS WordPress plugin, in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements (Clause 9.3).

Management reviews ensure:
- ISMS continues to be suitable, adequate, and effective
- Information security objectives are achieved
- Resources are appropriately allocated
- Continuous improvement opportunities are identified
- Strategic alignment with business objectives

---

## 2. Scope

Management reviews cover:
- ISMS policy and objectives
- Security performance and metrics
- Risk management effectiveness
- Audit findings and corrective actions
- Security incidents and lessons learned
- Resource adequacy
- Changes affecting ISMS
- Opportunities for improvement
- Compliance with legal and contractual requirements

---

## 3. Review Frequency

**Scheduled Reviews:**
- **Semi-Annual:** Comprehensive ISMS management review (required minimum)
- **Quarterly:** Focused security metrics review
- **Monthly:** Security performance dashboard review (informal)

**Ad-Hoc Reviews:**
- After major security incidents
- Following significant organizational changes
- After major system/feature releases
- When required by external factors (new regulations, major threats)

---

## 4. Review Participants

### Required Attendees
- **Management Representative:** CEO/Founder or designated executive
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** ISMS owner
- **Lead Developer:** Technical perspective
- **Operations Lead:** Infrastructure and operations perspective

### Optional/As Needed
- **Legal Counsel:** For compliance and legal matters
- **External Auditor:** During pre-certification or post-certification reviews
- **Risk Manager:** For risk assessment discussions
- **Specific SMEs:** Based on agenda topics

## 5. Review Inputs

Management reviews shall consider the following inputs:

### 5.1 Status of Actions from Previous Reviews
- Action items from last management review
- Status of implementation (complete, in progress, delayed)
- Reasons for delays or non-completion
- Updated timelines for pending actions

### 5.2 Changes in External and Internal Issues
**External Changes:**
- New or emerging threats and vulnerabilities
- Changes in threat landscape
- New attack vectors or techniques
- Industry security trends
- Competitor security incidents

**Internal Changes:**
- Organizational changes (team structure, responsibilities)
- New projects or features
- Technology changes
- Process changes
- Policy updates

### 5.3 Feedback on Information Security Performance

**Security Metrics:**
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Trend analysis

**Example Metrics:**
| Metric | Target | Current | Trend |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| Security incidents | ≤5/month | 2/month | ↓ |
| Mean time to detect (MTTD) | ≤24 hours | 18 hours | ↓ |
| Mean time to respond (MTTR) | ≤48 hours | 36 hours | ↓ |
| Vulnerability remediation time | Critical: ≤48h, High: ≤2wk | Meeting targets | → |
| Security training completion | 100% | 95% | ↑ |
| Failed authentication attempts | <1000/day | 450/day | → |
| Open critical vulnerabilities | 0 | 0 | → |

### 5.4 Feedback from Interested Parties
- User feedback on security features
- Community security reports
- Contributor feedback
- Customer/client security concerns
- Partner security requirements
- Regulatory feedback (if applicable)

### 5.5 Results of Risk Assessment and Status of Risk Treatment Plan
- Current risk register
- New risks identified since last review
- Risk treatment status (by risk ID)
- Residual risk levels
- Risk appetite alignment
- Changes in risk profile

### 5.6 Opportunities for Continual Improvement
- Process improvements identified
- Technology upgrades or changes
- Security tool enhancements
- Training opportunities
- Efficiency improvements
- Cost reduction opportunities

### 5.7 Internal Audit Results
- Summary of internal audit findings
- Non-conformities (critical, major, minor)
- Observations and recommendations
- Corrective action status
- Audit conclusion and overall ISMS effectiveness

### 5.8 Fulfillment of Information Security Objectives
- Progress toward each security objective
- Objective achievement metrics
- Reasons for non-achievement
- Revised objectives (if needed)

### 5.9 Incidents, Nonconformities, and Corrective Actions
**Security Incidents:**
- Number and severity of incidents
- Incident response effectiveness
- Root causes
- Lessons learned implementation

**Nonconformities:**
- From internal audits
- From external assessments
- Self-identified gaps
- Corrective action effectiveness

**Corrective Actions:**
- Status of ongoing corrective actions
- Verification of completed actions
- Effectiveness of corrective actions

### 5.10 Monitoring and Measurement Results
- Security monitoring results
- Availability metrics (uptime)
- Performance metrics
- Compliance monitoring results
- Third-party security assessments

### 5.11 Compliance Obligations
- Legal and regulatory compliance status
- Contractual security obligations
- Standards compliance (ISO 27001, OWASP, WordPress guidelines)
- Licensing compliance (GPL v3)
- Data protection compliance (GDPR)

## 6. Review Process

### 6.1 Preparation (2-3 Weeks Before Meeting)

**CISO Responsibilities:**
1. Schedule management review meeting
2. Invite required and optional participants
3. Prepare review input materials:
   - Previous review action items
   - Security metrics report
   - Risk assessment update
   - Incident summary
   - Audit findings summary
   - Objective achievement report
4. Distribute materials to attendees (1 week before meeting)
5. Request input from stakeholders

**Participant Responsibilities:**
- Review materials in advance
- Prepare specific inputs (if assigned)
- Submit questions or concerns in advance

### 6.2 Management Review Meeting

**Duration:** 2-4 hours (semi-annual), 1-2 hours (quarterly)

**Agenda Template:**

1. **Opening** (5 minutes)
   - Review agenda
   - Confirm attendees
   - Review previous meeting action items

2. **Review Inputs** (60-90 minutes)
   - Present each input topic (as per Section 5)
   - Discussion and questions
   - Identify issues and concerns

3. **Analysis and Discussion** (30-45 minutes)
   - Overall ISMS effectiveness assessment
   - Identification of patterns and trends
   - Discussion of significant issues
   - Consideration of opportunities

4. **Decisions and Actions** (30-45 minutes)
   - Review outputs (Section 7)
   - Make decisions
   - Assign action items with owners and due dates
   - Agree on priorities

5. **Closing** (10 minutes)
   - Summarize decisions and actions
   - Confirm next review date
   - Address any final questions

### 6.3 Documentation

**Meeting Minutes:**
- Date, time, location (or virtual)
- Attendees and apologies
- Summary of inputs reviewed
- Key discussion points
- Decisions made
- Action items (owner, due date, priority)
- Next review date

**Distribution:**
- Draft minutes within 5 business days
- Review and approval by management
- Distribution to all attendees and stakeholders
- Filed in ISMS documentation repository

Management reviews shall produce documented decisions and actions related to:

### 7.1 Opportunities for Continual Improvement
- Identified improvement opportunities
- Priority ranking
- Resources required
- Implementation timeline
- Expected benefits

### 7.2 Changes to the ISMS
**Policy Changes:**
- ISMS policy updates
- Procedure changes
- Control modifications
- Scope adjustments

**Resource Changes:**
- Budget allocations
- Personnel changes
- Tool/technology investments
- Training requirements

### 7.3 Resource Needs
**Identified Needs:**
- Additional personnel (hiring or contractors)
- Security tools or services
- Training and development
- Infrastructure upgrades
- Budget increases

**Approval:**
- Management approves resource allocations
- Budget adjustments documented
- Timeline for resource acquisition

### 7.4 Changes to Information Security Objectives
**Objective Review:**
- Progress toward current objectives
- Revision of objectives (if not achievable or no longer relevant)
- New objectives added
- Metrics updated

**Updated Objectives:**
- Clear, measurable, achievable
- Aligned with business goals
- Time-bound
- Assigned ownership

## 8. Action Item Tracking

### 8.1 Action Item Management

**Action Item Format:**
- **ID:** Unique identifier (e.g., MR-2026-01-001)
- **Description:** Clear description of action
- **Owner:** Individual responsible
- **Due Date:** Completion deadline
- **Priority:** High / Medium / Low
- **Status:** Not Started / In Progress / Complete / Overdue

**Tracking:**
- Action item register maintained by CISO
- Monthly status updates
- Escalation of overdue items
- Review at next management review

### 8.2 Action Item Categories

**Common Action Types:**
- Policy or procedure updates
- Control implementations or enhancements
- Risk treatment actions
- Corrective actions from audits or incidents
- Resource acquisitions
- Training or awareness initiatives
- Tool or technology implementations

## 12. Continuous Improvement

### 12.1 Review Effectiveness

**Evaluate Management Review Process:**
- Are reviews conducted on schedule?
- Are all required inputs provided?
- Are decisions implemented?
- Are action items completed on time?
- Is ISMS improving as a result of reviews?

**Feedback:**
- Solicit feedback from participants
- Assess meeting effectiveness (survey)
- Identify process improvements
- Update this procedure as needed

### 12.2 Maturity Assessment

**ISMS Maturity Levels:**
1. **Initial:** Ad-hoc processes, reactive
2. **Managed:** Documented processes, some automation
3. **Defined:** Standardized processes, integrated
4. **Quantitatively Managed:** Metrics-driven, predictable
5. **Optimizing:** Continuous improvement, innovative

**Goal:** Progress from current level to next level over time
