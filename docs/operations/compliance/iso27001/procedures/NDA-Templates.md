## 1. Purpose

This document provides comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) templates and procedures for protecting confidential and proprietary information of NV Digital Solutions and the NV oOS WordPress plugin project. These templates ensure:

- Protection of confidential information shared with employees, contractors, and third parties
- Clear definition of confidential information and obligations
- Legal enforceability of confidentiality commitments
- Compliance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Control A.6.6
- Consistent application of confidentiality requirements across all relationships

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

NDAs are required for:

- **All Employees:** Standard NDA as part of employment contract
- **All Contractors:** Comprehensive NDA before engagement begins
- **All Consultants:** Project-specific NDA with scope limitations
- **Third-Party Vendors:** Mutual NDA for service providers
- **Business Partners:** Mutual NDA for collaboration and integration partners
- **Beta Testers:** Limited NDA for pre-release access
- **Security Researchers:** Special NDA for vulnerability disclosure
- **Temporary Workers:** Standard NDA aligned with engagement duration

### 2.2 Information Covered

Confidential information includes but is not limited to:
- Source code and technical architecture
- API keys, credentials, and access tokens
- Customer and user data
- Business strategies and roadmaps
- Financial information
- Security procedures and vulnerabilities
- Proprietary algorithms and methodologies
- Trade secrets and intellectual property

## 3. NDA Template 1: Employee NDA

**Use Case:** All employees and long-term team members

### 3.1 Employee Non-Disclosure Agreement

**EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] ("Effective Date") by and between:

**NV Digital Solutions** ("Company")  
[Address]

and

**[EMPLOYEE NAME]** ("Employee")  
[Address]

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, Employee will have access to Confidential Information (as defined below) in the course of employment with Company; and

WHEREAS, Company desires to protect its Confidential Information and Employee agrees to maintain such information in confidence;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

"Confidential Information" means any and all information, whether written, oral, electronic, visual or in any other form, disclosed by Company to Employee or learned by Employee during the course of employment, including but not limited to:

a) **Technical Information:** Source code, algorithms, technical architecture, system designs, API specifications, database schemas, security implementations, encryption keys, access credentials, development methodologies, technical documentation, and any other technical data or know-how.

b) **Business Information:** Business plans, strategies, roadmaps, marketing plans, pricing information, customer lists, supplier information, financial data, sales data, business forecasts, and any other business-related information.

c) **Customer Information:** Customer data, user information, usage statistics, feedback, support tickets, personally identifiable information (PII), and any data collected or processed by the NV oOS plugin.

d) **Product Information:** Product specifications, features under development, release schedules, beta versions, testing data, bug reports, vulnerability information, and product roadmaps.

e) **Security Information:** Security procedures, vulnerability assessments, penetration test results, security incident reports, access control policies, authentication mechanisms, and any security-related documentation.

f) **Intellectual Property:** Patents, patent applications, trademarks, trade secrets, copyrights, inventions, discoveries, improvements, and any other intellectual property.

g) **Third-Party Information:** Any confidential information received from third parties that Company is obligated to keep confidential.

h) **Other Information:** Any other information that is marked as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or similar designation, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential based on the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

**2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

Confidential Information does not include information that:

a) Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by Employee;
b) Was rightfully in Employee's possession prior to disclosure by Company, as evidenced by Employee's written records;
c) Is rightfully received by Employee from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation;
d) Is independently developed by Employee without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or
e) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided Employee gives Company prompt written notice of such requirement and cooperates with Company's efforts to seek protective orders.

**3. OBLIGATIONS OF EMPLOYEE**

Employee agrees to:

a) **Maintain Confidentiality:** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without Company's prior written consent.

b) **Use Restrictions:** Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing Employee's duties for Company and not for any other purpose.

c) **Protection Measures:** Exercise the same degree of care in protecting Confidential Information as Employee uses to protect their own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

d) **Limited Disclosure:** Disclose Confidential Information only to other employees, contractors, or agents of Company who have a legitimate need to know and who have signed confidentiality agreements.

e) **No Copying:** Not copy, reproduce, or create derivative works based on Confidential Information except as necessary to perform Employee's duties.

f) **Return of Materials:** Upon termination of employment or upon Company's request, immediately return all Confidential Information and any copies thereof, including deleting any electronic copies from personal devices.

g) **No Reverse Engineering:** Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software, code, or technology disclosed as Confidential Information.

h) **Incident Reporting:** Immediately notify Company of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

**4. TERM**

The obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement shall:

a) **During Employment:** Apply throughout the term of Employee's employment with Company;

b) **Post-Employment:** Continue for a period of **five (5) years** after termination of employment for all Confidential Information, except for:
   - Trade secrets, which shall remain confidential indefinitely;
   - Source code, which shall remain confidential for seven (7) years;
   - Customer data, which shall remain confidential for three (3) years or as required by applicable data protection laws, whichever is longer.

**5. OWNERSHIP OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

Employee acknowledges that:

a) All Confidential Information is and remains the sole and exclusive property of Company;
b) No license or other rights to Confidential Information are granted except as expressly stated in this Agreement;
c) This Agreement does not grant Employee any ownership interest in Company's Confidential Information or intellectual property.

**6. REMEDIES**

Employee acknowledges that:

a) Breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to Company for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate;
b) Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief to prevent or remedy any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement;
c) Company may seek all available legal and equitable remedies, including monetary damages;
d) Employee may be held personally liable for any breach of this Agreement.

**7. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

a) **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [Jurisdiction], without regard to conflict of law principles.

b) **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.

c) **Amendment:** This Agreement may be amended only by written agreement signed by both parties.

d) **Severability:** If any provision is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

e) **Waiver:** No waiver of any provision shall be deemed a waiver of any other provision or subsequent breach.

f) **Survival:** The obligations under this Agreement shall survive termination of employment.

**8. ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

Employee acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.

## 4. NDA Template 2: Contractor/Consultant NDA

**Use Case:** Independent contractors, consultants, freelancers

### 4.1 Contractor Confidentiality Agreement

**CONTRACTOR NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

**NV Digital Solutions** ("Company")  
and  
**[CONTRACTOR NAME/ENTITY]** ("Contractor")

**1. PURPOSE**

Contractor will provide [DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES] to Company and will have access to Confidential Information. This Agreement governs the protection of such information.

**2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

Same definition as Employee NDA (Section 1 above).

**3. CONTRACTOR OBLIGATIONS**

Contractor agrees to:

a) **Maintain Confidentiality:** Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential.

b) **Use Restrictions:** Use Confidential Information only for the purpose of providing services to Company under the engagement agreement.

c) **Protection:** Apply industry-standard security measures to protect Confidential Information, including:
   - Encryption of data at rest and in transit;
   - Secure storage with access controls;
   - Use of secure communication channels;
   - Regular security updates and patches.

d) **Subcontractor Restrictions:** Not disclose Confidential Information to subcontractors without Company's prior written consent. If consent is granted, ensure subcontractors sign equivalent NDAs.

e) **Data Handling:** Comply with all applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) when handling Confidential Information.

f) **Return/Destruction:** Upon completion of services or termination of engagement:
   - Return all Confidential Information and copies within 7 days;
   - Securely delete all electronic copies;
   - Provide written certification of destruction;
   - Not retain any copies or excerpts.

g) **No Competition:** Not use Confidential Information to compete with Company or develop competing products during engagement and for 12 months thereafter.

**4. TERM**

Confidentiality obligations shall remain in effect for **five (5) years** from the date of disclosure, except for trade secrets which remain confidential indefinitely.

**5. REMEDIES**

Same as Employee NDA (Section 6 above), plus:

- Contractor agrees to indemnify Company for any damages resulting from breach;
- Company may terminate engagement immediately upon breach.

**6. INSURANCE**

Contractor shall maintain:
- Professional liability insurance (minimum $1,000,000);
- Cyber liability insurance (minimum $500,000);
- Provide certificates of insurance upon request.

**7. AUDIT RIGHTS**

Company reserves the right to audit Contractor's compliance with this Agreement upon 48 hours' notice.

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

**NV Digital Solutions** ("Party A")  
and  
**[VENDOR NAME]** ("Party B")

(Collectively, the "Parties")

**1. PURPOSE**

The Parties wish to explore a business relationship regarding [PURPOSE] and will exchange Confidential Information.

**2. DEFINITION**

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party ("Disclosing Party") to the other party ("Receiving Party") that is:
- Marked as confidential;
- Orally disclosed and confirmed in writing within 30 days as confidential;
- Would reasonably be considered confidential based on nature and circumstances.

**3. MUTUAL OBLIGATIONS**

Each Party agrees to:

a) **Protect:** Use same degree of care (but not less than reasonable care) to protect Confidential Information as it uses for its own confidential information.

b) **Use Restrictions:** Use Confidential Information only for evaluating and engaging in the business relationship.

c) **Limited Disclosure:** Disclose only to employees/contractors with need to know who are bound by similar confidentiality obligations.

d) **No Reverse Engineering:** Not reverse engineer any products, software, or technology.

**4. EXCEPTIONS**

Same exclusions as Employee NDA (Section 2 above).

**5. TERM**

Three (3) years from date of disclosure for each piece of Confidential Information.

**6. NO LICENSE**

This Agreement does not grant any license or rights to intellectual property except as expressly stated.

**7. RETURN OF MATERIALS**

Upon request or termination of discussions, each Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within 30 days.

**8. NO OBLIGATION**

This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any business relationship or transaction.

## 6. NDA Template 4: Security Researcher NDA

**Use Case:** Vulnerability disclosure, security testing

### 6.1 Security Researcher Confidentiality Agreement

**SECURITY RESEARCHER NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into between **NV Digital Solutions** ("Company") and **[RESEARCHER NAME]** ("Researcher") regarding vulnerability disclosure.

**1. PURPOSE**

Researcher has discovered or will test for security vulnerabilities in Company's products. This Agreement governs disclosure and handling of vulnerability information.

**2. VULNERABILITY INFORMATION**

"Vulnerability Information" includes:
- Security vulnerabilities discovered;
- Proof-of-concept code or exploits;
- Details of attack vectors;
- Affected systems and versions;
- Recommended remediation steps.

**3. RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE**

Researcher agrees to:

a) **Immediate Notification:** Report vulnerabilities to security@nvdigitalsolutions.com within 24 hours of discovery.

b) **Confidentiality Period:** Keep vulnerability confidential for minimum 90 days to allow Company to develop and deploy fixes.

c) **No Public Disclosure:** Not publicly disclose vulnerability details until:
   - Company has released a fix;
   - Coordinated disclosure date agreed upon; or
   - 90 days have elapsed (whichever comes first).

d) **No Exploitation:** Not exploit vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate its existence.

e) **No Data Exfiltration:** Not access, modify, or delete user data beyond minimal testing.

f) **Scope Limitations:** Only test systems explicitly authorized; do not test:
   - Production user data;
   - Third-party systems;
   - Physical security controls;
   - Social engineering attacks.

**4. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS**

Company agrees to:

a) **Acknowledge Receipt:** Within 24 hours of vulnerability report.

b) **Provide Updates:** Every 14 days on remediation progress.

c) **Credit:** Publicly acknowledge Researcher (unless Researcher requests anonymity).

d) **No Legal Action:** Not pursue legal action against Researcher for good-faith security research conducted in accordance with this Agreement.

**5. BUG BOUNTY** (If Applicable)

Based on severity (CVSS score), Company may provide:
- Critical (9.0-10.0): Up to $5,000
- High (7.0-8.9): Up to $2,500
- Medium (4.0-6.9): Up to $1,000
- Low (0.1-3.9): Up to $500

**6. TERM**

Obligations continue until:
- Public disclosure is made; or
- Five (5) years from vulnerability discovery, whichever comes first.

## 7. NDA Administration Procedures

### 7.1 NDA Lifecycle Management

**Step 1: Selection**
- Determine appropriate NDA template based on relationship type
- Customize template with specific details (names, dates, scope)

**Step 2: Review**
- Legal review for standard templates (annually)
- Legal review for customized NDAs before execution

**Step 3: Execution**
- Obtain signatures from all parties
- Ensure all parties receive executed copies
- Use electronic signature platforms (DocuSign, Adobe Sign) for efficiency

**Step 4: Storage**
- Store executed NDAs securely (encrypted storage)
- Maintain NDA register with key details
- Retain for duration of confidentiality period + 7 years

**Step 5: Monitoring**
- Track expiration dates
- Send reminders for renewal if needed
- Monitor compliance through periodic audits

**Step 6: Enforcement**
- Investigate suspected breaches immediately
- Document breach evidence
- Consult legal counsel
- Pursue remedies as appropriate

### 7.2 NDA Register

Maintain a register with:

| Name | Role | NDA Type | Execution Date | Expiration Date | Status |
|------|------|----------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| [Name] | Employee | Employee NDA | [Date] | [Date] | Active |
| [Name] | Contractor | Contractor NDA | [Date] | [Date] | Active |

### 7.3 Annual Review

Conduct annual review of:
- All active NDAs
- Template updates for legal/regulatory changes
- Compliance with NDA terms
- Breach incidents and remediation

## 9. Breach Response

### 9.1 Breach Identification

Signs of potential breach:
- Confidential information found in public repositories
- Competitor knowledge of non-public information
- Unauthorized access to confidential systems
- Reports from third parties

### 9.2 Response Procedure

**Immediate (Within 24 Hours):**
1. Confirm breach occurred
2. Identify scope and impact
3. Notify CISO and legal counsel
4. Contain further disclosure

**Investigation (Days 2-7):**
1. Collect evidence
2. Interview involved parties
3. Assess damages
4. Determine root cause

**Remediation (Days 8-14):**
1. Implement corrective actions
2. Notify affected parties if required
3. Pursue legal remedies if appropriate
4. Update procedures to prevent recurrence

**Documentation:**
- Maintain detailed incident log
- Document all investigative steps
- Record remediation actions
- Archive for 7 years
