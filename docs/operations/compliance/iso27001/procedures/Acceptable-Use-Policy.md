# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
## Open Operator System (NV oOS) WordPress Plugin

**Document Classification:** Internal  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-05  
**Review Date:** 2026-04-05  
**Document Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the acceptable and appropriate use of NV Digital Solutions' information systems, resources, and data by all personnel, contractors, and third parties. This policy aims to:

- Protect organizational information and assets
- Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
- Prevent misuse of information systems
- Maintain productivity and operational efficiency
- Protect the organization's reputation
- Ensure fair and ethical use of resources

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, consultants, and third-party personnel
- All information systems, including hardware, software, networks, and data
- All organizational resources, physical and digital
- All access methods, including remote access
- All organizational data, regardless of storage location
- Both work-related and personal use of organizational resources (where permitted)

---

## 3. General Acceptable Use Principles

### 3.1 Professional and Ethical Use

All users must:
- Use organizational resources primarily for authorized business purposes
- Act professionally, ethically, and legally at all times
- Respect intellectual property rights and licenses
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Not damage the organization's reputation
- Cooperate with security monitoring and investigations

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities

Users shall NOT:
- Engage in illegal activities using organizational resources
- Access, store, or transmit illegal content
- Harass, threaten, or discriminate against others
- Violate privacy rights or confidentiality obligations
- Compromise security controls or attempt unauthorized access
- Introduce malware, viruses, or other malicious code
- Engage in activities that could harm the organization's reputation

---

## 4. Access and Authentication

### 4.1 User Accounts and Credentials

**Requirements:**
- Use only assigned accounts; no account sharing
- Protect passwords and authentication credentials
- Do not disclose passwords to anyone, including IT staff
- Use strong, unique passwords for each system
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) where available
- Report lost, stolen, or compromised credentials immediately

**Prohibited:**
- Sharing accounts or credentials with others
- Storing passwords in plain text or insecure locations
- Using weak or easily guessable passwords
- Attempting to access others' accounts
- Circumventing authentication mechanisms

### 4.2 Access Authorization

**Requirements:**
- Access only systems and data necessary for job duties
- Request formal authorization for additional access
- Respect access controls and permissions
- Log off when leaving workstation unattended
- Report inappropriate access granted or observed

**Prohibited:**
- Accessing systems or data without authorization
- Attempting to elevate privileges without approval
- Using others' credentials to gain access
- Bypassing or circumventing access controls
- Maintaining access no longer needed for current role

---

## 5. Data Handling and Protection

### 5.1 Data Classification and Handling

**By Classification Level:**

**Restricted Data:**
- Access on strict need-to-know basis only
- Encryption required for storage and transmission
- No personal device storage without approval
- Special handling procedures must be followed

**Confidential Data:**
- Access based on business need
- Encryption recommended for transmission
- Protect from unauthorized disclosure
- No public sharing or posting

**Internal Data:**
- For organizational use only
- Do not share externally without approval
- Protect from public disclosure

**Public Data:**
- May be freely shared
- Maintain accuracy and quality

### 5.2 Data Protection Requirements

**All Users Must:**
- Classify data appropriately
- Handle data according to classification level
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Minimize data retention (delete when no longer needed)
- Report data breaches or suspected compromises immediately
- Follow data protection regulations (GDPR, etc.)

**Prohibited:**
- Storing confidential data on personal devices without encryption
- Sending confidential data via unencrypted email
- Posting confidential or internal data publicly (social media, forums)
- Excessive data collection or retention without justification
- Unauthorized data transfers or exports

---

## 6. Communication and Collaboration

### 6.1 Email Use

**Acceptable:**
- Business communications
- Professional correspondence
- Reasonable personal use (brief, infrequent)
- Authorized external communications

**Prohibited:**
- Chain letters, spam, or mass unsolicited emails
- Offensive, harassing, or discriminatory content
- Phishing or social engineering attempts
- Impersonating others or using fake identities
- Forwarding confidential information to unauthorized recipients

**Requirements:**
- Use professional tone and language
- Include appropriate disclaimer (if required)
- Verify recipients before sending confidential information
- Be cautious of phishing attempts
- Report suspicious emails to IT/security team

### 6.2 Instant Messaging and Chat

**Acceptable:**
- Work-related discussions
- Quick questions and coordination
- Team collaboration

**Requirements:**
- Use approved platforms only (Slack, Microsoft Teams, etc.)
- No confidential data in unsecured chat
- Professional communication standards apply

**Prohibited:**
- Sharing confidential data via insecure chat platforms
- Using personal messaging apps for work without approval
- Offensive or inappropriate communications

### 6.3 Video Conferencing

**Requirements:**
- Use approved platforms (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.)
- Secure meetings with passwords for sensitive topics
- Be aware of environment (background, privacy)
- Mute when not speaking in large meetings
- Record only with consent (where required by law)

**Prohibited:**
- Unauthorized recording of confidential discussions
- Sharing meeting links publicly for private meetings
- Disruptive or unprofessional behavior

---

## 7. Internet and Web Use

### 7.1 Internet Browsing

**Acceptable:**
- Work-related research and learning
- Business development and networking
- Reasonable personal use during breaks
- Professional development and education

**Prohibited:**
- Accessing illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content
- Streaming video/audio excessively (impacts bandwidth)
- Online gambling or day trading (on work time)
- Downloading copyrighted material illegally
- Visiting known malicious websites
- Excessive personal use affecting productivity

**Requirements:**
- Use judgment in internet usage
- Avoid high-bandwidth activities during work hours
- Be aware of security risks (phishing links, malware)
- Use organizational VPN for remote access

### 7.2 Social Media

**Personal Use:**
- Permitted during breaks or non-work hours
- Do not represent yourself as speaking for the organization
- Do not post confidential organizational information
- Maintain professional image (you may be associated with organization)
- Follow social media policy (if separate policy exists)

**Work Use:**
- Must be authorized for official social media management
- Follow organizational branding and messaging guidelines
- Coordinate with communications/marketing team

**Prohibited:**
- Posting confidential, proprietary, or sensitive information
- Disparaging the organization, customers, or colleagues
- Harassing or discriminatory posts
- Sharing information that could damage organization's reputation

---

## 8. Software and Application Use

### 8.1 Software Installation

**Requirements:**
- Use only approved and licensed software
- Obtain IT approval before installing new software
- Verify software source (official websites, app stores)
- Keep software updated with latest security patches
- Use organizational software licenses, not personal

**Prohibited:**
- Installing unauthorized or unlicensed software
- Using pirated or cracked software
- Installing software from untrusted sources
- Bypassing software licensing restrictions
- Using personal software licenses for business purposes

### 8.2 Open Source Software

**Acceptable:**
- Using approved open source components (Composer, NPM dependencies)
- Contributing to approved open source projects
- Following GPL v3 license requirements for this project

**Requirements:**
- Verify license compatibility (GPL v3 for this project)
- Review security and quality of open source components
- Document open source component usage
- Comply with open source license terms

**Prohibited:**
- Using open source with incompatible licenses
- Introducing vulnerable or unmaintained dependencies
- Violating open source license terms

---

## 9. Device Usage

### 9.1 Organizational Devices

**Acceptable:**
- Business use (primary purpose)
- Reasonable personal use (email, browsing during breaks)
- Remote work and travel

**Requirements:**
- Full disk encryption enabled
- Security software installed and updated
- Screen lock configured (5 minutes idle)
- Strong password/PIN protection
- Physical security (don't leave unattended)
- Report loss or theft immediately

**Prohibited:**
- Sharing devices with family or friends
- Disabling security software or controls
- Jailbreaking or rooting devices
- Removing organizational management software
- Excessive personal use affecting work

### 9.2 Personal Devices (BYOD)

**If Permitted:**
- Must meet minimum security standards
- Organizational data must be segregated or containerized
- Subject to remote wipe for organizational data
- Personal data not accessible by organization
- Consent to limited monitoring for security (organizational data only)

**Requirements:**
- Device encryption enabled
- Strong password/PIN
- Security software (antivirus, etc.)
- Latest OS updates installed
- Screen lock configured

**Prohibited:**
- Storing organizational data without encryption
- Mixing organizational and personal data insecurely
- Refusing reasonable security requirements

### 9.3 Removable Media (USB Drives, External Drives)

**Requirements:**
- Use only approved removable media
- Encryption required for confidential data
- Malware scan before use
- Secure disposal when no longer needed

**Prohibited:**
- Using unknown or found USB drives
- Storing confidential data on unencrypted removable media
- Excessive use (prefer cloud storage)
- Leaving removable media unattended

---

## 10. Network Use

### 10.1 Network Access

**Requirements:**
- Connect only authorized devices to organizational networks
- Use VPN for remote access to internal systems
- Secure Wi-Fi connections (WPA2/WPA3, strong password)
- Avoid public Wi-Fi without VPN for sensitive work

**Prohibited:**
- Unauthorized wireless access points or routers
- Network sniffing or packet capture without authorization
- Circumventing network security controls
- Bandwidth abuse (large downloads, streaming during work)
- Connecting insecure or compromised devices

### 10.2 Remote Access

**Requirements:**
- Use only approved remote access methods (VPN, SSH, etc.)
- Multi-factor authentication required
- Connect from secure networks only
- Disconnect when not in use
- Report suspicious remote access activity

**Prohibited:**
- Sharing remote access credentials
- Allowing others to use your remote access
- Remote access from public or insecure computers
- Circumventing remote access security controls

---

## 11. Physical Security

### 11.1 Workspace Security

**Requirements:**
- Lock screen when leaving workstation unattended
- Clear desk policy: secure confidential documents when not in use
- Visitor awareness: be mindful of visitors viewing screens
- Physical security: lock devices when not in use
- Proper disposal: shred confidential documents

**Prohibited:**
- Leaving confidential documents on desk overnight
- Allowing unauthorized individuals to use your workstation
- Sharing office access codes or keys
- Tailgating or allowing others to tailgate into secure areas

### 11.2 Equipment Protection

**Requirements:**
- Protect devices from theft (cable locks in public spaces)
- Store devices securely when not in use
- Report lost or stolen equipment immediately
- Maintain equipment properly (no food/drink near devices)

---

## 12. Incident Reporting

### 12.1 Reporting Obligation

**Must Report:**
- Security incidents or breaches
- Lost or stolen devices or credentials
- Suspected malware or virus infections
- Phishing attempts or social engineering
- Suspicious activity or unauthorized access attempts
- Policy violations observed
- Vulnerabilities discovered

**How to Report:**
- **Email:** security@nvdigitalsolutions.com
- **Timeframe:** Immediately for critical incidents, within 24 hours for others
- **Information:** What happened, when, impact, evidence

**No Penalty for Good Faith Reporting:** Organization encourages reporting without fear of punishment for accidental mistakes.

---

## 13. Monitoring and Privacy

### 13.1 Monitoring

**Organization May Monitor:**
- Network traffic and system logs
- Email and communication systems (work-related)
- Device usage and application usage
- Access logs and authentication attempts
- File storage and data transfers
- Security events and incidents

**Purpose:**
- Ensure compliance with policies
- Detect security threats and incidents
- Investigate policy violations
- Optimize system performance
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations

**Notice:**
Users should have no expectation of privacy when using organizational systems and resources. However, monitoring is conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

### 13.2 Privacy

**Personal Privacy:**
- Personal use of organizational systems may be monitored
- Personal data stored on organizational systems is not private
- Personal communications using organizational systems may be reviewed
- Minimize personal use and personal data storage on work systems

**Organizational Respect for Privacy:**
- Monitoring focused on business and security needs
- Personal data accessed only when necessary
- Monitoring conducted in accordance with applicable laws
- Employee privacy respected to extent possible under law

---

## 14. Compliance and Enforcement

### 14.1 Acknowledgment

All users must:
- Read and understand this Acceptable Use Policy
- Acknowledge acceptance (signature or electronic acknowledgment)
- Ask questions if any part is unclear
- Comply with this policy at all times

### 14.2 Violations

**Consequences of Policy Violations:**
- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory retraining
- Suspension of access privileges
- Disciplinary action (up to and including termination)
- Legal action (for serious violations involving illegal activity)

**Severity-Based Responses:**
- **Minor Violations:** Warning, retraining
- **Moderate Violations:** Written warning, access restrictions, retraining
- **Serious Violations:** Suspension, termination consideration
- **Critical Violations:** Immediate termination, legal action

Disciplinary actions detailed in [Disciplinary Process](./Disciplinary-Process.md).

### 14.3 Exceptions

**Requesting Exceptions:**
- Submit exception request to CISO
- Provide business justification
- Describe compensating controls (if any)
- Obtain written approval before proceeding

**Exception Criteria:**
- Legitimate business need
- Risk assessed and accepted
- Compensating controls in place (if possible)
- Time-limited (not permanent exceptions)

---

## 15. Personal Use

### 15.1 Reasonable Personal Use

**Permitted:**
- Brief, occasional personal email or browsing
- Personal calls or messages during breaks
- Accessing personal accounts (bank, email) during lunch/breaks
- Limited personal tasks that don't interfere with work

**Guidelines:**
- Minimal and does not impact productivity
- During breaks or non-work time
- No illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content
- No excessive bandwidth usage
- No storage of large personal files
- No confidential organizational data involved

**Prohibited:**
- Excessive personal use during work hours
- Personal use that interferes with job duties
- Personal business or side job using organizational resources
- Anything that would be prohibited for business use

**All Users:**

- Handle data appropriately
- Report security incidents and policy violations

**Managers/Supervisors:**

**IT and Security Teams:**
