This Privacy Policy explains how NV Digital Solutions ("NV Digital", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you:

  • visit our websites, including nvdigitalsolutions.com (the "Site");
  • purchase paid NV oOS products through our checkout service ("Products");
  • activate or use a license key (license verification calls); or
  • contact us for support, refunds, or sales.

This policy does not govern personal data processed on websites operated by our customers. NV oOS is self-hosted software: it runs on the customer's own server, and the data it handles (chat transcripts, site content, API keys, assistant configurations) is controlled by the customer, not by us. See "What We Do Not Collect" below.

1. Who We Are

NV Digital Solutions is a United States–based software company that develops and sells the NV Digital Open Operator System (NV oOS), a WordPress plugin for AI assistant orchestration.

Privacy contact: privacy@nvdigitalsolutions.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Information You Provide at Checkout

When you purchase a Product, our payment processor (Stripe) collects and processes your payment details. Through the checkout flow we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • the site URL you license the Product to;
  • your order history and license key;
  • any messages you include with the order.

We never receive, store, or transmit your full card number. Card data is collected inside Stripe's own secure payment elements and is subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

2.2. License Verification Data

When you activate a license key and during periodic checks, the Product contacts our license server (nvdigitalsolutions.com/api/licenses). We receive:

  • the license key;
  • the licensed site URL;
  • the WordPress version and PHP version of the environment;
  • the activation status and timestamp.

This data is used solely to validate entitlements, enforce the one-site license rule, and troubleshoot activation issues.

2.3. Support and Refund Correspondence

When you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your messages, and any attachments or order references you include.

2.4. Website Visit Data

When you browse the Site, our hosting infrastructure and analytics tools may collect standard technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. We use this data in aggregate to understand Site usage and improve our content.

2.5. Cookies

The Site uses a minimal set of cookies for essential functionality (for example, session and security cookies) and, where enabled, privacy-respecting analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. What We Do Not Collect

Because NV oOS is self-hosted, the following stay on the customer's server and are not transmitted to NV Digital:

  • Chat transcripts and AI conversations — stored locally (browser storage) and/or on the customer's own database.
  • Site content — posts, pages, products, media, and other WordPress data.
  • API keys for AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, etc.) — these are configured and stored on the customer's server.
  • Assistant configurations, system prompts, and tool settings.
  • User lists and other personal data the customer processes on their site.

When a customer chooses to send content to a third-party AI provider (for example, a chat message sent to an OpenAI model), that transmission is directly between the customer and the provider and is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy. The customer is responsible for configuring these integrations in compliance with applicable law — see our Terms of Service Section 8.

4. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • process orders and issue license keys (contract performance);
  • verify and enforce license entitlements (legitimate interest);
  • provide support, process refunds, and respond to inquiries;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized license use;
  • maintain the security of our Site and infrastructure;
  • meet legal obligations (tax, accounting, and record-keeping);
  • improve our products and Site based on aggregate usage data.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

  • Website logs: retained for up to 90 days.

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with data-transfer requirements, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses and processors' certifications (for example, Stripe's participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), as applicable.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Correct inaccurate personal information;

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@nvdigitalsolutions.com. We will verify your identity before fulfilling requests and respond within the timeframes required by law. You may also designate an authorized agent.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (orders, licenses), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security, support), and compliance with legal obligations. You have the right to:

  • access, rectify, or erase your personal data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8.3. All Users

Email privacy@nvdigitalsolutions.com to exercise your rights. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.
