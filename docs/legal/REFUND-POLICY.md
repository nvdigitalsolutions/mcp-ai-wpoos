# Refund Policy — 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of paid NV oOS products — including
the **NV oOS Complete** bundle and any other paid add-ons, bundles, or
license keys sold through our checkout service ("Products"). It forms part of
our [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md).

---

## 1. The Guarantee: Full Refund Within 30 Days, No Questions Asked

Because our Products are still under active development and testing, we want
every customer to feel completely safe buying them. **Every purchase is
covered by a 30-day, no-questions-asked, money-back guarantee.**

If you are unhappy with your purchase for **any reason** — it does not work
in your environment, it does not meet your expectations, or you simply
changed your mind — you are entitled to a **full refund of the price paid**,
provided you request it within **30 days of the date of purchase**.

- **No reason required.** You do not need to explain, justify, or debug
  anything.
- **No restocking or cancellation fees.** The full amount you paid is
  refunded.
- **Each purchase has its own 30-day window.** The window runs from the
  purchase date shown on your Stripe receipt.

## 2. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to **[support@nvdigitalsolutions.com](mailto:support@nvdigitalsolutions.com)**
with:

1. **Proof of purchase** — any one of the following is sufficient:
   - your Stripe receipt number or a copy of the receipt email,
   - the email address you used at checkout,
   - your license key (shown after checkout and stored on your site).
2. (Optional) The word "Refund" in the subject line.

We confirm receipt and process your request without asking questions.

## 3. Processing

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- Please allow **5–10 business days** for the refund to appear on your
  statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.
- If the original payment method is no longer available (expired or closed
  card), we will contact you to arrange an alternative.

## 4. What Happens After a Refund

- The license key for that purchase is **revoked**.
- You must **deactivate and delete** the Product from your site(s).
- Continued use of a refunded license is a breach of our Terms of Service and
  may result in suspension of access to future purchases.

## 5. What the Guarantee Does Not Cover

The guarantee covers the price of the Product itself. It does **not** cover:

- **Third-party charges** — fees from AI providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini,
  etc.), hosting costs, bandwidth, or any other charges billed to you by
  third parties in connection with using the Product.
- **Purchases made elsewhere** — the free base plugin distributed via
  WordPress.org and GitHub involves no payment and has nothing to refund.
  Payments made through unauthorised resellers must be refunded by that
  reseller.
- **Renewals or subscriptions** — the Products are currently sold as
  one-time purchases. If subscriptions are introduced, the same 30-day
  guarantee will apply to the most recent renewal payment.

## 6. Fair Use and Fraud Prevention

Our no-questions-asked guarantee is designed to protect genuine customers,
including first-time buyers exploring a product that is still in testing. To
prevent abuse of that goodwill:

- We may ask you to verify your identity or purchase details (e.g. your
  receipt) before processing a refund — this is only to confirm the request
  comes from the actual buyer.
- We reserve the right to decline refunds that we reasonably determine to be
  fraudulent or abusive (for example, repeated purchase-and-refund cycling,
  refund requests for purchases we can verify were not made with us, or
  requests combined with a chargeback that has already recovered the funds).

This section will never be used to deny an ordinary first-time refund
requested within the 30-day window.

## 7. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

We would much rather refund you directly than go through a dispute. If you
are entitled to a refund, please contact us first — you will get your money
back faster.

If you initiate a chargeback or dispute with your bank or card issuer:

- We will provide the payment processor with evidence of your purchase,
  including your acceptance of our Terms of Service at checkout.
- The license key for the disputed purchase is revoked while the dispute is
  open, and permanently if the dispute is resolved in our favour.
- If a refund was already issued and the dispute later recovers the funds
  again, we will ask you to return the duplicate amount.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version in force at
the time of your purchase applies to that purchase.
