# Acceptable Use Policy — NV oOS Products

**Effective date:** September 10, 2026
**Last updated:** September 10, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to your use of paid NV oOS
products, including the **NV oOS Complete** bundle and any other paid
add-ons, bundles, or license keys sold through our checkout service (the
"Products"). It forms part of our [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md).
Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

Because the Products are AI orchestration software, this AUP includes
AI-specific rules in addition to general rules of lawful use. You are
responsible for ensuring that your users, employees, and anyone you grant
access to the Products also comply with this AUP.

---

## 1. Permitted Use

You may use the Products to automate and enhance legitimate WordPress
operations on sites you own or are authorized to administer, including
content management, e-commerce, communications, and integrations with
third-party services you are authorized to use.

## 2. Prohibited Use

You may not use, or permit others to use, the Products to:

### 2.1. Violate Law or Third-Party Rights

- Engage in any illegal, fraudulent, or deceptive activity.
- Infringe copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, or other intellectual
  property rights — including inputting third-party copyrighted material
  into AI models without authorization, or publishing AI-generated content
  that infringes third-party rights.
- Violate privacy or data-protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, etc.),
  including processing personal data without a lawful basis or failing to
  honor data-subject rights.

### 2.2. Cause Harm

- Generate, distribute, or publish content that is defamatory, harassing,
  threatening, hateful, or discriminatory.
- Create or distribute sexual or exploitative content involving minors.
- Generate or distribute content that promotes violence, terrorism, or
  self-harm.
- Deploy the Products in ways that could cause physical harm, including in
  safety-critical or life-support systems, medical devices, or critical
  infrastructure.

### 2.3. Make High-Risk Decisions Without Human Review

- Rely on AI outputs as the sole basis for decisions with legal, financial,
  medical, tax, employment, or other significant consequences without
  qualified human review. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased,
  or fabricated, and are **not** professional advice.
- Represent AI-generated content to third parties as human-authored in
  contexts where disclosure is required by law (including FTC rules on AI
  disclosure and consumer deception).

### 2.4. Abuse Third-Party Services

- Violate the terms of service, usage policies, or rate limits of third-party
  AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, etc.) or other connected services.
- Evade or bypass content-safety measures, rate limits, quotas, or
  authentication controls of third-party providers.
- Trigger paid operations on third-party platforms (media generation,
  messaging, e-commerce actions) without authorization from the account
  holder responsible for those charges.

### 2.5. Compromise Security or Integrity

- Use the Products to attack, probe, or compromise any system, network, or
  account you are not authorized to access.
- Distribute malware, exploits, phishing content, or fraudulent
  communications.
- Attempt to reverse-engineer, circumvent, or disable license verification,
  security controls, or usage limits of the Products.
- Create bulk or unsolicited messaging campaigns (spam) in violation of
  anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, etc.) or the recipients' consent.

### 2.6. Resell or Misuse Licenses

- Resell, redistribute, sublicense, or share license keys beyond the scope of
  the license grant in the Terms of Service.
- Use automated or fraudulent means to obtain licenses, refunds, or support.

## 3. AI Outputs: Your Responsibility

- **You are responsible for all content** generated, transmitted, or
  published through the Products, including by AI assistants or connected
  agents, regardless of whether a human reviewed it.
- **Verify outputs before use.** AI outputs are not guaranteed to be
  accurate, complete, current, or free of third-party rights. Review
  AI-generated content for factual accuracy, originality, and compliance
  with this AUP before publishing or acting on it.
- **Disclose AI use where required.** Where applicable law requires
  disclosure that content is AI-generated (for example, under FTC rules),
  you are responsible for making that disclosure.

## 4. Monitoring and Enforcement

We may review reported violations or, where we have a reasonable basis to
believe a violation has occurred, investigate use of the Products. If we
determine that you have violated this AUP, we may, at our discretion and
without prior notice: suspend or revoke your license, terminate access to
downloads and updates, and/or decline future sales — consistent with the
Termination and Suspension section of the Terms of Service.

We do not monitor customer sites or chat traffic as a matter of course, and
nothing in this AUP obligates us to do so.

To report a suspected violation of this AUP, email
[abuse@nvdigitalsolutions.com](mailto:abuse@nvdigitalsolutions.com). We
review all reports in good faith and may request additional information.
