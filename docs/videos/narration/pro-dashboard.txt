Welcome to NV oOS Pro. In this video, we will explore the Pro dashboard and its analytics capabilities.

The Pro dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of your AI operations in one centralized view.

At the top, you can see key metrics including total chat sessions, token usage, and active assistants.

Scroll down to view the token usage chart. This tracks your AI consumption over time across all providers.

Switch to the Analytics tab to see more detailed breakdowns by assistant, model, and time period.

The Usage tab shows which assistants are most active and which tools are being called most frequently.

You can filter the data by date range to analyze specific time periods or identify usage trends.

Return to the main dashboard view. All your critical AI metrics are available at a glance.

That's it. The Pro dashboard gives you full visibility into how your AI assistants are being used.
