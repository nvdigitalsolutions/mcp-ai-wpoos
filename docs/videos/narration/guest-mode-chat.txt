Welcome to NV oOS. In this video, we will demonstrate guest mode, which lets anonymous visitors chat with your AI assistant.

We are opening the chat page in a fresh browser, just like a first-time visitor to your website.

Notice that no login is required. The chat interface loads immediately and is ready for messages.

Behind the scenes, a temporary guest token is generated automatically. This allows the system to maintain a conversation session.

Let's send a message as an anonymous guest. Type your question and click send.

The AI responds just like it would for a logged-in user. Guests get the same quality experience.

Let's ask a follow-up question to confirm the AI remembers our conversation.

The assistant references our earlier question, demonstrating that guest sessions maintain context.

Now let's reload the page. Watch what happens to our conversation.

The chat history is restored from local browser storage. The guest experience is persistent within the browser session.

That's it. Guest mode lets you offer AI assistance to every visitor without requiring registration.
